What is Health tourism?

Health tourism, in other words, medical tourism has been formed by people traveling to a different country for various medical treatments. It is mainly derived from the travel of underdeveloped countries with poor health systems to more developed countries to receive better quality medical treatment. The most important feature of this system is that patients choose to travel to countries that specialize in the type of treatment they need. The most attractive aspect of this system is that patients from countries that do not have a universal health system usually save thousands of dollars with the treatments they receive from countries that have this advanced health system. Especially in the post-COVID-19 period, this tourism has gradually increased, and it is expected that the demand will increase gradually in the coming years.

What’s the difference between health tourism and travelling for treatment?

The primary feature of medical tourism is to travel to another country for cheaper and more accessible treatment. The majority of travelers are looking for the most advanced technology, better quality service and want to reach health care faster. However, this may not be in the same lane as the trips made for treatment. 'Travelling for treatment' is being differentiated from medical tourism by the fact that they are more likely to be referred by doctors in their home country. At this point, the goal rather than tourism is to get an essential treatment.

1. Cardiology

The World Health Organization claims that cardiovascular diseases are the leading cause of death in Europe and explains that the cause of death of more than 16 million people is caused by heart disease. Medical tourists make various trips primarily for coronary artery bypass grafting, coronary angioplasty and implantation of pacemakers and implantable cardioverter defibrillators, which are types of cardiac surgery. Some of important medical travel destinations, including Mexico, Turkey, India, Malaysia, Taiwan, Singapore, Thailand and South Korea, are paying up to 80% less than in the UK and the US, increasing interest.

2. Cancer treatment

For types of cancer, known as one of the most important fatal diseases, time is very important during the treatment phase. For this reason, many patients prefer to travel abroad for treatment in order not to be placed on the waiting list in their home country. Another factor is that it has a high-cost treatment method. Some patients travel to other parts of the world to receive oncology treatment, including radiotherapy, chemotherapy, and cyberknife therapy. Some of the most important and successful cancer treatment centers in the world are located in Germany, Turkey, and South Korea. And it makes these locations a focal point.

3. Breast Augmentation

Cosmetic and plastic surgery is one of the most popular and in-demand fields today. One of the most intensive cosmetic procedures, especially when traveling abroad, is breast augmentation. The purpose of breast augmentation is to increase the fullness and shape of the breasts, improve body proportions, and increase self-confidence. This can be achieved by placing implants in the breasts, as well as using fat transfer. Among the best countries for breast augmentation are South Korea, Brazil, and Thailand, where visitors can save a large payment.

4. IVF

IVF is a procedure in which a woman's eggs are surgically removed and fertilized in a laboratory using sperm before being placed in the woman's uterus. It is known to be a very expensive treatment. This increases the need for couples to travel to different countries for this treatment. Along with IVF, other popular treatments and tests include egg freezing, donor egg IVF and embryo donation, fertility assessment, semen analysis, and preimplantation genetic diagnosis. Germany, Poland, Spain, and India are among the countries offering private fertility treatment at a fraction of the cost of the UK and US.

5. Hair transplant Surgery

Hair restoration surgery is known as an increasingly demanded and developing field. Today, hair transplantation aims to permanently restore the hair of people with partial baldness by transplanting new follicles into balding or thinning areas. Experts consider a transplant successful when 85 to 95 percent of the transplanted follicles grow new hair. It is a recommended method for anyone who wants a natural solution. In this field, Turkey stands out for having the most skilled and experienced specialists in the world. 

6. Dentistry

Dental treatments and oral surgery are not affordable in most countries around the world, partly because they require high-quality labor.  These reasons encourage people to travel to find high-quality dental care or treatment. Procedures that claim to provide the most natural treatment for tooth loss or toothache caused by tooth damage, injury, or tooth decay have become increasingly common. Common dental procedures for which people travel abroad include veneers, dental implants, crowns, teeth whitening, and braces. Hungary and Mexico are among the countries offering high-quality dental treatments and care at the lowest costs.

7. Liposuction

Liposuction is an aesthetic procedure that is widely applied worldwide against the problem of regional lubrication. In people who have regional fat problems despite diet and exercise, the liposuction method can help to get rid of regional fat loss. It plays an important role in shaping the body by removing stubborn fat deposits in various areas, especially in the abdomen, hips, thighs, calves, arms, hips, back or face. Some types of this treatment require the use of ultrasound and laser. Liposuction is not recommended for obese people, as it is not considered a weight loss treatment and only a certain amount of fat can be removed. The most important result is observed in the change and recovery of body shape. Brazil, South Korea, and Thailand offer this treatment to medical tourists at low prices compared to other major countries.

9. Scans, Tests, Screenings and Second Opinions

Today, techniques for early diagnosis of a large number of diseases have led to rapid treatment and improved survival rates. As for preventive medicine, many hospitals abroad have begun to offer comprehensive physical examinations at discounted prices. Several people travel further afield to access the most technologically advanced equipment and pay less for tests and scans including blood tests, ultrasounds, cancer screening, mammography, endoscopy, gastroscopy, colonoscopy, ulcer evaluation, ECGs, and diagnostic x-rays prefer to do so. Chief among these are CT, MRI, and PET scans. For example, many hospitals in Thailand, Malaysia, and Mexico offer diagnostic and screening packages for foreign patients.

10. Weight-Loss Surgery

Obesity surgery procedures include gastric bypass, sleeve gastrectomy, gastric banding, and duodenal switch. These operations have proven results in the treatment of class III obesity. They also help normalize your metabolism, including blood sugar, blood pressure, and cholesterol. Surgeries in this area are recommended only when patients try non-surgical weight loss methods, such as diet and exercise, which they apply properly, and do not succeed. Its priority audience is patients with serious obesity problems. However, at the end of the surgery, there is also a certain diet and exercise requirement. Some of the countries that specialize in weight loss surgery are Malaysia, Brazil, Costa Rica, Thailand, and India.
