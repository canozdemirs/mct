Reasons for Hair Loss

There are many causes of hair loss. It's critical to understand the potential causes of your hair loss and the reason(s) behind it. Male pattern baldness is the most frequent cause of hair loss in males, accounting for the vast majority of cases. It's crucial to see a doctor find out the precise cause of your hair loss. If you're not getting enough of certain essential vitamins and minerals, you may be at risk of hair loss. You can talk to a doctor before taking supplements by learning from which foods you can get these important nutrients. When it comes to vitamins, more is not always better, and sometimes too much can be harmful. Biotin The cells in your body require vitamin B7 biotin. Low levels might result in brittle nails, skin rashes, and hair loss. Your level may be low if:
  • pregnant or breastfeeding
  • taking certain antibiotics or epilepsy medications
Iron Red blood cells need iron to carry oxygen. Low levels can cause iron-deficiency anaemia. Fatigue, a pale complexion, and hair loss are symptoms. If any of the following apply to you:
  • If you experience severe menstruation as a woman
  • If you have a chronic illness
  • If you are vegetarian or vegan
Zinc Zinc can play an important role in making protein in your hair and other cells. Your body can't make it, so you have to get it from food or supplements. Hair loss, slow wound healing, and impaired taste or smell are all signs of low zinc levels. You might have low zinc if:
  • pregnant or breastfeeding
  • If you have bowel disease or severe diarrhoea
  • you have kidney diseases
Genetic factors Hair loss affects men and women differently depending on the cause. Male-type hormone-sensitive hair loss, which affects over 50% of the population, is the most common kind of hair loss found in males. Chronic hair loss is of this kind. When the underlying skin condition is treated, these spills may be cleaned up. Stress and depression Another reason for hair loss, along with any illness, is excessive stress. Care After the Operation

A surgical technique, hair transplantation might include a post-operative care component that is just as crucial as the surgical operation itself. If you are receiving your transplant overseas, it is crucial to confirm that your facility offers post-operative care. The first thing you should avoid doing after having hair transplantation is washing your hair. Wash your hair no later than three days after planting. After 3 days of hair transplantation, you should wash your hair at least 2 times a day for 2 weeks. In this way, you will relax your hair after transplantation. You should avoid regular massages in the hair transplant area. Because the hair follicles may not be completely settled. The massage you will do can cause these roots to fall out. When using care products after hair transplantation, be careful to use the products recommended by your doctor. Especially the nape area should be washed with a shampoo recommended by the specialist. Normal movements should be avoided during washing. After hair transplantation, definitely stay away from hair styling products such as a gel.
