What tests are typically included in a standard medical check-up in Turkey?

A standard medical check-up in Turkey includes a comprehensive set of tests designed to evaluate your overall health and detect any early signs of illness. Clinics structure these check-ups to cover all major body systems, making them suitable for people of all ages. Typical tests include:
  • Complete blood count (CBC)
  • Blood sugar and HbA1c for diabetes screening
  • Liver and kidney function tests
  • Lipid profile (cholesterol and triglycerides)
  • Thyroid function tests (TSH, T3, T4)
  • Vitamin and mineral levels (such as Vitamin D, B12, calcium)
  • Urinalysis for kidney and urinary tract health
  • Electrocardiogram (ECG) for heart rhythm
  • Blood pressure and pulse monitoring
Depending on age and gender, additional tests may include:
  • PSA test for men (prostate screening)
  • Pap smear or mammogram for women
  • Abdominal or thyroid ultrasound
  • Stool test for hidden blood
These tests offer a clear snapshot of your internal health and are usually done on the same day. Turkish clinics ensure that check-ups are detailed but efficient, providing you with real value and peace of mind.  

How long does a full medical check-up take?

A full medical check-up in Turkey typically takes between 3 to 6 hours, depending on how many tests are included in your chosen package. Most clinics complete all required tests within one working day, and you often receive same-day or next-day results. The process is smooth and well-organized. Here's a general timeline:
  • Morning blood and urine samples: Collected after fasting
  • Physical exam and vital signs: Checked by a general physician
  • Imaging tests: Such as ultrasound, ECG, or X-ray done afterward
  • Specialist consultations: Added if needed based on test results
  • Result review and health report: Provided same day or the following day
Because many clinics are experienced in treating international patients, the system is designed to be fast and convenient. You won’t need to wait in long lines or go to multiple centers. If your package includes extra screenings like endoscopy, stress testing, or detailed organ scans, the check-up may take a bit longer or be split over two days. Are the test results reviewed by specialists?

Yes, in Turkey, all test results from your medical check-up are reviewed by qualified medical specialists to ensure accuracy and provide proper interpretation. This is a key reason why Turkish check-ups are considered reliable and comprehensive. After your tests are completed, results are analyzed by:
  • Internal medicine doctors or general physicians for overall evaluation
  • Cardiologists for ECGs or heart imaging
  • Radiologists for interpreting ultrasounds or X-rays
  • Endocrinologists, urologists, or gynecologists if specific concerns arise
This multidisciplinary review guarantees that even small irregularities are noticed and addressed. If any abnormalities are found, you’ll be guided toward further testing or treatment recommendations. Most clinics also schedule a face-to-face or video consultation where a doctor explains your results clearly and answers your questions. You won’t just receive numbers—you’ll get real insight into your current health.

Is imaging like ultrasound or X-rays part of the check-up?

Yes, most full medical check-up packages in Turkey include basic imaging tests, such as ultrasound and X-rays, as part of the standard offering. These tests help detect problems that blood or urine tests might miss. Common imaging procedures include:
  • Abdominal ultrasound to view organs like the liver, kidneys, pancreas, and bladder
  • Thyroid ultrasound to detect nodules or enlargement
  • Chest X-ray to assess lung and heart size
  • Mammography or breast ultrasound (for women over 40)
  • Bone density scan (optional for those at risk of osteoporosis)
These scans are painless, quick, and performed using modern equipment. They’re reviewed by a radiologist, and any concerns are immediately discussed with your attending doctor. Including imaging in your check-up adds depth and accuracy to your health evaluation, making it easier to catch issues early—especially silent conditions like kidney stones, fatty liver, or nodules.

Can I choose specific tests to include in my check-up?

Yes, most clinics in Turkey allow you to customize your check-up package based on your age, gender, medical history, or personal concerns. While standard packages are available, patients can often add or remove specific tests as needed. You may choose to include:
  • Hormone panels if you're experiencing fatigue or weight changes
  • Cancer markers based on family history
  • Vitamin levels (D, B12, iron, etc.)
  • Stress tests or heart imaging if you have cardiovascular risks
  • Gynecological or urological exams depending on gender and age
Many clinics offer tiered packages (basic, advanced, executive) and let you upgrade specific parts. If you're unsure, doctors can help recommend which tests are most relevant during your first consultation. This flexibility ensures your check-up is not just routine but relevant and meaningful for your personal health goals.
