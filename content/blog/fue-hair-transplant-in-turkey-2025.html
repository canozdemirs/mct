What is FUE Hair Transplant in Turkey?

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) is one of the most advanced and widely preferred hair transplant techniques worldwide. Unlike traditional methods, it does not involve incisions or stitches, making it a minimally invasive and highly effective procedure. First introduced in Japan in 1988 and officially recognized in medical literature in 2002, the FUE technique involves extracting hair follicles individually from the donor area and implanting them one by one into the target area. This precise approach ensures a natural-looking result but requires longer sessions compared to other methods. Today, advanced micromotors are used instead of manual punches, making the procedure more efficient and comfortable. FUE has largely replaced the older FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation) method, which involved removing a strip of scalp, leaving a visible scar. With FUE, hair follicles are carefully transplanted, following the natural hairline and growth direction to achieve seamless results. However, for optimal outcomes, the procedure must be performed by skilled professionals; otherwise, unnatural-looking results may occur. This technique is particularly effective for restoring hair in individuals experiencing baldness, as it uses hair from the nape area, which is resistant to shedding. Ensuring careful separation, preservation, and implantation of follicles is crucial for a successful transplant. When done correctly, FUE provides a dense, natural hairline with virtually no scarring, making it the preferred choice for those seeking permanent and natural hair restoration.

FUE Hair Transplant in Turkey Advantages

The FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) method has been the most advanced hair transplant technique since 2004, offering a more comfortable and efficient experience for both patients and specialists. Thanks to its adaptability to technological advancements, FUE continues to improve, making hair transplantation faster and more effective. With these advancements, FUE has become the preferred choice for those seeking a fast, safe, and natural-looking hair restoration solution.

The Reason Why the Hair Follicles to be Transplanted are Taken from the Nape Area

Hair follicles from the nape area are commonly preferred for hair transplantation because they are resistant to testosterone and are less likely to fall out over time. This makes the nape area ideal for harvesting follicles for transplantation. However, if the hair in the nape area is thinning or shedding due to a specific condition, follicles can be sourced from other areas of the body, such as the chest, ensuring a successful transplant.

How Long Does Fue Hair Transplantation Process?

The duration of Fue hair transplantation varies according to the size of the desired area. The amount of hair follicle and hair tissue required for the transplantation area is important for the duration of the procedure. If 4000-4500 hair follicles and hair tissue called grafts are needed to be taken during hair transplantation, this process may take approximately 6-7 hours. Depending on the number of grafts, there may be cases where the hair transplant procedure takes 9 hours. Depending on the situation of the session, short breaks may be given during the procedure. Removal of grafts during the Fue technique can take between 2-4 hours. There is a short break after the graft removal process. The most important factors that determine the duration of the process are as follows:
  • The size of the area to be planted,
  • The number of grafts to be taken according to the size of the area,
  • Experience of the healthcare professional performing the procedure,
  • The number of sessions and session duration preference of the healthcare professional who performed the procedure,
  • Having a structure that can affect the process of the scalp.

Post Fue Hair Transplant Process

After Fue hair transplantation, some changes occur in the transplanted hair. The most important of these changes is the shedding of the transplanted hair in a very short time. Although the loss of newly planted hair scares the person, the hair that has been lost will grow back in a very short time, permanently. After the operation, the person is sent home to rest. The person is requested not to travel by car or use such vehicles for 6-10 hours from the end of the operation. After hair transplantation, care should be taken not to touch the treated area with hands, this area should not come into contact with water. Keeping the head upright is important for the edema to heal in a short time. Medicines and creams given by the doctor should be used.

Washing After Hair Transplant in FUE Hair Transplant in Turkey

After the hair washing time begins, it’s essential to use the shampoo recommended by the doctor.
