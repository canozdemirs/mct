Cuisine

Some of the world's most wonderful food comes from Turkey. Whoever you are and whatever you like, you can always find something to eat in Turkey. So, it is safe to travel Turkey and it is safe to eat in Turkey. You won't be dissatisfied with the extensive menu, which includes mouthwatering kebabs and decadent, rich desserts. Eat a filling breakfast of borek, which is filo pastry stuffed with cheese and herbs, or indulge in some mezes and a refreshing glass of raki, a liqueur with an anise flavor. After sipping Turkish tea to wash everything down, you'll be ready to see everything Turkey has to offer. Additionally, doner kebabs and other street cuisine from the area should definitely be tried while you're there. Of course, a few recipes you should try are:
  • Döner kebab: Slow-cooked beef is prepared on a vertical rotisserie in this national cuisine. It is available in most cafés and restaurants along the Boulevard.
  • Dolma: Rice, ground meat, and spices are wrapped in grape leaves to make this traditional delicacy. Every area provides its own variant!
  • Börek: Böreks are savory pastries stuffed with meat or cheese and fashioned of filo dough. You can eat them with a meal or as a snack.
  • Baklava: The most well-known dessert in Turkey. It is comprised of layers of filo pastry that are filled with syrup and chopped nuts.
  • Manti: A type of Turkish pasta made of folded dough triangles stuffed with minced meat and frequently garnished with minced onions and parsley. It is usually served hot with a variety of toppings, including red pepper powder, dried mint, oregano, melted butter, and warmed olive oil.
