How to convert prepared 3D face images to mask?

After deciding on the new shape of the nose, the pre- and post-acquired 3D drawing device images are printed on the 3D printer. Prepared face molds are removed from the printer with a white powder and laser sintering technique. Masks are put in the final stage of color printing. The face masks that are cut and cleaned before and after are made ready by dipping a special solution with hardening feature.
