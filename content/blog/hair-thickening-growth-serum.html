Hair Growth Cycle

The hair shaft itself is dead; however, the cells that produce it or the hair follicles (hair follicles) are not dead. These cells produce our keratin hair strands in 3 phases:
  1. Active growth phase (anagen phase). This takes 3-5 years and allows the hair to grow up to 15 centimeters per year.
  2. Regression period (catagen phase): It is a process that lasts up to 10 days when hair growth stops.
  3. The resting period (telogen phase) lasts about 100 days. Meanwhile, the hair falls out, the hair-producing follicle rests for 1-3 months, and then hair production begins again.

What Is Male Pattern Hair Loss?

Male pattern hair loss is a disease that is frequently seen in men, but has become more common in women in recent years, in which the hair on the temples and crown is thinned or completely shed. Since this type of shedding is seen more in men, it is known as male pattern hair loss or hereditary hair loss. In men, this type of hair loss is seen in the temple and crown area. In women, thinning is seen in the whole of the hair in the upper part of the scalp. At the edges of these areas where the hair is thinning, there are immature, thin, short hairs that gradually thin towards the tip.
