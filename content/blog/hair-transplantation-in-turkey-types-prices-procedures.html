What is Hair Transplant?

A hair transplant is a medical procedure in which hair follicles are removed from one part of the body, typically the back or sides of the scalp (known as the donor area), and transplanted to an area experiencing hair loss, known as the recipient area. The goal is to restore hair growth in areas affected by baldness or thinning. There are two main types of hair transplant techniques:
  • FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation): In this method, a strip of scalp with healthy hair follicles is removed from the donor area. The strip is then dissected into individual follicular units, which are transplanted into the balding area. The area where the strip is removed is stitched, and it may leave a linear scar.
  • FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction): In this technique, individual hair follicles are extracted directly from the donor area using a micro-punch tool. These follicles are then transplanted to the balding area. FUE typically leaves tiny dot scars that are less noticeable than the scar from FUT.

Hair transplants are most commonly performed to treat male pattern baldness, but they can also be used for eyebrow restoration, repairing hair loss caused by injuries, or helping individuals who have lost hair due to medical conditions or treatments. The success of the procedure depends on several factors, including the surgeon’s expertise, the quality of the donor hair, and the unique characteristics of the patient’s hair.

Types of Hair Transplant in Turkey

Turkey is known as a popular destination for hair transplants due to its advanced techniques and affordable costs. The country offers several types of hair transplant methods, with most clinics providing a range of services depending on the patient’s needs. Here are the main types of hair transplant procedures available in Turkey: FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction)
  • Most Common Method: FUE is the most widely used hair transplant technique in Turkey. In this method, individual hair follicles are extracted from the donor area using a micro-punch tool and transplanted to the recipient area.
  • No Linear Scarring: This method leaves tiny dot scars that are not noticeable after healing, making it a popular choice.
  • Minimal Downtime: Recovery is usually faster compared to FUT, with minimal scarring and less discomfort.
DHI (Direct Hair Implantation)
  • Advanced FUE: DHI is a modified version of the FUE technique. It involves the use of a special pen-like tool (Choi Implanter Pen) that allows the surgeon to extract and implant hair follicles directly without creating recipient sites in advance.
  • High Precision: This method gives the surgeon more control over the depth, angle, and direction of each hair follicle, which can result in more natural-looking hairlines.
  • Slower Process: Since each follicle is placed individually with great precision, the process can be slower and more labor-intensive than standard FUE.
Stem Cell Hair Transplant
  • Innovative Technique: This technique is still in its experimental phase and involves using stem cells from the patient's body to stimulate hair growth in thinning areas.
  • Regenerative Approach: Stem cells are believed to help regenerate and grow hair, offering a potential future alternative to traditional transplants.

Hair Transplant Procedure in Turkey

The hair transplant procedure in Turkey generally follows a structured process, designed to ensure patient comfort and successful outcomes. Here’s a step-by-step overview of what you can expect: Pre-Procedure Consultation
  • The process begins with an initial consultation, either in person or online. The surgeon evaluates your hair loss pattern, overall health, and expectations.
  • The surgeon explains the available hair transplant methods (FUE, DHI, Sapphire FUE, etc.) and recommends the most suitable option based on your needs.
  • You may undergo tests to assess your suitability for the procedure, including blood tests and sometimes imaging.
Preparation
  • Medical History Review: The clinic reviews your medical history to ensure there are no contraindications.
  • Hair and Scalp Examination: The donor and recipient areas are examined to plan the procedure.
  • Pre-Procedure Instructions: You will receive instructions on how to prepare for the surgery, including any medications to avoid and how to manage your hair before the procedure.
Day of the Procedure
  • Arrival and Check-In: On the day of the surgery, you’ll arrive at the clinic and check in. You may meet with the medical team to finalize details.
  • Anesthesia: The procedure is performed under local anesthesia. You’ll be awake but won’t feel pain in the treated areas.
  • Preparation of Donor Area: The donor area (usually the back or sides of the scalp) is trimmed and prepared. For FUE and DHI, individual follicles are extracted. For FUT, a strip of scalp is removed.
Hair Follicle Extraction
  • FUE: Using a micro-punch tool, individual hair follicles are extracted from the donor area.
  • DHI: Hair follicles are extracted similarly but are directly implanted into the recipient area using a Choi Implanter Pen.
Follicle Preparation
  • Dissection (FUT): If you’re undergoing FUT, the strip of scalp is dissected into individual follicular units under a microscope.
  • Storage: Extracted follicles are kept in a special solution to maintain their viability.
Recipient Area Preparation
  • Tiny incisions are made in the recipient area where the hair follicles will be implanted. The pattern and direction of these incisions are designed to match natural hair growth.
Hair Follicle Implantation
  • The prepared follicles are carefully implanted into the recipient sites. In DHI, this is done using the Choi Implanter Pen, which allows for precise placement.
Post-Procedure Care
  • Bandaging: Light bandages may be applied to the donor area to protect it and reduce swelling.
  • Instructions: You’ll receive detailed post-operative care instructions, including how to wash your hair, what medications to take, and how to manage any discomfort.
  • Aftercare Products: Some clinics provide aftercare products like shampoos, sprays, and medications to aid recovery.
Recovery and Follow-Up
  • Immediate Aftercare: You may need to stay in the clinic or hotel for a day or two for monitoring and initial care.
  • Follow-Up Appointments: The clinic typically schedules follow-up appointments to monitor progress, ensure proper healing, and address any issues.
  • Hair Growth Timeline: Hair growth begins within a few months, with full results visible after 9-12 months.
Long-Term Care
  • Maintenance: To maintain results, you may be advised to use certain hair care products or undergo additional treatments if needed.
  • Lifestyle Adjustments: Adopting a healthy lifestyle and avoiding activities that stress the scalp can support long-term success.

How Does a Hair Transplant Look?

A hair transplant, when done correctly, can look very natural, especially with modern techniques like FUE and FUT. Here's a breakdown of how it typically looks at different stages: Immediately After the Procedure:
  • Scabbing and Redness: Right after the transplant, the recipient area may look red and have small scabs around each transplanted follicle. This is because each follicle is placed into a tiny incision in the scalp. The donor area may have some swelling or small dot scars (in the case of FUE) or a linear scar (in the case of FUT).
  • Bandages: Sometimes, a bandage is placed over the donor area to help with healing.
First Few Days to Weeks:
  • Initial Healing: The scabs around the transplanted follicles usually fall off within a week or two. Redness may persist for a few weeks.
  • Hair Shedding: The transplanted hairs often shed within 2-4 weeks. This is normal and part of the process, known as "shock loss," where the transplanted follicles shed the existing hair before new growth begins.
3 to 6 Months After the Procedure:
  • New Hair Growth: Around 3-4 months post-surgery, the transplanted follicles begin to grow new hair. The hair may initially appear thin and fine, but over time it thickens.
  • Filling In: By 6 months, most patients start seeing a noticeable difference in the density and coverage of their hair.
1 Year and Beyond:
  • Final Results: Full results typically appear around 9-12 months after the transplant. At this stage, the hair looks completely natural and blends with the rest of the patient’s hair. The transplanted hair grows and behaves like normal hair, including being able to be cut, styled, and washed.
A well-done transplant will result in hair that looks natural and is undetectable as a surgical procedure. The surgeon’s skill in designing a natural-looking hairline and ensuring the right direction of hair growth is key to achieving a good result.
