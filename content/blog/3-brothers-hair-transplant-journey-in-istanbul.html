How do the differences between FUE and DHI techniques affect our decision?

Choosing between FUE and DHI hair transplant techniques is an important part of the decision-making process. Both methods are effective and widely used, but their differences can directly influence treatment planning, recovery, and final results. FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) is a technique where hair follicles are individually extracted from the donor area and then implanted into the recipient area through small channels. This method allows for wide coverage and is often preferred for patients who need a higher number of grafts. FUE offers flexibility in hairline design and is suitable for different stages of hair loss. DHI (Direct Hair Implantation) follows a slightly different approach. In this technique, extracted grafts are implanted directly using a special pen-like tool. This allows for more controlled placement of each hair follicle, especially in areas where precision is critical, such as the front hairline or areas requiring higher density. The main factor affecting our decision is personalization. FUE may be more suitable for patients looking for broader coverage, while DHI can be ideal for those focusing on detailed hairline work or density improvement. Healing time and post-procedure comfort can also differ slightly depending on the technique used. Another important difference is planning. DHI often requires a more detailed and time-intensive implantation process, which can influence procedure duration. FUE, on the other hand, allows for a more flexible workflow when treating larger areas. At Medical Center Turkey, the choice between FUE and DHI is not based on preference but on medical evaluation. Hair type, donor capacity, hair loss pattern, and patient expectations are all considered before selecting the most suitable technique. Understanding the differences between FUE and DHI helps patients make informed decisions. The right technique is the one that aligns with individual needs, realistic expectations, and long-term hair health.
