How is the dental veneer process done and what are the treatment stages and How Can the Cost Vary?

The dental veneer process is carried out by experienced dentists working in oral and dental health centers or dental hospitals. To ensure the patient feels no discomfort during the procedure, local anesthesia is applied at the beginning of the treatment. After anesthesia, the teeth are carefully reshaped and reduced to the ideal size required for veneer placement, and precise measurements are taken.

To maintain aesthetics and comfort during the preparation phase, temporary teeth are attached immediately after the shaping process. The final tooth color is selected together with the patient to achieve the most natural and harmonious appearance. Within approximately five days, the custom-made veneers are prepared in the laboratory. Once ready, the temporary teeth are removed and replaced with the permanent veneers.

Periodic follow-up appointments are recommended to ensure the veneers fit well and the patient adapts smoothly to the new smile. Since each treatment involves individualized planning, material choices, and professional expertise, the overall treatment cost may vary from patient to patient. Understanding these stages helps individuals better evaluate both the clinical process and the potential price factors involved.

How is the Coating Process Done?

In order to have a dental veneer, the patient must first be examined by a specialist dentist. Then it is decided which type of coating will be made. After the measurements of the teeth are taken, the cleaning of the teeth will begin. While the teeth are being cleaned, molds are placed on the teeth at certain times. The purpose of these molds is to understand whether the tooth has come into the required shape. If the tooth does not fit properly in the mold, the doctor will correct the shape of the tooth again after removing the mold. If the mold is fully seated when this process is repeated; it will be understood that the tooth has been brought into the proper shape. After the veneer is placed on the tooth, it is ensured that the veneer is placed on the tooth by using special adhesives. In this way, the tooth gets rid of caries and regains its new state. If everything is suitable for dental veneer, all of the procedures will be handled in 3 sessions. However, in some cases, if the doctor's request is in this direction and the patient's condition is favorable, it is possible to veneer the tooth in 2 sessions. A better fit of the veneer will be achieved when there is a 2 or 3 day difference between the sessions while the veneer is being made. After the dental veneer is done, the patient should never neglect oral care. As a normal person should brush the teeth at least 2 times a day. In addition, it will be very useful for people who have veneer teeth to use dental floss. The tooth does not require any extra care after the crown is made. It will be enough to do routine care for it like a normal tooth.

  • Tooth veneer cannot be restored. When coatings are damaged, they are generally not repairable.
