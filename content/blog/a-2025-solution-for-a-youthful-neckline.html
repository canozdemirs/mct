What is the recovery time for neck rejuvenation treatments in Turkey?

The recovery time for neck rejuvenation treatments in Turkey varies depending on whether you choose a non-surgical or surgical procedure.

Non-surgical treatments such as Ultherapy, radiofrequency skin tightening, and laser-based rejuvenation require little to no downtime. Most patients experience mild redness or swelling, which typically disappears within a few hours to a couple of days. You can return to your daily routine immediately, and the skin gradually becomes firmer and smoother in the following weeks.

Surgical procedures, such as a neck lift, involve a longer recovery period. The initial healing process usually takes 7–10 days, during which stitches are generally removed. Most patients can resume light activities within 2–4 weeks, while full recovery may take up to 6 weeks. Swelling and tightness slowly diminish during this time.
