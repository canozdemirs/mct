Recovery and Aftercare

Post-Surgery Care: After undergoing a fat transfer procedure, proper post-surgery care is essential to achieve the desired results and ensure a smooth recovery. Patients are typically advised to avoid sitting or lying directly on their buttocks for several weeks. This precaution is vital to protect the newly transferred fat cells and give them the best chance of survival. Specialized cushions or pillows can be used to help distribute weight evenly and avoid pressure on the treated area. Additionally, wearing compression garments is strongly recommended as they help reduce swelling, provide support to the newly contoured areas, and promote faster healing. These garments also contribute to shaping the buttocks and maintaining the overall results of the procedure. Fat Survival: It's important to understand that not all the fat cells transferred during the procedure will survive, as the body naturally reabsorbs some of them over time. To ensure that the final outcome meets expectations, surgeons typically overfill the buttocks during the surgery. This approach accounts for any natural loss of fat, resulting in a beautifully sculpted appearance once healing is complete. The survival of fat cells can vary based on factors such as the patient’s lifestyle, adherence to post-surgery instructions, and individual healing capacity.
