What types of bariatric surgeries do tourists commonly undergo in Turkey?

Turkey is one of the most popular countries in the world for bariatric surgery, and international patients continue to choose it for both its quality and affordability. Tourists often travel to Turkey to achieve healthy, long-term weight loss through various bariatric procedures. So, which types of surgeries are most commonly performed? The leading option is gastric sleeve surgery. This procedure involves removing a large portion of the stomach, leaving behind a smaller, sleeve shaped section. It reduces the amount of food a person can eat and lowers hunger by affecting hormone levels. Gastric sleeve surgery is highly preferred because it is effective, permanent, and usually has a quick recovery period.

Another frequently chosen bariatric procedure is gastric bypass surgery. In this technique, a small stomach pouch is created and directly connected to the small intestine, helping patients lose weight by limiting both food intake and calorie absorption. It is particularly effective for individuals with a high BMI or obesity-related conditions such as type 2 diabetes and high blood pressure.

From a cost perspective, the cost of gastric bypass surgery in Turkey varies depending on the hospital, surgeon’s experience, and the services included in the treatment package. However, Turkey offers this procedure at significantly more affordable prices compared to many other countries, often including hospital stay, anesthesia, pre-operative tests, and post-operative follow-up in all-inclusive packages.

For patients seeking a non-surgical weight loss option, the gastric balloon is a popular alternative. A soft balloon is placed into the stomach through a short endoscopic procedure and inflated to create a feeling of fullness, helping reduce portion sizes. The cost of gastric balloon treatment in Turkey is generally lower than surgical options, making it an accessible and cost-effective solution for patients who do not qualify for surgery.

Both procedures support significant weight loss, improve overall health, and enhance quality of life. With advanced medical techniques, experienced bariatric specialists, and competitive treatment costs, Turkey continues to be a trusted destination for safe, effective, and affordable bariatric treatments.
