What is Orthodontic Treatment?

Braces treatment, also known as orthodontic treatment, involves using a system of brackets and wires to gradually move teeth into their proper positions to improve alignment, bite, and overall dental health. Here’s an in-depth look at braces treatment: Purpose of Braces
  • Correcting Misalignment:
    • Straighten crooked or crowded teeth.
    • Close gaps between teeth.
  • Bite Correction:
    • Address issues such as overbite, underbite, crossbite, and open bite.
  • Improving Oral Health:
    • Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease.
    • Improved bite function can reduce wear on teeth and alleviate jaw pain.

Types of Orthodontic Treatments in Turkey

  • Traditional Braces:
Metal Braces: The most common type, using metal brackets and wires to gradually move teeth into place. Ceramic Braces: Similar to metal braces but use tooth-colored or clear brackets to be less noticeable.
  • Clear Aligners:
Invisalign: A popular choice for adults and teens, using a series of clear, removable aligners to straighten teeth.
  • Lingual Braces:
Behind the Teeth: Braces that are attached to the back of the teeth, making them invisible from the front.
  • Self-Ligating Braces:
Damon Braces: Use a slide mechanism instead of elastic ties, reducing friction and potentially shortening treatment time.
  • Other Specialized Treatments:
Orthognathic Surgery: For severe jaw alignment issues. Palatal Expanders: Used to widen the upper jaw in growing children.

Treatment Process

The treatment process for orthodontic treatment involves several stages, from the initial consultation to post-treatment care. Here’s a detailed breakdown of each step: Initial Consultation
  • Assessment:
    • The orthodontist conducts a thorough examination of your teeth, jaw, and facial structure.
    • This may include taking X-rays, photographs, and dental impressions or digital scans.
  • Discussion:
    • The orthodontist discusses your concerns, goals, and expectations.
    • They explain the potential treatment options, including the types of braces or aligners that might be suitable for you.
  • Treatment Plan:
    • A personalized treatment plan is created based on the assessment and your preferences.
    • The plan outlines the type of braces, estimated duration, cost, and any other relevant details.
Pre-Treatment Preparations
  • Dental Cleaning:
    • A professional dental cleaning may be recommended before starting treatment to ensure good oral health.
  • Cavity and Gum Treatment:
    • Any cavities, gum disease, or other dental issues need to be addressed before placing braces.
Application of Braces
  • Preparation:
    • Teeth are thoroughly cleaned and dried.
    • A special adhesive is applied to the teeth to attach the brackets.
  • Placing Brackets:
    • Brackets are carefully positioned on each tooth and bonded with the adhesive.
  • Attaching Archwires:
    • Once the brackets are in place, the orthodontist threads an archwire through the brackets.
    • Elastic bands or clips are used to secure the wire to the brackets.
Regular Adjustments
  • Follow-Up Visits:
    • Regular appointments are scheduled every 4-6 weeks to monitor progress and make necessary adjustments.
    • During these visits, the orthodontist may tighten the wires, change the elastic bands, or make other adjustments to continue guiding the teeth into their correct positions.
  • Patient Cooperation:
    • It's essential to maintain good oral hygiene by brushing and flossing regularly.
    • Avoid certain foods that can damage the braces, such as sticky or hard items.
Managing Discomfort
  • Initial Discomfort:
    • Some soreness and discomfort are common after the initial placement and adjustments.
    • Over-the-counter pain relievers can help manage any discomfort.
  • Orthodontic Wax:
    • Orthodontic wax can be applied to the brackets and wires to prevent irritation of the cheeks and lips.
Duration of Treatment
  • Varies by Case:
    • The length of treatment depends on the severity of the misalignment and the type of braces used.
    • Treatment typically lasts between 1 to 3 years.
Removal of Braces
  • Removing Brackets and Wires:
    • Once the desired alignment is achieved, the orthodontist removes the brackets and wires.
    • The process is relatively quick and painless.
  • Cleaning and Polishing:
    • Teeth are cleaned and polished to remove any adhesive residue.
Post-Treatment Care
  • Retainers:
    • Retainers are provided to maintain the new alignment of the teeth.
    • They can be removable or fixed (bonded to the back of the teeth).
  • Wearing Schedule:
    • Initially, retainers may need to be worn full-time, gradually reducing to nighttime use only.
    • The orthodontist will provide specific instructions based on your case.
  • Follow-Up Visits:
    • Regular follow-up visits are scheduled to monitor the stability of the teeth and ensure the retainers are functioning correctly.
Long-Term Maintenance
  • Oral Hygiene:
    • Continue practicing good oral hygiene to maintain healthy teeth and gums.
    • Regular dental check-ups and cleanings are essential.
  • Lifestyle Adjustments:
    • Avoid habits that can negatively impact your teeth alignment, such as biting nails or using teeth as tools.
