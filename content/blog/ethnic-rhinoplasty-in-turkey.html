For Which Deformations Can Ethnic Rhinoplasty be Planned?

Ethnic rhinoplasty, like other types of rhinoplasty, requires fine workmanship and great care. The nasal deformities in which this surgery can be performed are as follows;
  • very small nose structure
  • Very flat descending nose structure
  • Incompatibility of the nose, chin and forehead
  • Very wide nostrils
  • Wide nose tip structure
  • Very upturned nose
  • Asymmetric nose structure
  • The dorsal part of the nose is closer to the face than it should be
