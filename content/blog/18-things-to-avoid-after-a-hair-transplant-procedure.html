1. Driving

Sometimes, a hair transplant is performed under strong sedation. If you have got been given a sedative, don't drive for the following 24 hours. Confirm you have got someone to drive you to and from your appointment. It is not recommended to get behind the wheel in a short time after the operation, as the painkillers or drugs to be applied to make the hair transplant comfortable and easy may dull your driving ability in Turkey.

2. Sleeping Flat

To mitigate the danger of swelling, it's essential that you just hold your head elevated for the primary seven days after treatment. Some clients think they only need to hold their heads elevated for some days. Keep in mind, however, that not everyone experiences swelling after hair implants. If you don’t feel a small pressure around your forehead, there's no reason why you can’t sleep as normal. If you discover you're experiencing mild swelling, we recommend converting your recliner into temporary bed sheets. It’s not ideal, but some people find their recliners to be way more comfortable than sleeping on top of 4 pillows. The aim is to hold your head elevated by 45 degrees. Lying on your head right after the hair transplant operation can delay the procedure, hurt you and cause unexpected results. It is up to you to prevent this. Pay attention to your doctor's recommendations and try not to lie on your head.

3. Ignoring Your Labels

One of the worst things you can do after your hair transplant procedure is to ignore the labels on your prescription and over-the-counter medications. Antibiotics should be taken exactly as prescribed to mitigate the chance of infection. Moreover, you ought to not drive after taking prescription analgesics if the warning label advises you against operating heavy equipment and machinery. You furthermore may want to be sure you’re not taking an excessive amount of any kind of medication. Finally, take your medication with a meal if advised on the label. Taking certain medications, especially painkillers, on an empty stomach can cause gastrointestinal distress. Not taking the drugs prescribed by the doctor after hair transplantation or taking more than the dose may cause undesirable results. In order to prevent this, you are obliged to do exactly what you are told. Pay attention to what the doctor says for your own health and the health of your hair in Turkey.

4. Forgetting The Ice

Do you remember the RICE technique your parents taught you once you got hurt as a child? The key to treating swelling “injuries” is rest, ice, compression and elevation. We’ve already covered the elevation. If you experience any swelling, we strongly recommend that you just apply ice for 20 to half-hour just above your eyebrows. This is often a wonderful preventative measure, as well, whether or not you don’t have any swelling, you will want to try and do this three to four days after your hair restoration. You will even find that ice is all you need to ease any general discomfort you’re feeling. The cold effect of ice, can both reduce swelling and help relieve pain if there is any. After the hair transplant operation, ice will also help you relax. Caution, do not put the ice directly on your head.

5. Scrubbing Your Hair Or Shower

For the primary 48 hours after your hair transplant procedure, don't wash your hair. On the third day after treatment, you'll be able to wash your hair gently. Don't wash your hair too aggressively through the fifth day after treatment. Also, don't blast the stream onto your scalp directly. Rather, use a cup to rinse the shampoo out of your hair. If you suffer from itching, you can apply a conditioner to your scalp gently. Taking a shower or scratching the head severely after a hair transplant operation may cause undesirable results. To prevent this, it is very important to listen to the doctor's post-operative care.

6. Dyeing Your Hair

You can’t dye your hair within four weeks of your hair restoration.  If you feel like you can’t make it four weeks without dying your hair, get it done shortly before your treatment. There are not any negative side effects related to dying your hair shortly before your hair transplant procedure, but afterwards, the harsh chemicals found in dyestuff can compromise the success of the transplanted follicles. For several weeks after the transplantation, the transplanted follicles are in an incredibly fragile state. They need to get all the care they need. After the hair transplant operation, dyeing the hair is both difficult and can cause undesirable results. It is useful to take the advice of your doctor. We all know that water is crucial always, but not everyone knows that hydration is even more important before and after your transplant. A good general rule of thumb is that you simply should drink half your weight in ounces of water. For instance, if you weigh 120 pounds, you should aim to drink 60 ounces (7.5 cups) of water daily.
