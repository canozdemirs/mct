What Can Happen in the Early Period

Bleeding: It gradually decreased due to the drugs given in the tumescent technique and the use of a thin cannula. Natural leaks seen in the first 24 hours should not be confused with bleeding. Infection: Extremely rare. The use of antibiotics after the procedure and compliance with hygiene rules reduce this risk. Antibiotics are given for prevention. Increased sensitivity in a certain area, increase in temperature and temperature may be signs of infection. In this case, you should call your doctor. Excessive bruising and swelling: It is natural to have some bruising and swelling in the treated area. excessive bruises; looseness of the skin structure, less use of the corset, some medications are taken, may be due to the bleeding tendency of the person. Swelling after liposuction is considerably reduced within 1-3 months and the final result is obtained at the end of 6-12 months. Although the bruises that occur vary from person to person, they disappear in almost all patients within a few weeks. Seroma: Extra fluid may accumulate in the areas where liposuction is applied. Additional application intervention may be required to drain this fluid. Numbness in the application area: There may be temporary loss of sensation on the skin after liposuction. Decreased or complete loss of sensation is very rare and may not improve completely. Allergic reaction: Rarely, local reactions to plasters, suture materials, and topical preparations have been reported. Systemic reactions to drugs used during surgery are more serious. Allergic reactions require additional treatment.
