What is rhinoplasty?

A nose job also called rhinoplasty, is an operation performed to change the shape and/or size of the nose. Since the nose is located in the centre of the face, even minor changes in the nose cause serious improvements in the overall attractiveness of the face. Rhinoplasty is one of the oldest examples of cosmetic surgery and is still in high demand today.

What are the procedures to be done before nose surgery?

Before the rhinoplasty surgery, be sure to share with your doctor all the medications you are using and your habits such as smoking and alcohol use. In particular, drugs with blood-thinning effects should be discontinued one week before the operation, while some other drugs may need to be used without interruption, including on the day of surgery. Apart from this, an anesthesiologist evaluation and blood tests will be performed to control bleeding risk and some infections before the surgery. When are the silicone sheets and plaster placed in the nose removed in nose surgery?

While the silicone sheets inside the nose are removed on the 2nd or 3rd day, the thermoplastic plaster outside the nose stays for 7-10 days. Since the silicone sheets do not stick into the nose, pain does not occur during removal.

What is health tourism?

Health Tourism; If we use these scientific criteria to explain health tourism, we can say that it is the desire of a person to leave their own nation and seek treatment in another country in order to restore their health. Health tourism in the world and in Turkey is a rapidly rising trend in recent years and is a very important alternative tourism type. It is necessary to include all kinds of treatment for health within the scope of health tourism. However, health tourism is generally examined in 3 groups. 1-Medical Tourism 2-Thermal Tourism 3-Geriatrics and Disabled Tourism Medical tourism is a sort of tourism that occurs when a nation's medical resources are insufficient, or when a patient has to be treated in a hospital in another country or undergo surgery. Thermal tourism is a sort of tourism aimed at rehabilitating and resting in thermal facilities in areas where thermal waters have been recognized to be curative for years, for both bodily and spiritual well-being. Tourism for the Elderly and Disabled: Long-term stays with social activities; sightseeing tours and occupational therapy for the elderly; aged care services; rehabilitation services at the clinical hotel; it includes special care and sightseeing excursions for the disabled. Clinical hotels, leisure areas, holiday resorts, and nursing homes are the most common service locations.

For What Purposes Is Health Tourism Used?

There may be different reasons why patients travel for treatment purposes. Dental, eye, cosmetic, cardiovascular and joint prosthesis, infertility therapy, and in vitro fertilization are all topics that come up often in health tourism. In addition to these, the number of patients who make health tourism for simple examinations or for control and examination for a disease is high. The following are some of the most common reasons for people to travel for medical treatment:
  • Scarcity or absence of high-tech healthcare and professional human resources in their home country
  • Long standby time
  • The fact because health services are expensive in their own countries, the same service can be obtained more economically in other countries
  • Desire to receive quality health care
  • Doesn't want his surgery to be known for any reason in his own country
  • Having problems with health insurance,
  • The chronic patients, the elderly and the disabled want to be treated in a different environment,
  • People who are addicted to drugs and alcohol want to be indifferent or more suited situations.
  • Desire to take a vacation with treatment
  • Desiring to benefit from the climatic and geographical characteristics of a country different from his country (such as seeing countries with forests, highlands, different history and culture)
  • Desire to meet with thermal waters in countries with thermal facilities
Health tourism is very diverse and may have many reasons. The main reason is that patients want to solve their health problems in a different country with better facilities or to receive the same service more economically.
