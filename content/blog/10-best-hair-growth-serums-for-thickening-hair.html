Do Hair Serums Always Work? Do They Guarantee?

Although hair serums make the hair look lively and healthy, they do not guarantee hair growth. In order for hair to grow, you must already have hair follicles. If there is no hair follicles or if the number is low, hair serums will not be the definitive solution. As a result, hair serums cannot guarantee new hair growth and do not give the same results for everyone.
