What is Bichectomy and how does it enhance facial features?

Bichectomy, also known as cheek reduction surgery, is a cosmetic procedure aimed at sculpting the face by removing the buccal fat pads in the cheeks. These fat pads can give the face a rounder or fuller appearance, which some individuals may wish to refine. By carefully removing them, Bichectomy helps contour the lower face, creating a more chiseled and youthful look.

The procedure enhances facial features by defining the cheekbones and sharpening facial angles. Removing the buccal fat pads accentuates natural contours, highlights the jawline, and produces a slimmer, more sculpted appearance. The result is a balanced and harmonious face, with improved proportion between the upper and lower regions.

What is the recovery process like after Bichectomy surgery?

The recovery process after Bichectomy surgery is relatively quick and straightforward, making it an appealing option for many. Since Bichectomy is a minimally invasive procedure that involves small incisions inside the mouth (no external cuts or visible scarring), the recovery time is much shorter than other facial surgeries. Patients typically experience mild swelling and discomfort in the first few days following surgery, but this can be easily managed with prescribed pain medication and cold compresses. Most patients can return to work and light activities within 5-7 days after the procedure. However, it is advised to avoid strenuous activities and exercise for at least 2-3 weeks to allow for proper healing. During the recovery period, it is important to follow the surgeon’s instructions carefully to reduce the risk of complications such as infection or poor healing. Swelling typically subsides within the first week, but it can take a few months for the final results to be fully visible.
