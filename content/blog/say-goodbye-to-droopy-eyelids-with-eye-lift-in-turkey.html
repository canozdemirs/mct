What is the recovery process like after an eye lift procedure in Turkey?

One of the most common concerns patients have before undergoing an eye lift (blepharoplasty) is the recovery process—and we’re here to tell you that it’s often easier and smoother than you might expect. Immediately after your procedure, you may experience mild swelling, bruising, and tightness around the eyes, which are all completely normal. These symptoms usually peak within the first 48 hours and start to subside within a few days. Cold compresses and prescribed eye drops can help ease any discomfort and accelerate healing. Most patients feel well enough to resume light daily activities within 5 to 7 days, though it's best to avoid strenuous exercise and heavy lifting for about 2 to 3 weeks. Your surgeon will remove any non-dissolvable stitches around the fifth to seventh day, during your follow-up appointment. Are the results of eyelid surgery permanent, and how long do they typically last?

If you’re considering eyelid surgery (blepharoplasty), you’re probably wondering: "Is it a one-time procedure, or will I need it again in the future?" The good news is that for most patients, the results are long-lasting and often permanent in the upper eyelids. Eyelid surgery is designed to remove excess skin, fat, and sometimes muscle that cause droopiness and puffiness around the eyes. Once these tissues are removed or repositioned, they don’t come back. For the upper eyelids, the results can last 10 to 15 years or even longer, depending on your skin type, lifestyle, and the natural aging process. The lower eyelids, which often address under-eye bags or sagging, typically offer very long-lasting results as well. Many patients find they never need a second procedure, especially if they maintain a healthy lifestyle and protect their skin from sun damage. However, it's essential to recognize that, although the surgical results are stable, the natural aging process will continue. Over time, your skin may lose elasticity, and minor changes can occur. But even years later, you'll still look more refreshed and youthful than if you had never undergone the surgery.
