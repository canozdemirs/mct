What Is Nose Aesthetic (Rhinoplasty)?

It is a surgical procedure to change or correct the shape of the nose. We also call it as rhinoplasty or rhinoplasty. Rhinoplasty is the shaping of the nose, whose origin is rhinion in Latin. The dissatisfaction with the shape and appearance of the nose due to its natural state or subsequent defects is corrected by rhinoplasty surgery. Shape change in accordance with the characteristic features of the face occurs by restructuring the anatomical (cartilage, bone and soft tissue) structures of the nose with surgical techniques. Rhinoplasty is a medical art that blends anatomy, physiology and living tissue knowledge with aesthetic vision. The nose, which is an important component of facial appearance with its external structure, is also an indispensable respiratory organ with its internal structure and function. In the treatment of septum irregularity (septum deviation), which is the most common cause of nasal congestion, septum surgery and complementary procedures are applied together. Natural nasal breathing, that is, the normal function of the nose is ensured. The nose can change shape over time, as the age progresses, it grows, sags and the skin becomes thicker. The most affected area as a result of facial trauma is the nose. Nasal congestion develops as a result of collapse, fracture and slippage in bone and cartilage structure as well as deformities. Often, rhinoplasty is performed with the desire to correct or change the shape of the nose. While the purpose is aesthetic, the function must be preserved and not impaired. The history of aesthetic nose surgeries, which we call rhinoplasty, is quite old. There is no incision in the outer part of the nose. With this method, it is tried to shape the nose by feeling more with the fingers due to limited vision. Who Can Have Rhinoplasty (Nose Aesthetics)?

Persons who have realistic expectations, know what they want, are psychologically normal, understand the rhinoplasty process, are highly motivated, have no serious health problems that prevent surgery, and are at the age required for bone and cartilage formation are suitable candidates for rhinoplasty.

Age in Rhinoplasty? At What Age Can Nose Aesthetics Be Performed?

Surgery can be performed after the age of 16-17 in girls and 17-18 in boys, when cartilage and bone development is largely completed. However, I should also mention that the development of cartilage and bone continues until the age of 22, albeit a little. Therefore, surgery should be planned after the age of 18 unless there is a very obvious deformity. There is no upper age limit for rhinoplasty if there are no medical problems and it is not inconvenient for the person to receive anesthesia. Surgery can be performed at the age of 70. However, people considering rhinoplasty at an advanced age should reconsider their expectations.

If the Person Who Will Have Rhinoplasty Has Other Health Problems? About Rhinoplasty and General Health Status?

Most of the rhinoplasty candidates do not have significant health problems. However, rhinoplasty candidates with chronic diseases (hypertension, diabetes, bleeding and coagulation disorders, lung diseases, etc.) should not undergo surgery without being sure that these diseases are under control and without the approval of the relevant doctor. Those with active psychological problems may have more difficulties in this process. For this reason, they should take the decision of surgery when they are psychologically stable.

Photo Shooting and Design Before Nose Aesthetics

After the examination, the photo shoot begins. Front, right and left side, right and left oblique, bottom and top photos are taken. On the photographs, together with the patient, the problems and the points to be corrected are evaluated. Then, with a special program, how to make a nose in surgery is designed. The size of the nose, the upturning-shape of the tip of the nose, the arch of the nose, the nostrils, etc. drawn by the program. Usually 2-3 drawings are enough. Then, these designs are put side by side and it is decided together which drawing looks better and which drawing has a more balanced and harmonious nose on the face as a whole. These drawings will also help the patient to get used to the new nose (first the eye then the mind gets used to the change in the nose, the adaptation process includes the early postoperative period). It also helps us to plan the surgery. The drawings do not guarantee the result of the surgery, they determine what we will aim for in the surgery. In general, 90% of the time, we get the result that we specified or very close to it.

Preparation Before Rhinoplasty (Nose Aesthetics)

Before the operation, routine blood tests are performed to determine whether there is a risky situation for the operation. An anesthesiologist examination is performed together with the test results, and it is determined whether there is a risk for anesthesia. In the meantime, you must specify whether you have any diagnosed disease, previous surgery, a drug you use regularly, allergies (drugs, food, etc.). For at least 1 week before and 1 week after surgery; it is recommended to quit blood thinners, vitamins and herbal supplements, and smokers to quit smoking. Before the operation, it is necessary to be hungry and thirsty for at least 8 hours. If the operation will be in the morning, you should not eat or drink anything (including water) after 12 the night before. If the surgery will be in the afternoon, a light breakfast can be eaten early in the morning. It will be comforting to take a warm shower and go to bed before the surgery and try to be positive that everything will be fine for the next day. After the surgery, easy-to-wear, front-buttoned clothes are required. Female patients who will undergo surgery should go to the hospital without make-up and nail polish.

Healing Process After Rinoplasty (Nose Aesthetics)

It is recommended to stay in the hospital for 1 night after the operation. Your stay in the hospital is preferred both in terms of follow-up and in terms of feeling safe. However, patients who have surgery in the morning can be discharged 3-4 hours after the surgery if there is no problem. In order to minimize the possibility of swelling and bruising after the surgery, it is recommended to lie upright at 45 degrees and apply ice for 10-15 minutes every hour. This practice should continue for the first 3 days. After the 3rd day, you can sleep lower, there is no need to put ice. Applying more ice than recommended reduces tissue nutrition and increases skin edema. Mild to moderate pain may occur after surgery. Patients usually rate the level of pain on a scale of 1-4 out of 10. These pains can be easily relieved with normal painkillers (eg paracetamol). Silicone splints (formerly tampons) are removed 3-4 days after the surgery. Removing the splints takes 5-10 seconds, during which there may be a slight pain. After the silicone splints are removed, crusting and edema occur in the nose for a few weeks. In this period, which is the wound healing process, there will be occasional congestion and occasional opening in the nose. Crusting takes longer in patients who have deviation surgery and nasal concha reduction with rhinoplasty. During this period, salt water (saline, sea water, ocean water) is used to soften the crusts and reduce edema. The plastic splint/splint (formerly plaster) outside the nose is removed 7-8 days after the operation. This procedure is painless. After the splint/outer splint is removed, necessary controls are made and the tape is adhered. The bands are removed after 1 week. There is no harm in getting the tapes wet, you can take a shower so that the tapes get wet. If the bands do not disturb visually, you can go to work or school 8-10 days after the surgery. As the swelling goes away, the nose will return to its appearance at the end of the surgery. A warm shower can be taken 1 day after the operation.
