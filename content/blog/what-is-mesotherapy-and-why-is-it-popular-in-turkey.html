What is mesotherapy and how does it work?

Mesotherapy is a minimally invasive cosmetic treatment that involves injecting a mixture of vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and other beneficial substances directly into the middle layer of the skin (called the mesoderm). The goal is to nourish, hydrate, and rejuvenate the skin, improve circulation, and stimulate collagen and elastin production. How it works:
  • Tiny injections deliver a customized cocktail of nutrients into the targeted area.
  • These substances help repair damaged cells, boost skin metabolism, and promote natural healing processes.
  • The treatment can improve skin texture, reduce wrinkles, and address issues like cellulite, hair loss, or localized fat.
Because mesotherapy works directly on problem areas at the cellular level, it is considered an effective and versatile option for skin rejuvenation and body contouring.

Who is a good candidate for mesotherapy?

A good candidate for mesotherapy is someone who:
  • Wants to improve the appearance and health of their skin, such as reducing fine lines, wrinkles, or dullness
  • Is looking to treat localized issues like cellulite, stretch marks, or mild fat deposits
  • Suffers from hair thinning or hair loss and wants to stimulate hair growth
  • Prefers a minimally invasive, non-surgical treatment with little downtime
  • Has realistic expectations about the results and understands that multiple sessions may be needed
  • Does not have serious skin infections, allergies to injection ingredients, or certain medical conditions that could complicate the procedure
Mesotherapy is suitable for people of various ages and skin types, but a professional consultation is essential to determine if it’s the right treatment based on individual needs and health.

Which skin or health problems can mesotherapy treat?

Mesotherapy can be used to treat a variety of skin and health concerns, including:
  • Skin Rejuvenation: Improves skin texture, hydration, and elasticity, reducing fine lines and wrinkles
  • Cellulite Reduction: Helps break down fat deposits and improves circulation in affected areas
  • Localized Fat Removal: Targets small areas of stubborn fat for body contouring
  • Hair Loss and Thinning: Stimulates hair follicles to promote hair growth and strengthen existing hair
  • Stretch Marks: Improves the appearance of stretch marks by enhancing skin regeneration
  • Dark Circles and Puffy Eyes: Reduces pigmentation and improves circulation under the eyes
  • Hyperpigmentation: Helps lighten dark spots and even out skin tone
Because it delivers nutrients directly to the affected area, mesotherapy can offer targeted treatment for these issues with minimal invasiveness.

Is mesotherapy safe? Are there any side effects?

Mesotherapy is generally considered safe when performed by a qualified and experienced medical professional. However, like any medical procedure, it carries some risks and potential side effects. Common side effects include:
  • Mild swelling or redness at the injection site
  • Bruising or tiny pinpoint bleeding
  • Temporary itching or tenderness
  • Minor discomfort during and after treatment
Less common but possible risks:
  • Allergic reactions to the injected substances
  • Infection if the procedure is not done under sterile conditions
  • Uneven results or lumps under the skin if injections are not done properly
To minimize risks, it’s important to choose a certified clinic with trained doctors and to follow all pre- and post-treatment care instructions carefully. Patients should also disclose any allergies, medical conditions, or medications to their practitioner beforehand.
