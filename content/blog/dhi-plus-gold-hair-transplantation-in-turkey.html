Difference Between DHI and FUE Hair Transplant

The FUE hair transplant technique differs from the DHI technique in several ways. In the FUE method in Turkey, bleeding may occur on the scalp while opening channels, whereas in the DHI technique, there is no bleeding during channel opening because the hair follicles are implanted directly without creating separate incisions. In DHI, both channel opening and implantation are performed simultaneously using a specialized pen.

Another key difference between FUE and DHI is the handling of grafts. With the DHI technique, each follicle is implanted immediately after extraction, while in the FUE method, follicles wait outside the scalp before implantation, which may increase the risk of damage. Both techniques are widely performed and highly developed in Turkey, offering patients safe, effective, and advanced treatment options.
