What technologies are used for laser eye surgery in Turkey?

In 2025, clinics in Turkey use the latest laser eye surgery technologies, comparable to top hospitals in Europe and North America. These include:
  • Femtosecond lasers for bladeless LASIK procedures
  • Excimer lasers for reshaping the cornea with high precision
  • SMILE (Small Incision Lenticule Extraction) for minimally invasive correction
  • Wavefront-guided LASIK for customized vision correction
  • Topography-guided systems for treating irregular corneas
These machines are FDA-approved and regularly maintained to meet safety standards. The technology allows for faster healing, fewer complications, and better visual outcomes. Clinics also use advanced eye-mapping tools to create 3D models of your cornea before surgery. This ensures that every treatment is tailored specifically to your eyes. Turkey’s investment in technology, combined with experienced staff, makes it one of the most technologically advanced countries for vision correction in 2025.

Is there a recovery period after the procedure?

Yes, there is a short recovery period after laser eye surgery, but most patients in Turkey experience minimal downtime and quick healing. Depending on the type of procedure (LASIK, PRK, or SMILE), recovery varies slightly. For LASIK:
  • Most patients see clearly within 24 to 48 hours
  • You can usually return to work in 2–3 days
  • Full visual stabilization may take a few weeks
For PRK (surface laser):
  • Recovery is slower; vision improves over 5–7 days
  • Mild discomfort or light sensitivity may occur
  • Healing is complete in about 2 weeks
For SMILE:
  • Fast healing with less dryness and discomfort
  • Functional vision returns in 1–2 days
After surgery, doctors in Turkey provide eye drops, written instructions, and follow-up care. You’ll need to avoid rubbing your eyes and stay away from water or heavy exercise for a few days.
