What is the Duration of Eyelid Aesthetic Surgery?

Before eyelid surgery, the treating healthcare professional assesses the patient's eye area and medical suitability. If the patient who is decided to have the operation has eye pressure, we want them to be examined by an ophthalmologist beforehand. After the necessary controls are made, we plan the operation. Upper eyelid aesthetics takes between 30 minutes and 1 hour. In this surgery, both excess skin on the upper eyelid and excess fat are removed. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia. In lower eyelid surgery, excess fat is removed from the eye (transconjuntival approach), reducing the complication rate of lower eyelid pulling. If there is excess skin, the excess skin tissue is removed from the lower eyelid with an incision made 1 mm below the eyelashes. This procedure is done under general anesthesia. Ice application after surgery is important. Ice application for 36-48 hours minimizes the bruising around the eyes. You can return to social life in 3-4 days after upper eyelid surgery. The number of days after the lower eyelid surgery reaches 1 week. How is Eyelid Aesthetics (Blepharoplasty) Performed?

Blepharoplasty surgery is usually performed under local anesthesia with sedation. The fact that the patient is not fully asleep under general anesthesia is more comfortable and safe for the patient, and it is more suitable for the doctor when the patient's cooperation is needed. Since the eyelids are movable tissues, unlike the nose or breast, the surgeon can ask the patient to open and close their eyes, look up and down, and control the necessary symmetry settings during the operation. Although the duration of the operations varies according to the method to be applied, they are terminated within 1-2 hours. Upper lids and lower lids can be corrected in the same session or separately. If there is an existing valve ptosis or any valve deformity during valve aesthetics, these should also be taken care of in the same session. Lower lid operations can sometimes be combined with a mid-face lift. The incisions during the operation are applied by using the natural lines formed by the folding of the skin on the upper lids for the upper lids, and by using the thin natural lines at the bottom of the eyelashes, or from the inner surface of the lower lid in suitable patients and closed with very thin aesthetic sutures, so that there is usually no obvious incision scar afterwards. Although it is very unlikely that incision scars may remain in the bodies that cause excessive wound reaction (keloid), it is possible to intervene with laser afterwards. The patients are discharged on the same day after the operation without the need for hospitalization and the eyes are not covered with bandages. The pain is either non-existent or minimal during or after the operation, it can be relieved with oral painkillers, which is one of the most important factors that increase patient comfort during the operation. However, there may be swelling, bruising on the eyelids due to trauma, and minimal itching at the incision sites. Intense ice application and topical drops and ointments are sufficient for the first few days after the operation. Stitches, if used, can be removed in about a week to 10 days. After the operation, patients quickly return to their normal lives. Since the eyes are not closed in the post-op period, it is free to read, write, watch television and use a computer from the first day. Patients are usually 2-3 days after surgery. They can go out for days and continue their social life. However, it is necessary to take a break from heavy activities and heavy sports for about 2-3 weeks. Make-up can be done 1-2 weeks after the stitches are removed, and contact lenses can be used 1 week and 10 days after the stitches are removed. Returning to work is linked to the work and social structure. In general, office workers can return to work by 2-3 days, but it should be taken into account that there may be bruises and swellings around the eyes. In occupations that require heavy physical activity, it will be more appropriate to rest for at least a week and 10 days until the stitches are removed. In the periods following the recovery, the eyelids will maintain their new structure and health for many years, which is one of the advantageous parts of the operations. 

What awaits me after the surgery?

There may be dressings consisting of bands around the eyes for up to a week after eyelid aesthetic surgery. After they are removed, the dressing is continued with the cream that the person will use at home. Swelling and bruising continue for a week after eyelid surgery. In the 2nd week, swelling and bruises begin to heal and a tolerance is formed to be able to walk among the community. Complete wound healing after eyelid surgery takes 6 months to 1 year. Although there is no edema to a degree that disturbs people for 6 months to 1 year, it may take 6 months to 1 year to obtain the complete surgical result.

-What are the risks of the surgery?

If the operation is preferred with general anesthesia, risks related to anesthesia can be seen. In addition, if more excision is made from the lower eyelid, rare complications such as inward or outward turning of the eyelid may occur.

-Can eyelid surgery be performed together with a different facial aesthetic surgery?

Eyelid surgery can be combined with many facial aesthetics such as rhinoplasty, mid-face lift and lip lift, and more aesthetic results can be obtained. There is no harm in combining it with another surgery.

-How many hours does the surgery take?

Eyelid aesthetic surgery takes between 30 minutes and 60 minutes if only the upper eyelid is to be made, while it takes between 40 and 60 minutes if only a few eyelids are to be made.

-Is it understandable that I had an operation?

It can be understood that the operation was performed due to edema and bruises in the first 2 weeks after the operation. However, after the recovery period, it is not understood that he had surgery because it is a natural appearance, since he completely returns to his own eye shape.

-How long will I stay in the hospital?

-How are the surgery prices determined?

-Can both men and women have this surgery?

-Will there be any scars at the end of the surgery?
