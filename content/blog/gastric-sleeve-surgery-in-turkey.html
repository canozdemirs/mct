What is gastric sleeve surgery?

Gastric sleeve surgery is a safe and efficient surgical weight loss method performed under general anesthesia to help people with obesity problems lose weight. Laparoscopic surgery is the most commonly used surgery today. The surgery is performed through a small incision in the upper abdomen using a laparoscope. In this procedure, approximately 80 percent of the stomach is removed so that the rest remains in the form of a tube, without any changes in the intestines. Thanks to sleeve gastrectomy surgery, the size of the stomach is reduced so that the amount of food that the person can eat is limited. In addition, the taken part of the stomach contains the region where the hunger hormone is produced. Entering this area triggers hormonal changes, which help to reduce weight and keep people full for a long time by reducing food intake. Hormonal changes will help reduce the health problems associated with obesity such as high blood pressure or heart disease. In other words, by applying laparoscopic surgery, the tubular part of the stomach that provides the continuity of the esophagus and intestines will be preserved and a large part of the stomach body will be removed. In the surgery, the most elongated and enlarged part of the stomach and managing the appetite is removed. The main purpose of this surgery is to reduce the volume of the stomach and food intake. Thanks to the hormone called Ghrelin secreted from the removed part of the stomach, appetite control and a feeling of satiety are provided. It is a more physiological method because it maintains a normal intestinal tract and does not cause complications. In recent years, this method is only seen as an effective bariatric surgery and is widely used.

How is gastric sleeve surgery performed in Turkey?

Gastric Sleeve procedure is performed completely by laparoscopic (closed) surgical method. Laparoscopic surgery is performed by making many small incisions, and the ports opened through these incisions are used for hand tools to reach the abdomen. One of them is the surgical telescope connected to the video camera, the others are used to introduce special surgical instruments. The surgeon will watch the surgery on a video monitor. Carbon dioxide gas is injected into the abdomen and the abdomen is inflated. In the next stage, a special tool called a trocar is used to enter the abdomen. First of all, a silicone tube is placed from the mouth to the stomach outlet in order to adjust the remaining stomach width. The stomach will be separated from the fatty tissue surrounding the vessels and adjacent spleen. In the next stage, the excess part of the stomach is cut and separated with special devices called staples. There is still a stomach volume of about 80-150 ml. This part, which is separated from the stomach, is taken from the abdomen and sent to pathology. Afterward, the bleeding in the cut and stapled part is controlled. Additional metal clips can be used to control bleeding or additional sutures can be placed if necessary some special medications can be applied to the wound area to reduce bleeding. A silicone drain is then placed in the surgical area to remove the accumulated fluids. The wound area is closed aesthetically, thus ending the operation.

What are the advantages of gastric sleeve surgery?

Advantages of this operation include 40-70% weight loss in an average year, preservation of stomach function due to no change in gastric inlet and outlet valves during the operation, and low probability of vitamin and mineral deficiency since no changes are made in the small intestine. In addition, performing gastric sleeve surgery with laparoscopic methods provides less pain relief after the procedure, there is less risk of infection, you can recover faster and you can return to daily activities, and it provides very small incisions on the skin and fewer hospital stay.

How is the recovery process after gastric sleeve surgery?

You can return to work 7-10 days after the operation. For people with heavy work, the restriction lasts until the 3rd week after the surgery. It is important for those who return to work after the 7th day to avoid movements that compress the abdominal wall. Daily activities will reduce the risk of embolism. Laparoscopic gastric reduction surgery allows patients to return to their daily lives quickly thanks to the closed method, and enclosed surgical operations, small incisions of about half an inch are found on the skin and these incisions, which are hidden sutures, heal completely after a week. The suture of the shrinking stomach will heal completely in about ten days. So patients are at risk of leakage for about ten days and they should be very careful. For this reason, you should be careful not to lift heavy activities and not to eat solid foods for the first ten days. If the patient who underwent surgery does not work in a heavy work done with muscle strength, they can start working after a week.
