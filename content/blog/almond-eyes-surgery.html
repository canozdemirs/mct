What is the aim of almond eyes surgery?

The main goals of lateral canthoplasty are to:

Eye Shape Modification

The procedure can be used to alter the shape of the eyes, making them appear more "almond-shaped" or creating a specific desired aesthetic.

Eyelid Tightening

Almond eye surgery, also known as lateral canthoplasty, can help tighten the eyelid and improve the support of the lateral canthus, which can be beneficial for people with laxity or drooping in the outer corner of the eye.

Eye Correction

In some cases, almond eye surgery is performed to correct abnormalities or conditions affecting the outer corner of the eye, such as ectropion (outward-turning of the eyelid) or canthal dystopia (misalignment of the outer canthi).

Is almond eyes surgery permanent?

Almond eye surgery is generally considered to be a permanent procedure. During the surgery, the surgeon makes changes to the outer corner of the eye by repositioning the lateral canthus and securing it with sutures. Once the tissues have healed and the sutures have dissolved or been removed, the new position of the lateral canthus should be stable. However, it's important to note that while almond eye surgery itself is considered permanent, the aging process and other factors can still affect the appearance of the eyes over time. For example, natural aging can lead to changes in the skin and supporting tissues around the eyes, which might alter the appearance of the almond eyes surgery results. Additionally, as with any surgical procedure, there is always a small chance of complications or unexpected outcomes that might require further corrective measures. To maintain the best possible results and reduce the impact of aging on the eyes, it's essential to follow post-operative instructions provided by the surgeon, practice good skin care, and protect the eyes from excessive sun exposure. Regular follow-up visits with the surgeon can also help monitor the long-term results of the lateral canthoplasty.

How does almond eyes surgery happen?

Almond eye surgery is a procedure that involves surgery. It can be done using local anesthesia, in which just the operated area is numbed with medicines, or general anesthesia, in which the patient is completely asleep. Although local anesthesia is used, the patient will not feel any pain. During the surgery, incisions outside the lower eyelids will be made. Retractor bands will be put through the incisions that will go inside the eye and around the upper cheekbones. As a result, the patient’s eyelid will be moved upward. Also, spacer grafts will be used to make sure that the retractor band does not get reattached. By this, a vertical length will be given to the eye.

Is there any side effects of almond eyes surgery?

Below are side effects that can be experienced temporarily after the almond eye surgery:
  • Pain
  • Bruising
  • Swelling
  • Mild bleeding
  • Nausea
  • Inflammation
If you are experiencing any of the side effects, your doctor will prescribe you antibiotics or painkillers. These side effects should go away after two weeks.

How does recovery after almond eyes surgery happen?

Recovery after the almond eye surgery is an essential phase in achieving successful results and healing properly. The exact recovery process can vary from person to person, but here is a general overview of what you can typically expect:

Immediate Post-Operative Period

  • After the surgery, you will be taken to a recovery area, where medical staff will monitor your vital signs and ensure you are comfortable.
  • Your eyes may be covered with bandages or a protective dressing to minimize swelling and promote healing.
  • You may experience some discomfort, bruising, and swelling around the surgical site. Pain medication prescribed by your surgeon can help manage any post-operative discomfort.
  • It's normal to have blurry vision and sensitivity to light for a short period after the surgery.

During the first week

  • You should take it easy and avoid any strenuous activities that could put stress on the surgical site.
  • Your surgeon may recommend using cold compresses to reduce swelling and bruising.
  • It's essential to keep your head elevated while resting to further reduce swelling.
  • Follow your surgeon's instructions on wound care, which may include keeping the area clean and avoiding rubbing or touching the eyes.

First Month

  • Swelling and bruising should gradually improve during the first month, but some mild swelling may persist.
  • You may need to use lubricating eye drops or ointments as prescribed by your surgeon to keep your eyes moist and comfortable.
  • Avoid wearing contact lenses until your surgeon gives you the green light to do so.
  • As your eyes heal, you may notice changes in the appearance of the almond eye surgery results.

First Few Months

  • It may take a few months for the final results of the almond eye surgery to become apparent as the tissues fully heal and settle.
  • Attend follow-up appointments with your surgeon as scheduled to monitor your progress and address any concerns.

Long-Term

  • After the initial healing period, the results of lateral canthoplasty are considered permanent, but natural aging processes can still affect the appearance of the eyes over time.
  • Protect your eyes from excessive sun exposure by wearing sunglasses with UV protection.
  • Throughout the recovery process, it's crucial to follow your surgeon's post-operative instructions diligently. If you have any concerns or notice any unusual symptoms during your recovery, don't hesitate to contact your surgeon for guidance. Every individual heals differently, so be patient with the recovery process and give your eyes ample time to heal properly.
