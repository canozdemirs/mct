How many types of rhinoplasty are there?

Rhinoplasty is basically done with open and closed techniques. Other than that, other rhinoplasty techniques mentioned are the methods that fall under the subtitles of the closed or open technique, taking the name of the technology used or a new name derived. These techniques are; Primary Rhinoplasty: In this method, which is the most commonly performed technique, aesthetic problems such as removing the nasal arch and reducing the wings are eliminated and the nose is made compatible with the face. Secondary Rhinoplasty: It is the secondary intervention applied in case the desired results cannot be obtained from the first rhinoplasty surgery. Typlasty: In this method, which is applied in cases such as low nose tip and wide wings, the nasal arch is not interfered with. It is known as nasal tip aesthetics. Septoplasty: In this method, which aims to correct the structure called the septum, arch-cartilage curvatures that can prevent breathing and cause aesthetic problems are corrected. Concha Surgery: The meat in the nose is called turbinate. The turbinates can grow over time due to different reasons and cause breathing problems. With this method, the concha is reduced and air channels are opened. Piezo-Micromotor Rhinoplasty: These methods, named after the technology and devices used during the surgery, facilitate the surgeon's work and help to obtain more effective results. Letdown Rhinoplasty: The wall forming the back of the nose is pulled back and the belt is removed.

What should I do before the operation?

Take a shower the day before the surgery and wash your face with an antibacterial soap. Do not wear make-up and nail polish while coming to the surgery. Your psychology on the day of surgery is very important. Our patients who are not stressed get through this process very easily. The patient who sleeps comfortably wakes up comfortably.

What should I do after the operation?

You spend the night of your surgery in the hospital. From that day, sleep with 3 pillows for 1 week. The application of ice for 10-15 minutes per hour on the day of surgery and the next day has very positive effects on swelling and bruising. It is important that you regularly use the medicines we have prescribed after your discharge. Except those:
  • Do not stay in a very hot place.
  • Avoid activities that increase your blood pressure. Do not tilt your head forward.
  • You can touch your nose, just don't get hit. Even the smallest blows can cause pain while in the splint.
  • Try to stay in an upright position.
  • After the second day, you can go out and walk around, it will not get infected.
  • You can breathe through the silicones placed in the nose. These silicones can create a cold feeling and cause a sneezing reflex. Don't panic, if you're going to sneeze, sneeze with your mouth open. If you sneeze, press your tongue against your palate, it usually passes.
  • At the end of a total of 2 weeks of splint period, apply the massages that I will show you to yourself several times a day.
  • Do not do heavy sports for 1 month.
  • Do not go to the sea for 1 month and to the pool for 2 months.
  • Do not wear glasses for at least 3 months.

Can breathing problems be corrected with rhinoplasty?

Rhinoplasty, which is performed to correct the shape of the nose and remove bone and meat, is also preferred by many patients for breathing problems. Rhinoplasty can correct their breathing problems, and this is possible. Thanks to the application of septorhinoplasty, as it is called in medicine, it is possible to both correct the shape of the nose and remove the septum that causes breathing difficulty.

What kind of anesthesia is used?

General anesthesia is used, it can also be completed with local anesthesia. In general, general anesthesia will be preferred for nasal aesthetic surgeries (rhinoplasty), which will be performed in a standard way, to keep the patients calm, not be affected psychologically, and for the doctors to work more comfortably.

How many days do I need to stay in the hospital?

As the patient receives general anesthesia, it is generally recommended to stay in the hospital overnight. You can return to daily life the next day. However, it is recommended that the patient spend one week at home after the surgery.

How many hours does the surgery take?

Between 2 and 2.5 hours. Although it varies from person to person, on average, a rhinoplasty surgery should be completed within this period.

How long does the nasal bandage last?

After an average of 7 to 10 days, the nasal bandage is removed by the doctor. The plaster splint is applied on the nose to protect the design of the nose after the operation is taken under the supervision of a doctor.

When can I return to work?

You can return to work and social life within 10-14 days after the surgery.

How should I sleep?

It is important to have your head in an elevated sleeping position after nose surgery. Therefore, it is recommended to sleep with 2 or more pillows. Another important sleeping position you should take after sleeping is to lie on your back.

Will there be any scars in rhinoplasty?

Since there is no incision on the skin during rhinoplasty performed with the closed technique, there is no scar after the operation. With the open technique, an incision is made over the skin. Where the incision is made, a scar will remain. This trace is not visible from the front. Generally, it doesn't bother people.

What is the age limit?

Emotional and physical development is extremely important in rhinoplasty. If rhinoplasty surgery is performed on an adolescent who has not completed his development, it disrupts the bone structure in the face and nose, and the nose image is deformed. Therefore, the lower limit of rhinoplasty is recommended as an average of 16 for women and 17 for men.
