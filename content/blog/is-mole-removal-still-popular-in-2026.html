Is there a difference between laser mole removal and surgical excision?

Many people wonder about the difference between laser mole removal and surgical excision. Both are popular methods to remove moles, but they work differently and suit different needs. Laser mole removal uses focused light beams to break down the mole’s pigment and tissue. It is a non invasive method, meaning no cuts or stitches are needed. This technique is best for small, flat, or slightly raised moles. Laser removal usually causes less pain and has a quicker healing time. It also lowers the chance of scarring because it targets only the mole without affecting the surrounding skin. On the other hand, surgical excision is a more traditional method. In this procedure, the mole is cut out completely with a scalpel. This is often preferred for larger, deeper, or suspicious moles that need to be tested for medical reasons. After the mole is removed, the doctor usually stitches the skin back together. Healing takes longer, and there might be a small scar, but this method guarantees the entire mole is gone. Choosing between these methods depends on the mole’s size, location, and type. Laser mole removal is quick and cosmetic friendly, while surgical excision is thorough and sometimes medically necessary. In 2026, both options are safe and effective. Consulting with a professional is important to decide the best choice for your skin and health. Laser mole removal vs surgical excision differ mainly in technique, healing time, and scarring risk.
