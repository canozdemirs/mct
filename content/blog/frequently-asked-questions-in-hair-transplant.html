How Long Does It Take to Get New Hair?

After a hair transplantation procedure in Turkey, red crusts may appear in the transplanted area. Washing your scalp once a day helps remove these crusts, which usually shed completely within 5–6 days. Initially, the transplanted hair may fall out, and patients typically regain their pre-transplant appearance within 7 to 15 days.

The overall look stabilizes after about a month, while new hair growth generally begins after two months. Significant changes in appearance are most noticeable during the third and fourth months. Full hair growth can take 7–8 months or more, depending on the individual.

You can engage in light physical activity even immediately after the procedure. You can do short and light walks and light cultural and physical movements.
