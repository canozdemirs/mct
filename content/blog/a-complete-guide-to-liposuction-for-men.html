What is liposuction?

Liposuction is a fat removal surgery that helps to break up and vacuum the fat masses that have taken place in the body. A stubborn layer of fat has spread over their bodies, the kind that people who lose weight or are overweight can never get rid of. No matter how hard a person tries, it is very difficult for this fat mass to be broken down. The person who wants to lose weight with diet and sports and have the slim appearance of his dreams thinks that his diet has failed due to this stubborn fat mass, and leaves the diet and sports unfinished. This term, especially in recent years, has become one of the first applications recommended to people who want to have fat removal. So much so that liposuction is an application that is highly preferred not only by women but also by men. It is the second most preferred aesthetic surgery by men. A lot of misinformation about this popular procedure has been instilled in the public. Overweight people who do not have enough research on liposuction think that they will lose their excess weight with liposuction. However, this is completely a mistake. Liposuction is not performed for the purpose of losing weight. The main purpose of this procedure is to purify the patient from the fat masses. Liposuction is not an obesity treatment. After the procedure, the patient does not lose more than ten 15 kilos suddenly.

Male "Ultrasound assisted RealDefinition liposuction surgical procedure" (Vaserhidef) is not a method to remove fat from the abdomen or waist area like superficial liposuction. There is no such thing as touching every part of every patient. Abdominal, waist and chest regions are the most desired ones by male patients who usually apply. The operation on the arms increases the prominence of the deltoid, biceps and triceps muscles. The operation is a procedure performed under general anaesthesia in the hospital and takes 2-7 hours. After the operation, the patient wears a special hi-def corset and uses this corset for 2 -4 weeks. The results appear immediately after the operation. There is no waiting period. Of course, swelling and bruises may occur, but all of these will pass within 7-10 days. Patients can return to their normal lives within 2-6 days.

How is Liposuction Surgery Performed?

Liposuction surgery begins with marking the areas desired by the patient and numbing these areas with local anaesthesia. Thanks to the local anaesthesia applied during this surgery, the patient can control and monitor the course of the surgery without feeling any pain. If the rate of fat mass that is planned to be separated from the body is high, the hospital applies general anaesthesia instead of local anaesthesia by foreseeing hospitalization for the patient. The patient is given a set of sedatives so that the patient can be comfortable during the operation. The sedative given frequently is only valid for patients who prefer local anaesthesia.

The cost of liposuction surgery may vary depending on several factors such as the number of areas treated, the amount of fat removed, the type of anaesthesia used (local or general), the surgeon’s experience, and whether hospitalization is required. What do tell to your doctor before the surgery?

All patients who will be operated on with the liposuction method should notify the specialist surgeon without skipping any issues related to their health status. Share all the information about your health history and condition, especially genetic and hereditary diseases, surgeries and medications used.

What to eat or stop consuming before the surgery?

If there are aspirin-derived drugs or nutritional supplements, fruits and vegetables prescribed for blood thinning, among the drugs you use, you should stop consuming them at least ten days before the surgery. Otherwise, both during and after liposuction surgery, the bleeding risks reach their maximum values ​​and important risks may occur for both the doctor and the patient. If you have harmful habits such as smoking and alcohol, you should completely eliminate these consumptions and start them at least ten days before. These habits should be continued with the same care until recovery occurs after the surgery. Otherwise, you should remember that your healing process will be prolonged and risks may occur. You should stay away from all sugary, chocolate and pastry foods. You should know that you can get through this process more easily by minimizing your salty-oily eating habits. There is no harm in taking foods such as meat, chicken, or fish, but you should not forget the consumption of vegetables and fruits, and you should stay away from sugar and salt as much as possible.
