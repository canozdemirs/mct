What kind of aftercare do patients receive in Turkey following a tummy tuck?

After a tummy tuck in Turkey, patients receive professional and attentive aftercare designed to support healing and maximize results. Right after surgery, patients are closely monitored in a recovery room by experienced medical staff. Vital signs are regularly checked, and pain is managed with prescribed medication to ensure comfort during the first few hours. The surgical area is cleaned and dressed with sterile materials, and patients are given clear instructions on how to take care of the incision site once they return to their hotel or accommodation. Follow-up visits are typically scheduled within the first few days to monitor healing and address any concerns. Compression garments are also provided as part of the post-surgery routine. These special garments help minimize swelling, support the abdominal muscles, and improve the overall shape of the body. Patients are advised to wear them for several weeks, depending on their individual recovery process. Mobility is encouraged soon after surgery, usually within 24 hours, to help prevent blood clots and promote circulation. However, patients are instructed to avoid strenuous activities, lifting heavy objects, or stretching the abdomen for at least 4–6 weeks. In addition to in person check ups, many clinics offer ongoing communication through phone or online platforms, so patients can ask questions or share updates after returning home. Detailed aftercare instructions, medication schedules, and lifestyle tips are all part of the patient support process. The aftercare provided in Turkey is comprehensive, well-organized, and patient-focused ensuring a safe and comfortable recovery for those undergoing tummy tuck procedures.
