What are the Popular Surgeries in Turkey?

Turkey is widely known for offering high-quality cosmetic and aesthetic procedures at a fraction of the cost compared to countries like the US or the UK. Turkey gives the best and multiple options for surgeries so you can have a healthy, beautiful, and aesthetically pleasing life.Some of the most popular surgeries in Turkey are in the fields of cosmetic surgery, bariatric surgery, dentistry, and hair transplantation. Here's a breakdown of the most sought-after surgeries in Turkey: Cosmetic surgeries like rhinoplasty, facelifts, breast augmentation, and liposuction. Hair transplants, especially FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction). Bariatric surgeries like gastric sleeve and gastric bypass. Dental procedures, such as implants, veneers, and full dental makeovers. Eye treatments, particularly LASIK surgery. Fertility treatments, including IVF, egg donation, and sperm donation. Let's take a look at some of the popular surgeries in Turkey. Cosmetic and Aesthetic Surgery
  • Rhinoplasty (Nose Job): Turkey is renowned for its skilled plastic surgeons, particularly in rhinoplasty. Many patients travel to Turkey for nose reshaping surgery due to the combination of quality care and affordability.
  • Breast Augmentation and Reduction: Both breast enlargement (using implants) and breast reduction surgeries are popular in Turkey. Surgeons in Turkey offer advanced techniques and post-operative care that ensures great results.
  • Facelifts (Rhytidectomy): Facelift surgeries are popular, especially among those looking to reverse the signs of aging. Skilled surgeons perform facelifts with minimal scarring and quick recovery times.
  • Liposuction: Liposuction to remove fat deposits from various areas of the body, including the abdomen, thighs, and arms, is a widely performed procedure in Turkey.
  • Butt Lift/Enhancement: Both Brazilian butt lifts (BBL) and implants for buttock augmentation are increasingly popular in Turkey.
  Hair Transplantation Hair transplants are one of the most well-known procedures that attract medical tourists to Turkey. With its affordable prices and advanced techniques, Turkey is considered a global leader in FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) hair transplantation. The procedure involves transplanting hair follicles from one part of the body (usually the back of the head) to areas with thinning or no hair. Turkish clinics offer top-notch services with experienced surgeons, state-of-the-art equipment, and minimal recovery time. Bariatric Surgery With the increasing global prevalence of obesity, bariatric surgeries are highly sought after in Turkey. These procedures help individuals lose significant weight and improve obesity-related health issues, such as diabetes and hypertension. Popular bariatric surgeries in Turkey include:
  • Gastric Bypass: A widely performed surgery where a small pouch is created at the top of the stomach and part of the small intestine is bypassed to limit food intake and nutrient absorption.
  • Gastric Sleeve (Sleeve Gastrectomy): This procedure involves removing a large portion of the stomach, creating a smaller, sleeve-like stomach. It reduces hunger and promotes long-term weight loss.
  • Gastric Banding: This involves placing a band around the stomach to reduce its size and limit the amount of food that can be consumed.
  • Mini Gastric Bypass: A simpler, less invasive version of gastric bypass that has gained popularity for its effectiveness and lower risk of complications.
Dental Procedures Dental tourism in Turkey has grown rapidly, as the country offers high-quality dental procedures at much lower prices than Western countries. Some of the most popular dental treatments in Turkey include:
  • Dental Implants: Turkey is a popular destination for dental implants, which replace missing teeth with long-lasting and natural-looking alternatives.
  • Veneers: Porcelain veneers are widely sought after for transforming smiles. This procedure improves the appearance of teeth by covering imperfections.
  • Teeth Whitening: Many patients visit Turkey for professional teeth whitening to achieve a brighter, more attractive smile.
  • Crowns and Bridges: Crowns and bridges are used to restore damaged or missing teeth, and Turkey is known for offering high-quality options at affordable prices.
