What are the main differences between open and closed rhinoplasty techniques?

When thinking about nose surgery, many patients ask about the difference between open rhinoplasty and closed rhinoplasty. Both procedures can deliver excellent results, but they are done in different ways and each has its own benefits. Knowing how they differ makes it easier for patients to feel confident about their choice. In open rhinoplasty, the surgeon creates a small incision in the strip of skin between the nostrils. This makes it possible to lift the nasal skin and see the bone and cartilage clearly. Because of this direct view, open rhinoplasty is often chosen for more complex situations, such as correcting a very crooked nose, revision surgeries, or when detailed reshaping is necessary. The biggest advantage of open rhinoplasty is the high level of precision it allows. However, recovery may take a little longer, and swelling in the first weeks can be more noticeable compared to the closed method. With closed rhinoplasty, all incisions are placed inside the nostrils, so there are no visible scars. Healing time is usually quicker, and swelling tends to be less compared to the open technique. This method is a good option for patients who only need smaller corrections or want a smoother and faster recovery. The decision between open and closed rhinoplasty depends on the patient’s goals, nose structure, and the complexity of the surgery. Open rhinoplasty is generally preferred for advanced corrections, while closed rhinoplasty is suitable for subtle changes with a shorter healing period. Consulting with an experienced surgeon is the best way to find out which technique is the most suitable for individual needs.
