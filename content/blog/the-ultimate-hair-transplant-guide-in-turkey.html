What is a hair transplant?

Hair transplantation is a painless, surgical technique that removes hair follicles from one part of the body, called the 'donor site', to a bald or balding part of the body known as the 'recipient site'. The surgeon usually moves hair from the back or side of the head to the front or top of the head. Hair loss can be a concern for most people, and hair transplantation surgery is the most common, at the same time is the most effective procedure to handle this problem. Hair transplant is carried out with minimal surgical intervention therefore, who have lost hair because of various diseases or Individuals with scars, can also safely undergo a hair transplant. How is the hair transplantation done?

The principles used in hair transplantation are as follows:
  • Harvesting hair follicles
  • Opening the channels
  • Transplanting hair follicles
Harvesting hair follicles: This is an FUE feature that allows hair follicles to be collected with an extremely high aesthetic success rate. With the FUE method, hair follicles are collected one by one thanks to the fine tips. In this way, follicles can be collected with minimal disrotation. Depending on the length of each follicular unit, it is important to use needles with a certain diameter, or if there are follicular unit grafts, 3-4-5 hair strands and 1.0 mm diameter needles are used. The sharpness of the needles and the single use of each patient are important factors. Another important factor is to gently remove the hair follicles to avoid any follicular damage. All these elements are specially designed for each patient's condition and are planned by the doctor before the surgery. Opening the channels: In the classical FUE method, metal (steel) slots used during the opening of the channels were used. Sapphire blades, which are much smoother and sharper, are used today. By using sapphire tips to open the hair follicles, it is possible to determine the natural hair transplantation angle and direction much more easily. While the direction of the hair is created while opening the channel, the angle of the hair relative to the skin is the factor to be considered during the transplantation phase. With the use of sapphire tips, faster recovery is seen after the channels are opened. Depending on the thickness of the hair follicles, a few different tips are sufficient for each patient. Again, care should be taken to use each tip for one patient only. Transplanting hair follicles: The last stage, which is the transplantation of hair follicles; It is the process of planting the roots one by one at an angle of 40-45 degrees into the incisions created in the directions that ensure correct placement.
