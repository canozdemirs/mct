Are most of the patients satisfied with the rhinoplasty results?

Patients with realistic goals for rhinoplasty are generally very happy with their new nose shape. While you should look and feel great within a week or two, you may experience swelling for at least up to a year after your rhinoplasty. What are the potential risks of rhinoplasty?

Complications from rhinoplasty are rare and if they do occur, are usually minor. These may include infection, nosebleeds, or a reaction to anesthesia. Some patients may experience repeated nosebleeds or tingles in or around the nose. In rare cases, perforation of the nasal septum may occur, but this can be repaired. Rhinoplasty is generally considered a very safe surgical procedure.
