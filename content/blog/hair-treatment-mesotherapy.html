Hair treatment mesotherapy involves injecting a mixture of vitamins, minerals, amino acids, antioxidants, and other active ingredients directly into the mesoderm, the middle layer of the skin. What ingredients are used in mesotherapy?

Patients' requirements will determine the ingredients that will be used during the mesotherapy. However, here are some common ingredients that are often included in mesotherapy formulations for hair treatment:

Vitamins

Essential vitamins like Biotin (Vitamin B7), Panthenol (Vitamin B5), and various forms of Vitamin B complex are frequently used to promote hair health and growth.

Minerals

Minerals such as Zinc and Iron are preferred because they play a role in maintaining scalp health and supporting hair follicle function.

Amino Acids

Amino acids like Arginine and Cysteine are building blocks of proteins essential for hair structure and growth.

Hyaluronic Acid

This hydrating compound helps maintain scalp moisture, contributing to healthier hair.

Peptides

Specific peptides may stimulate collagen production and support hair follicle function.

Plant Extracts

Extracts from plants like Saw Palmetto, Ginseng, and Ginkgo Biloba are often included for their potential to inhibit hair loss and promote hair growth.

Antioxidants

Antioxidants like Vitamin E protect hair follicles from oxidative stress, which can contribute to hair loss.

Coenzymes

Coenzyme Q10 is believed to play a role in cellular energy production, which can benefit hair follicles.

Hormones

Some mesotherapy formulations may include hormones like Minoxidil to stimulate hair growth.

Enzymes

Enzymes might be added to enhance blood circulation and nutrient delivery to the scalp.

Nucleic Acids

Components like DNA and RNA might be included to support cell repair and growth.

Dexpanthenol

Also known as Provitamin B5, it helps improve moisture retention and skin barrier function.

Who can be a candidate for hair treatment mesotherapy?

People experiencing various hair and scalp issues may consider getting hair treatment mesotherapy. Candidacy is determined based on factors such as:

Hair loss type

People going through mild to moderate hair loss or thinning can be candidates for hair treatment mesotherapy. This procedure may not be effective for those experiencing advanced hair loss, as they should be considering other, more intensive treatments.

Underlying cause

It is vital to understand the underlying cause. Mesotherapy is mostly effective for patients with issues like androgenetic alopecia, which is genetic hair loss.

Allergies

Since mesotherapy involves the injection of a solution that includes several solutions, allergies must be discussed with the doctor prior to the procedure.

Scarring alopecia

If the underlying cause of hair loss is scarring, such as injuries, then mesotherapy will not be effective.

What are the advantages of hair treatment mesotherapy?

Hair treatment mesotherapy offers several potential advantages for individuals seeking non-surgical solutions to address hair loss and improve hair health. Here are some of the key advantages:
  • Hair treatment mesotherapy is a non-surgical procedure that involves minimal discomfort. It doesn't require incisions, stitches, or a lengthy recovery period.
  • The mesotherapy solution is injected directly into the scalp's mesoderm layer, precisely targeting the area where hair follicles are located. This direct delivery enhances the effectiveness of the treatment.
  • The solution used in mesotherapy contains a blend of vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and other nutrients that provide essential nourishment to hair follicles. This can promote healthier hair growth and improve hair texture.
  • The injections and the solution's ingredients can improve blood circulation in the scalp. Better blood flow means increased oxygen and nutrient delivery to the hair follicles, which can support hair growth.
  • Mesotherapy treatments can be tailored to an individual's specific needs and conditions. The formulation of the solution can be adjusted based on the type of hair loss and the individual's goals.
  • Most individuals can resume their regular activities shortly after the procedure. There's typically no need for an extended recovery period.
  • While results vary among individuals, many patients report improvements in hair texture, thickness, and a reduction in hair shedding after undergoing a series of mesotherapy sessions.
  • Hair treatment mesotherapy can be used on various hair types and colors, making it a versatile option for many individuals.
  • After the initial treatment sessions, some individuals may have occasional maintenance sessions to prolong the results and continue promoting hair health.

Is there any risks associated with hair treatment mesotherapy?

While hair treatment mesotherapy is generally considered a safe procedure when performed by qualified and experienced medical professionals, there are some potential risks and side effects to be aware of. It's important to thoroughly understand these risks before deciding to undergo the treatment. You can find some potential risks and side effects associated with hair treatment mesotherapy:
  • Some individuals might experience mild pain, discomfort, or a stinging sensation at the injection sites during or after the procedure. However, this is usually temporary.
  • It's common to experience slight swelling or bruising at the injection sites. These effects are usually mild and should subside within a few days.
  • While the risk is low, there is a potential risk of infection at the injection sites. It's important to ensure that a trained professional performs the procedure in a sterile environment.
  • Some individuals might be allergic to certain ingredients in the mesotherapy solution. Allergic reactions can range from mild skin irritation to more severe responses. Providing a comprehensive medical history to your healthcare provider is crucial to minimize this risk.
  • It's possible to experience temporary hair shedding following mesotherapy sessions. This shedding is often a sign that the treatment is stimulating the hair follicles and allowing new, healthier hair to grow.
  • The effectiveness of mesotherapy can vary among individuals, and some might experience more significant improvements than others.
  • Changes in skin pigmentation can occur at the injection sites, leading to temporary darkening (hyperpigmentation) or lightening (hypopigmentation) of the skin.
  • While many individuals experience positive results, mesotherapy might not provide the desired outcomes for everyone. Realistic expectations are important.
To minimize these risks, choosing a reputable and qualified medical professional to perform the procedure is important. How does recovery happen after mesotherapy?

Recovery after hair treatment mesotherapy is generally easy, as the procedure is minimally invasive.
  • After the procedure, the medical professional might provide you with specific aftercare instructions. These instructions could include avoiding washing your hair for a certain period of time (usually within the first 24-48 hours) and refraining from activities that might lead to excessive sweating.
  • It's advisable to protect your scalp from direct sun exposure and wear a hat if you're outside. Sunscreen for the scalp might also be recommended to prevent sunburn.
  • Some individuals might experience mild discomfort, tenderness, or a sensation of tightness in the scalp following the injections. Over-the-counter pain relievers can help alleviate any discomfort.
  • It's normal to experience slight swelling and bruising at the injection sites. These effects should gradually subside within a few days to a week.
  • Most individuals can resume their regular daily activities shortly after the procedure. However, avoiding strenuous exercise or activities that could cause excessive sweating in the first few days is a good idea.
  • Depending on your healthcare provider's recommendations, you might be advised to avoid hair treatments like dyeing, perming, or using harsh chemicals for a certain period after the procedure.
  • Some temporary hair shedding might occur in the weeks following the procedure. This is often a normal part of the hair growth cycle and can be a sign that the treatment stimulates hair follicles.
  • While individual results can vary, you might start noticing hair texture, thickness, and overall health improvements after a few sessions. Follow-up appointments with your medical professional will help track progress and determine whether additional sessions are needed.
