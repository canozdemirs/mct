What is a hip lift and how is it different from a butt lift?

A hip lift is a cosmetic procedure designed to tighten and reshape the outer hip area by removing loose or sagging skin, and in some cases, small amounts of excess fat. It’s especially beneficial for individuals who have experienced major weight loss or who want to enhance the firmness and contour of their lower body. While a butt lift focuses on lifting or adding volume to the buttocks often through fat transfer (BBL) or implants a hip lift targets the sides of the hips. This area can droop or lose definition due to aging, genetics, or significant weight changes. Instead of adding volume, a hip lift works by lifting the tissue and removing excess skin to create a smoother, tighter, and more toned appearance. For those seeking a more complete transformation, the two procedures can also be combined. A butt lift enhances projection and shape at the back, while a hip lift improves the curve and definition along the outer hips. Together, they create a more harmonious, youthful, and balanced lower body silhouette. When performed by a skilled surgeon, a hip lift is an excellent option for anyone looking to improve hip contour rather than buttock volume.

Who is a good candidate for a hip lift in Turkey?

A good candidate for a hip lift in Turkey is someone who struggles with sagging or excess skin around the outer hips—especially when this concern does not improve with diet, exercise, or lifestyle changes. Many patients considering this procedure have experienced significant weight loss, age-related skin laxity, or simply have a genetic tendency to carry loose skin in the hip area.

You may be an ideal candidate for a hip lift in Turkey if:

  • Your outer hips appear droopy, uneven, or lack firmness
  • You are at or close to a stable, healthy weight
  • You do not smoke, or you are willing to quit before and after the procedure

  • You want to refine your body contour rather than solely reduce fat

Patients with good skin elasticity and without major health concerns typically achieve strong, long-lasting results. Emotional readiness is also important—understanding the recovery process and having clear aesthetic goals helps ensure a smooth experience.

During your consultation, your surgeon will assess your body shape, skin condition, and personal expectations to determine whether a hip lift alone, or a combination of procedures, will provide the best outcome. How long does it take to recover from a hip lift surgery?

Recovery from a hip lift surgery typically takes about 4 to 6 weeks, although the exact time may vary depending on the individual and the extent of the procedure. Most patients can return to light daily activities within 10 to 14 days, but full healing takes longer. In the first few days, you’ll likely feel tightness, swelling, and mild to moderate soreness around the hips. You’ll need to wear compression garments to reduce swelling and support healing tissues. Pain is usually manageable with prescribed medication. By week two, many patients feel more mobile, though it’s important to avoid strenuous activity or heavy lifting for at least four weeks. Walking is encouraged to promote circulation, but exercises targeting the hip area should be delayed until your surgeon gives the green light. Most swelling and bruising subside within the first month, and final results begin to appear as the body continues to heal. Scars will fade over time, especially with proper care. Are hip lift results permanent?

Yes, the results of a hip lift are generally long-lasting, especially if you maintain a stable weight and follow a healthy lifestyle. Since the procedure removes excess skin and tightens the surrounding tissue, the improvements in contour and firmness can last for many years. However, while the removed skin and fat won’t return, your body will still age naturally. Over time, some loss of firmness may occur, particularly if there are major weight fluctuations, pregnancies, or hormonal changes. To preserve your results, it’s important to:
  • Maintain a consistent weight through balanced diet and activity
  • Avoid smoking and excessive sun exposure
  • Stay hydrated and follow your surgeon’s skincare recommendations
  • Wear supportive garments during recovery as advised
Most patients are very satisfied with their hip lift results long term, especially when they follow post-op care instructions carefully.  Advancements in surgical technique and aftercare make it easier than ever to enjoy lasting, natural-looking outcomes.

What kind of scars should I expect after a hip lift?

After a hip lift, you can expect a thin, horizontal scar that typically runs along the upper outer hip or lower back area, depending on the surgical approach. The exact placement will be discussed with your surgeon beforehand, and efforts are made to place incisions in discreet locations often hidden by underwear or swimwear. In the first weeks after surgery, the scar will appear red or pink and may feel slightly raised. Over time usually within 6 to 12 months it fades to a flatter, lighter line. Applying scar creams, keeping the area moisturized, and avoiding sun exposure can speed up the fading process.  Many Turkish clinics use refined suturing techniques and laser scar therapy as part of the post-op care plan to minimize visible scarring. While the scar won’t disappear completely, it becomes much less noticeable with time. Patients often find the trade-off worth it, as the improved hip shape and confidence far outweigh the presence of a subtle scar.
