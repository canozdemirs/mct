Do DHT Blockers Have Side Effects?

There are disadvantages as well as benefits to using DHT blockers such as Propecia. Propecia is associated with a few side effects that must be considered before starting lifelong use. Although not very common, Propecia can cause the following side effects:
  • Aversion to sexual desire, libido, or other sexual problems
  • The tendency to develop breast tissue in men
  • Testicular discomfort
  • Skin rashes
  • Problems with increased levels of liver enzymes

DHT Blockers and Sexual Issues

The most common side effects of taking a DHT blocker like Propecia are problems with individuals experiencing anorexia, erectile dysfunction or impotence. These problems may occur in 1 out of every 100 men who use Propecia. Thus, while confidence can be restored as a result of renewed hair growth, this trust can be lost again due to the feeling of underperformance in the bedroom. Propecia can also cause a decrease in sperm volume during ejaculation. The overwhelming majority of men find these side effects go away when they stop taking Propecia. However, there are some men who report loss of libido, erectile dysfunction and decreased sperm volume after stopping Propecia. And of course, stopping Propecia intake will mean new hair will be lost and hair loss will continue.

DHT Blockers and Male Breast Tissue

Male breast enlargement, known in the medical literature as gynecomastia, is a less common side effect of taking Propecia. There have also been very rare reports of male breast cancer in men taking the drug.

DHT Blockers and Testicular Disorder

Some users of Propecia have described discomfort in the testicles, including tenderness and swelling, pain when urinating, and pain during intercourse. It was also accompanied by nausea, vomiting, fever, and sometimes blood coming out of the penis. If you have such complaints, consult your doctor.

DHT Blockers and Skin Rashes

Another less common side effect of Propecia is the development of skin rashes on the body and scalp. These can be hives-like red and extremely itchy. Some users also report swelling of the lips.

DHT Blockers and Liver Enzymes

Occasionally, men taking Propecia have been reported to have elevated levels of liver enzymes, which are detected during routine blood tests. This is not usually a cause for clinical concern. However, if your liver enzymes are affected by Propecia and you are worried about this, you should talk to your doctor about all these side effects. It is important to remember that many men from all over the world have successfully taken DHT blockers like Propecia without experiencing any side effects.
