What Is the Difference Between Restorative and Cosmetic Dentistry?

While both restorative and cosmetic dentistry aim to enhance your smile, their primary objectives differ, and understanding these distinctions can help you choose the right treatment for your needs. Turkey provides outstanding care in both areas, offering high-quality treatments at affordable prices. Restorative Dentistry Restorative dentistry focuses on restoring the function and health of your teeth, addressing dental issues caused by decay, injury, or wear. These treatments are often medically necessary and aim to repair, replace, or support damaged or missing teeth. Common procedures include:
  • Fillings for cavities
  • Crowns to protect damaged teeth
  • Bridges or dentures for missing teeth
  • Root canals to save infected teeth
  • Dental implants to replace lost teeth
Cosmetic Dentistry Cosmetic dentistry, on the other hand, centers on improving the appearance of your smile, often with little regard for its function. These elective procedures are designed to enhance the aesthetics of your teeth and gums, even if they are already healthy. Popular cosmetic treatments include:
  • Veneers to cover imperfections
  • Teeth whitening to brighten stained teeth
  • Bonding to fix chips or gaps
  • Smile makeovers to transform the entire smile
Turkey is an ideal destination for both restorative and cosmetic dentistry, offering expert care that enhances both the health and appearance of your smile at competitive prices.

What Are the Best Options for Replacing Missing Teeth?

Replacing missing teeth is essential not only for restoring your smile but also for maintaining oral health and functionality. Whether caused by decay, injury, or aging, there are several options to replace your teeth and help you regain confidence. Turkey has become a leading destination for high-quality, affordable dental care, providing world-class treatments at a fraction of the cost compared to other countries. Dental Implants Dental implants are considered the best solution for missing teeth due to their durability, natural look, and functionality. A titanium post is placed in the jawbone to support a crown or bridge, offering a permanent solution that looks and feels like real teeth while preventing bone loss. Turkey provides affordable, top-quality implants with skilled specialists and advanced techniques. Dental Bridges Dental bridges fill the gap created by missing teeth by attaching crowns to the surrounding teeth and using a false tooth to bridge the space. Ideal for patients who may not be candidates for implants, bridges in Turkey are made from high-quality materials and offer a long-lasting, affordable solution. Dentures Dentures are removable solutions for replacing multiple missing teeth or a full set. Whether partial or full, Turkish dental clinics offer custom-made dentures that fit perfectly, providing a comfortable, cost-effective option for those needing to replace several teeth. Dental Veneers Veneers are thin shells placed over the front surface of teeth, ideal for enhancing appearance or covering slightly damaged or missing front teeth. Often used for cosmetic purposes, veneers provide a quick solution to aesthetic concerns and are perfect for those looking to improve their smile. All-on-4 or All-on-6 Implants For patients needing to replace a full arch of teeth, All-on-4 or All-on-6 implants offer an efficient and affordable solution. This technique involves placing 4 or 6 implants to support a full set of teeth, providing a more cost-effective option compared to traditional implants for each tooth.
