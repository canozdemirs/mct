What is Butt Lift?

A butt lift surgery, is a cosmetic procedure designed to improve the appearance of the buttocks. There are generally two types of butt lift procedures:
  • Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL): This procedure involves the transfer of fat from other parts of the body, such as the abdomen, thighs, or hips, to the buttocks. The harvested fat is purified and then injected into specific areas of the buttocks to enhance their shape, volume, and contour.
  • Traditional Butt Lift: Also known as a gluteoplasty, this surgery involves the removal of excess skin and fat from the buttocks, resulting in a tighter, lifted appearance. This procedure is typically recommended for individuals who have experienced significant weight loss or sagging due to aging.
Both types of butt lift surgeries are intended to reshape and enhance the appearance of the buttocks, giving them a more youthful and aesthetically pleasing contour. It's essential to consult with a qualified plastic surgeon to determine the most suitable procedure based on individual goals and anatomical considerations in Turkey.  

How is the Process Progressing?

The process of a butt lift surgery can vary depending on the specific technique used (Brazilian Butt Lift or traditional butt lift) and the individual's unique anatomy and goals. However, here's a general overview of what the process may entail: Consultation: The journey typically begins with a consultation with a board-certified plastic surgeon. During this consultation, the surgeon will evaluate your medical history, discuss your goals and expectations, and assess your suitability for the procedure. They will also explain the different options available and help you decide which approach is best for you. Pre-operative preparation: Once you've decided to proceed with the surgery, your surgeon will provide you with pre-operative instructions. These may include guidelines on medication, diet, smoking cessation (if applicable), and other preparations to optimize your health and ensure a successful outcome. Anesthesia: On the day of the surgery, you'll be taken to the operating room, where you'll be given anesthesia. The type of anesthesia used (general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation) will depend on the specific procedure and your surgeon's preference. Your surgeon and anesthesia team will ensure you are comfortable and safe throughout the surgery. Surgery: The surgical technique will depend on whether you're undergoing a Brazilian Butt Lift or a traditional butt lift: Brazilian Butt Lift: For a BBL, the surgeon will begin by performing liposuction to harvest fat from donor areas such as the abdomen, thighs, or hips. The harvested fat is then processed and purified. Once prepared, the fat is strategically injected into the buttocks to enhance volume and shape. Traditional Butt Lift: In a traditional butt lift, the surgeon will make incisions in the buttock area to remove excess skin and fat. The remaining skin is then lifted and tightened to achieve a more lifted and contoured appearance. In some cases, the surgeon may also use liposuction to further refine the shape of the buttocks. Closure: After the desired changes have been made, the incisions are carefully closed with sutures. Your surgeon may also apply dressings or compression garments to support the healing process and minimize swelling. Recovery: Following the surgery, you'll be taken to a recovery area where you'll be monitored as you wake up from anesthesia. You may experience some discomfort, swelling, and bruising in the days and weeks following the surgery. Your surgeon will provide you with post-operative instructions on how to care for yourself, manage any discomfort, and gradually resume normal activities. Follow-up: It's essential to attend follow-up appointments with your surgeon to monitor your progress, address any concerns, and ensure optimal healing and results. Throughout the process, it's crucial to choose a skilled and experienced plastic surgeon who can customize the procedure to your individual needs and goals while prioritizing your safety and well-being in Turkey.

What to Expect After Butt Lift in Turkey?

After undergoing a butt lift surgery in Turkey, it's essential to be prepared for the recovery process. Here's what you can generally expect:
  • Immediate Post-Operative Period: Immediately after the surgery, you will likely experience some discomfort, swelling, bruising, and possibly numbness in the treated area. You may also have drains placed to help remove excess fluid and aid in healing. Your surgeon may provide pain medication and antibiotics to manage pain and prevent infection.
  • Recovery Facility or Hotel Stay: Depending on your specific situation and the recommendations of your surgeon, you may need to stay in a recovery facility or hotel for a few days following the surgery. This allows you to receive proper care and monitoring during the initial stages of recovery.
  • Compression Garments: You will likely be instructed to wear compression garments to help reduce swelling, support the surgical area, and promote healing. These garments may need to be worn continuously for a certain period and then gradually tapered off as instructed by your surgeon.
  • Restricted Activities: Your surgeon will provide specific instructions regarding physical activity and movement restrictions during the recovery period. Typically, you'll need to avoid strenuous activities, heavy lifting, and sitting directly on your buttocks for a certain period to allow the treated area to heal properly.
  • Follow-Up Appointments: You'll be scheduled for follow-up appointments with your surgeon to monitor your progress, remove any sutures or drains, and assess the healing of the surgical site. These appointments are essential for ensuring that you're healing properly and for addressing any concerns or complications that may arise.
  • Swelling and Bruising: Swelling and bruising are common after butt lift surgery and may take several weeks to subside fully. Your surgeon may recommend certain techniques, such as gentle massage or lymphatic drainage, to help reduce swelling and promote healing.
  • Final Results: It's important to understand that it may take several weeks to months to see the final results of your butt lift surgery. During this time, your body will continue to heal, and the swelling will gradually decrease, revealing the improved contour and appearance of your buttocks.
Throughout the recovery process, it's crucial to follow your surgeon's instructions carefully, attend all scheduled follow-up appointments, and contact your surgeon immediately if you experience any concerning symptoms or complications.
