What are the most popular plastic surgery operations in Turkey?

Rhinoplasty

Rhinoplasty is the most frequently performed plastic surgery treatment in Turkey. Many people seek medical assistance to enhance the appearance of their nose because it is the dominant feature of the face and dictates how it expresses itself. The surgical reshaping of the nose, sometimes referred to as a "nose job," is called rhinoplasty. The structure of the nose is primarily composed of bone at the top and cartilage at the bottom. Rhinoplasty can be done to change the nose's appearance, enhance breathing, or both. Discuss the benefits of rhinoplasty with your surgeon to see if it is the right procedure for you. Your surgeon will take into account your other facial characteristics, the skin on your nose, and the modifications you want to have done while planning a rhinoplasty. Your surgeon will create a unique plan particularly for you if you are a candidate for surgery.  

Hair Transplantation

In Turkey, hair transplantation is a very common form of plastic surgery. In this procedure, hair follicles are surgically taken from one area of the body, known as the "donor site," and then transplanted to another portion of the body that is bald or thinning out, known as the "receiver site." The method is mostly used to treat male pattern baldness. Patients who have hair transplants have their hair restored permanently, just as if they had never lost it.  

Liposuction

Another common procedure for plastic surgery in Turkey is liposuction. The biggest benefit of liposuction surgery is that it has nothing to do with weight loss, which is difficult to accomplish. By taking on the task of body contouring on its own, liposuction becomes more appealing. It entails getting rid of little pockets of fat that are challenging to remove with exercise and a nutritious diet. It is performed on body parts like the buttocks, hips, thighs, and stomach where fat tends to accumulate. The objective is to change the way your body looks, and if you keep a healthy weight, benefits are usually long-lasting. It functions best on people with tight skin and a healthy weight.

Breast Augmentation

Another operation that is frequently performed in Turkey is breast augmentation. A "boob job" is another term for it.Surgery for breast implants is performed under general anesthesia. The process involves creating an incision in the skin near or below the breast (as determined during your consultation), putting the implant behind your chest muscle or between your breast tissue and the implant, closing the wound with sutures, and dressing it. Breast augmentation operations are becoming more and more popular as surgeons deliver results that are incredibly natural-looking.  

Breast Reduction

One of the most popular plastic surgery procedures in Turkey is breast reduction. Breast reduction surgery, commonly referred to as reduction mammaplasty, involves the removal of skin, breast tissue, and fat. Particularly mothers with larger breasts and recurrent pregnancies choose this surgery. If you're thinking about having breast reduction surgery, speak with a board-certified plastic surgeon. Understanding the risks and potential issues related to breast reduction surgery is crucial. It is also essential to understand what the method can and cannot achieve.  

Eyelid Aesthetics

Eyelid aesthetics, also known as blepharoplasty, is the procedure used most frequently in Turkey for facial plastic surgery. The name "blepharoplasty" refers to a surgical treatment performed to enhance the look of the eyelids in the medical field. Bagging or sagging of the eyes and eyelids gives the individual an older appearance, which immediately changes the facial expression. In addition to eyelid sagging, eyelid aesthetics also involves the removal of excess skin, muscle, and fat from the lower or upper eyelids.

What are the risks of plastic surgery?

Surgical operations are risky in general. Procedures in plastic and reconstructive surgery are frequently complicated. They might change delicate tissue or sensitive structures close to key organs. Areas of your body that are generally healthy can undergo dramatic changes as a result of cosmetic operations. Complications are possible depending on a number of variables such as the type of operation, your medical history, and lifestyle habits like smoking. While some issues could arise during surgery, others might appear after your recovery. One of the biggest risks is that you could not get the outcome you were hoping for. Additional dangers associated with cosmetic surgery include:
  • Irregular scarring
  • Clots of blood
  • Losing blood
  • Complications from the anesthesia, like breathing problems while you're having surgery
  • Fluid accumulation (edema)
  • Infection
  • Damage to the nerves that causes neuropathy
  • Scarring that restricts motion naturally
  • Incisions and wounds that take long time to heal

What is the recovery procedure like?

You might be allowed to return home once the anaesthetic wears off and you wake up. It usually only takes one or two nights at the hospital if you need to stay there. You'll be given home-care instructions before returning home. Your risk of problems can be decreased by closely adhering to them. Your recovery at home could involve:
  • A lot of rest, no heavy lifting, and little time spent standing.
  • Surgery dressings should be changed often.
  • Eating only soft foods or adapting a certain diet.
  • You can minimize swelling by elevating the place where you underwent surgery.
  • Using stool softeners to treat constipation brought on by the analgesic drugs.
  • Putting off taking a bath or a shower for a few days.
You might feel well enough to resume your normal activities in a few days, depending on the surgery. Recovery from other surgeries, such as a stomach tuck, might take many weeks or months. Within a few weeks, some patients experience the full advantages of their operation and are pain-free. It can take up to a year for swelling to go down after complex treatments like facial feminization surgery. Additional therapies could occasionally be necessary for your recovery. You can regain range of motion or reduce stiffness with physical therapy. To overcome difficulties speaking and swallowing, you can get speech and swallowing therapy. You can relearn how to conduct daily tasks like getting dressed with the aid of occupational therapy.

Your insurance plan's specifics will decide whether or not cosmetic surgery is covered. Most insurance companies categorize plastic surgery procedures as cosmetic, reconstructive, or medically required to preserve quality of life or bodily function. Unlike reconstructive or medically necessary plastic surgery, cosmetic plastic surgery is often not covered by insurance.
