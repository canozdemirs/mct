What types of medical tests and screenings are included in a check-up in Turkey?

In Turkey, medical check-ups are thorough and tailored to meet the needs of each patient. Leading hospitals, such as those affiliated with Medical Center Turkey, offer a range of tests and screenings designed to provide a complete picture of your overall health. Here’s an overview of the types of medical tests commonly included in a general check-up in Turkey: Blood Tests Blood tests are a crucial part of any medical check-up, providing valuable insights into various health conditions. Common tests include:
  • Complete Blood Count (CBC) – To check for anemia, infections, and other conditions.
  • Liver Function Tests – To assess the health of the liver.
  • Kidney Function Tests – To monitor kidney health.
  • Cholesterol and Lipid Profile – To evaluate heart disease risk.
  • Blood Sugar Level – To detect diabetes or prediabetes.
  • Thyroid Function Test – To monitor thyroid health.
Imaging Tests Imaging tests are used to check for any internal issues that may not be visible through physical exams. These may include:
  • X-rays – To detect bone fractures, infections, or lung conditions.
  • Ultrasound – To examine organs such as the liver, kidneys, and reproductive organs.
  • ECG (Electrocardiogram) – To assess heart health and detect arrhythmias.
  • CT Scans or MRIs – For more in-depth analysis, especially in cases of suspected neurological or cardiovascular issues.
Cardiovascular Screenings Given the importance of heart health, check-ups in Turkey often include tests to assess the heart and circulatory system:
  • Blood Pressure Measurement – To check for high blood pressure (hypertension).
  • Electrocardiogram (ECG) – For heart rhythm and electrical activity.
  • Stress Test – To evaluate how the heart performs under physical stress.
Cancer Screenings Cancer screenings are included in many comprehensive check-up packages, especially for individuals over 40:
  • Mammography (for women) – To detect breast cancer.
  • Pap Smear (for women) – To screen for cervical cancer.
  • Prostate-Specific Antigen (PSA) Test (for men) – To check for prostate cancer.
  • Colonoscopy – To screen for colorectal cancer.
General Health Screenings Routine screenings to assess your overall health may include:
  • Body Mass Index (BMI) – To determine if you are overweight or obese.
  • Vision and Hearing Tests – To check for issues with sight or hearing.
  • Skin Check – To examine for any suspicious moles or skin conditions.
  • Bone Density Test – For detecting osteoporosis, especially for postmenopausal women.
Respiratory Health Tests For a comprehensive view of lung and respiratory health, your check-up may include:
  • Spirometry – To test lung function and detect conditions like asthma or COPD.
  • Chest X-ray – To detect lung infections, tumors, or other issues.
 Specialized Tests Depending on your age, gender, or medical history, specialized tests may also be included in your check-up, such as:
  • Hormonal Tests – To check hormone levels, especially for women in menopause or men with symptoms of low testosterone.
  • Genetic Testing – To assess the risk of hereditary conditions or predispositions.
  • Allergy Testing – If you have a history of allergic reactions.
