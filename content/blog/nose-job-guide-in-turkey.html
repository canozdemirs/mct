Types of Rhinoplasty Procedures in Turkey

  • Closed Rhinoplasty
Involves making incisions inside the nostrils, leaving no visible scars. Best for smaller changes (e.g., reducing the size of the nose, reshaping the tip).
  • Open Rhinoplasty
Incisions are made across the columella (the tissue between the nostrils), allowing for better access to the nasal structures. Often used for more complex procedures or when more significant changes are needed.
  • Non-Surgical Rhinoplasty
This is a non-invasive procedure where dermal fillers are used to reshape the nose temporarily. It’s suitable for minor adjustments (e.g., smoothing out a bump or reshaping the tip).
  • Revision Rhinoplasty
A secondary procedure to correct or refine a previous rhinoplasty. It is often more complex and requires a skilled surgeon to manage the scar tissue and previous work done.
