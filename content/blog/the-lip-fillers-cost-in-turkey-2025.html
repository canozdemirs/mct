What is lip filler?

Lip filler is a cosmetic procedure that involves injecting a dermal filler substance, typically made of hyaluronic acid, into the lips to enhance their shape, volume, and symmetry. Hyaluronic acid is a natural substance found in the body, which makes it a popular choice because it’s less likely to cause adverse reactions. The procedure is minimally invasive and is usually performed by dermatologists or cosmetic surgeons. Lip fillers can be used to:
  • Plump up thin lips.
  • Correct asymmetry or enhance the natural lip line.
  • Add definition to the lip contours.
Results are usually temporary, lasting anywhere from 6 to 12 months, depending on the type of filler used and individual factors like metabolism. Touch-ups are often needed to maintain the desired look. Side effects are usually mild and can include swelling, bruising, and tenderness at the injection site.

Who can have lip filler?

Lip fillers are generally safe for most healthy adults, but certain criteria are considered to determine if someone is a good candidate for the procedure. You may be a suitable candidate if: Suitable Candidates:
  • Age: Most practitioners require patients to be at least 18 years old, though some clinics may have stricter age policies.
  • Good Health: You should be in good overall health without any major chronic conditions that may interfere with healing.
  • Non-smoker: Non-smokers tend to have better outcomes, as smoking can impair healing and increase the risk of complications.
  • Realistic Expectations: Candidates should have a clear and realistic idea of what lip fillers can and cannot achieve.
  • Desire for Enhancement: You want fuller lips or a change in lip shape, either due to genetics, age-related volume loss, or aesthetic preference.
Who Should Avoid Lip Fillers:
  • Pregnant or Breastfeeding: It's generally advised to avoid lip fillers during pregnancy or breastfeeding, as the safety of the procedure in these circumstances hasn't been thoroughly studied.
  • Allergic Reactions: People with a known allergy to any ingredients in the filler (like hyaluronic acid) should avoid the procedure.
  • Chronic Health Issues: Individuals with autoimmune diseases, bleeding disorders, or infections near the treatment area may not be suitable.
  • Active Skin Conditions: Anyone with active cold sores, rashes, or other skin conditions around the lips should wait until the issue has resolved.
It’s important to consult with a qualified healthcare professional to discuss your medical history and ensure the procedure is safe for you. What to Expect After Lip Filler in Turkey?

After getting lip fillers in Turkey, here's what you can generally expect: Immediate Post-Procedure Effects:
  • Swelling and Bruising: It’s common to experience swelling, bruising, or redness at the injection sites for a few days after the procedure. This is a normal reaction and typically subsides within a week​
  • Tenderness or Pain: Some mild discomfort or sensitivity in the lips may occur, but it’s usually manageable and fades over time.
  • Lumps or Bumps: Small lumps or irregularities might be felt initially, but they often settle as the filler integrates into the tissue.
Healing and Recovery:
  • Healing Time: Most people will experience noticeable swelling for 1-2 days, with full recovery typically within 1-2 weeks. The lips may feel firmer at first, but they will soften over time​
  • Results Visibility: The final results will become visible once the swelling subsides, usually within a week. Expect fuller lips with more definition, depending on the amount and type of filler used.
Aftercare Instructions:
  • Avoid Pressure on Lips: For the first 24-48 hours, avoid touching or massaging your lips to prevent disturbing the filler. Sleeping with your head elevated can also help reduce swelling​
  • Avoid Exercise and Heat: Strenuous exercise, saunas, and sun exposure should be avoided for at least 48 hours, as these activities can increase swelling and bruising.
  • Stay Hydrated: Drinking plenty of water will help maintain hydration and support healing​
  • Cold Compresses: Applying ice packs or cold compresses can help minimize swelling and discomfort in the first few days​
Potential Side Effects:
  • Common Side Effects: Temporary swelling, bruising, or redness is expected and generally resolves within a week.
  • Rare Complications: Though uncommon, there is a small risk of infection, allergic reaction, or unevenness in the lip filler results, which can be corrected by the practitioner.
Touch-Up Appointments: In some cases, a touch-up session may be needed within a few weeks if the patient wants additional volume or minor corrections​ In Turkey, clinics catering to international patients often provide comprehensive aftercare advice and may offer check-ups as part of their package.
