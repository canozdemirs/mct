· PRP

PRP is a natural component of your blood that's rich in growth factors. During a PRP treatment, a small amount of your blood is drawn and then processed to extract the concentrated PRP.PRP is injected into the scalp, specifically in areas where hair growth is desired. The growth factors in PRP stimulate the hair follicles, promoting hair growth and improving the overall quality of existing hair.

· Blood Test

Blood tests are a standard part of the pre-operative evaluation for hair transplant surgery. These tests help ensure that you are in good overall health and can safely undergo the procedure.

· Medication, Foam

To promote hair growth, prevent hair loss, and support the healing process. Painkillers and antibiotics are commonly prescribed medications for individuals who undergo hair transplant procedures to manage pain and prevent infections.
  • Painkillers, also known as analgesics, are prescribed to manage pain and discomfort following a hair transplant surgery.These medications help reduce pain by blocking pain signals in the nervous system or by reducing inflammation.
  • Antibiotics are prescribed to prevent infection after a hair transplant surgery. Antibiotics are medications that kill or inhibit the growth of bacteria.
  • Post-Operative Foam: After a hair transplant surgery, a special foam or solution may be recommended by the surgeon to aid in the healing process. This foam is applied to the transplanted area gently to keep it clean and free from infection. It can also help with reducing inflammation and promoting a more comfortable recovery.
