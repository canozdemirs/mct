How is Breast Lift Surgery Decided?

In breast lift surgery, the patient is analyzed on a short-term premise. Nipple location and amount of exocrine gland are measured. The patient's expectations are evaluated. If there's a small sagging, if the quantity of duct gland is at an appropriate level, the breast becomes fuller and upright with the silicone breast prosthesis placed under the duct gland. The fat injection will lift the breast. If the sagging is simply too much and therefore the amount of exocrine gland is sufficient, it's recovered the same as breast reduction techniques. In other words, excess breast skin is removed. If necessary, excess fat is additionally removed.

Who is Breast Lift Surgery Performed for?

Breast lift surgery, also known as mastopexy, is performed for individuals who wish to improve the shape and position of their breasts. This procedure is suitable for a variety of candidates, including:

Common Candidates for Breast Lift Surgery

Women with Sagging Breasts:
  • Aging: Natural aging can lead to a loss of skin elasticity and breast sagging.
  • Pregnancy and Breastfeeding: The breasts can stretch and lose shape after pregnancy and breastfeeding.
  • Weight Fluctuations: Significant weight loss or gain can cause the breasts to lose firmness and shape.
Women with Ptosis (Breast Drooping):
  • Mild Ptosis: The nipple is at the level of the breast fold.
  • Moderate Ptosis: The nipple is below the breast fold but still above the lower contour of the breast.
  • Severe Ptosis: The nipple is significantly below the breast fold and points downward.
Women with Asymmetrical Breasts: Breast lift surgery can correct asymmetries where one breast is positioned lower than the other. Women with Enlarged Areolas: The procedure can reduce the size of the areolas if they have become enlarged over time. Women Who Desire an Improved Breast Contour: Candidates looking for a more youthful and aesthetically pleasing breast shape without increasing breast size.

What Awaits Us After Breast Lift Surgery?

After breast lift surgery (mastopexy), patients can expect a series of recovery stages and outcomes. Here’s a detailed overview of what to anticipate:

Immediate Post-Operative Period

  • Recovery Room:
Observation: Immediately after surgery, you’ll be monitored in a recovery room. Vital signs will be checked, and you'll be observed until you are fully awake and stable. Dressings and Bandages: Your breasts will be covered with dressings and possibly a surgical bra to support healing.
  • Discomfort and Pain Management:
Pain: Expect some discomfort and pain, which can be managed with prescribed pain medications. Swelling and Bruising: Moderate swelling and bruising around the breasts are common.

First Few Days

  • Rest and Limited Activity:
Rest: Rest as much as possible to aid the healing process. Movement: Avoid strenuous activities, heavy lifting, and bending over. Gentle walking is encouraged to promote circulation.
  • Follow-Up Appointments:
First Follow-Up: A follow-up appointment with your surgeon is usually scheduled within a few days to check your healing and remove or change dressings.

First Week to Two Weeks

  • Pain and Swelling:
Decreasing Pain: Pain and discomfort should gradually decrease. Persistent Swelling: Swelling will persist but should begin to subside.
  • Activity Level:
Light Activities: You can resume light activities and return to work if your job is not physically demanding. Avoid Exercise: Avoid vigorous exercise and activities that involve upper body movement.
  • Wound Care:
Incision Care: Follow your surgeon’s instructions for caring for the incision sites. Keep them clean and dry to prevent infection.

Weeks Three to Six

  • Improved Comfort:
Less Swelling: Swelling should continue to decrease, and bruising will fade. Increased Comfort: You should feel more comfortable and notice an improvement in your breast shape.
  • Activity Level:
Gradual Increase: Gradually increase your activity level but continue to avoid heavy lifting and high-impact exercises. Exercise: Light exercise can typically be resumed, but consult your surgeon before starting any fitness routine.

Months Three to Six

  • Final Results:
Settling of Breasts: Your breasts will settle into their new shape and position. Final results become more apparent. Fading Scars: Scars will begin to fade but may take up to a year or more to fully mature.
  • Return to Normal Activities:
Full Activity: Most patients can return to all normal activities, including exercise, without restrictions. Supportive Bra: Continue to wear a supportive bra to maintain the new breast shape and position.

Long-Term Considerations

  • Scarring:
Appearance: Scars will continue to fade and should become less noticeable over time. They will likely be placed in less visible areas, such as around the areola or under the breast crease. Care: Use recommended scar treatments to aid in scar healing and minimize their appearance.
  • Follow-Up Visits:
Regular Check-Ups: Attend regular follow-up visits with your surgeon to monitor healing and address any concerns.
  • Maintenance:
Stable Weight: Maintain a stable weight to preserve the results of your breast lift. Healthy Lifestyle: Follow a healthy lifestyle, including a balanced diet and regular exercise.

Is Breast Lift Surgery an Expensive Surgery Worldwide?

Breast lift surgery (mastopexy) is considered a relatively advanced cosmetic procedure worldwide, with its overall cost influenced by factors such as location, surgeon expertise, and medical standards. Expectations and conditions can vary significantly across different regions.

North America

United States:
Breast lift surgery is typically performed by board-certified plastic surgeons in accredited facilities. Factors that influence overall costs include the surgeon’s experience, facility standards, anesthesia, and geographic location.

Canada:
Similar to the United States, procedures are carried out in well-regulated medical environments, with variations depending on the city and clinic.

Western Europe

United Kingdom:
Costs vary based on clinic reputation, surgeon expertise, and whether the procedure is performed in London or other regions.

Germany, France:
Differences in medical facility standards, surgeon fees, and healthcare regulations influence overall costs.

Australia

Breast lift surgery is performed in highly regulated medical settings, with overall expenses affected by location, clinic type, and surgeon experience.

Asia

South Korea, Japan:
These countries are known for advanced medical facilities and high aesthetic surgery standards.

Southeast Asia (e.g., Thailand, Malaysia):
The region is popular for medical tourism, offering experienced surgeons and modern clinics.

Latin America

Mexico, Brazil, Colombia:
These countries are well known for cosmetic surgery, combining experienced surgeons with internationally accredited clinics.

Other Factors Influencing Overall Cost

Surgeon’s Experience:
Highly experienced and well-known surgeons may charge higher professional fees.

Type of Facility:
Private hospitals or specialized cosmetic surgery clinics may differ in pricing compared to general hospitals.

Complexity of the Procedure:
Costs may increase if breast lift surgery is combined with other procedures, such as breast augmentation or reduction.

Additional Considerations:
Pre-operative tests, anesthesia, post-operative care, medications, follow-up visits, and special garments can all affect the total cost.
