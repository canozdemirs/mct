Liposuction is not a weight loss method. It is a method of getting the body into the desired shape by removing regional fats. It ensures that the body is dropped at the desired aesthetic dimensions by removing the regional excess fat tissues that disrupt the body lines specific to women and men.

How much time does liposuction surgery take?

Although the liposuction operation varies according to the application area, it usually takes between 1 and 3 hours.

What type of anesthesia is used in liposuction surgery?

If it is to be done in a small area, it is done with local anaesthesia, if it will be done for more than one but also for small areas, it is done under sedation and local anaesthesia, if it will be done in large and many areas, it is done under general anaesthesia.

Based on current worldwide experience, the harms of liposuction are incredibly small. Rare problems which will potentially occur with any surgical treatment include infections, bleeding, skin ulcers and nerve injury. The intumescent technique minimizes these risks.

Patients do not feel pain during this surgery. Patients may have mild tingling pain after the surgery, but these are short-lived and temporary. Therefore, liposuction is not a difficult operation for patients.

Most surgeons recommend waiting at least 3-4 months for liposuction after pregnancy, while others recommend waiting 6-9 months.

Pregnant and lactating women, as well as people with diabetes, heart problems, and lung disease, are not suitable for liposuction.
