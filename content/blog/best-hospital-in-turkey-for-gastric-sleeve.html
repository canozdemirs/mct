What Is Gastric Sleeve?

Sleeve Gastrectomy is a bariatric procedure that involves removing 75-80% of the stomach. This procedure is currently the most widely performed bariatric surgery worldwide. Tube Stomach Surgery provides great benefits, including the following.
  • Overall improved quality of life
  • Loss of approximately 60-70% of excess weight within one year after surgery
  • Alleviation or improvement of obesity-related health conditions such as type II diabetes mellitus, hypertension, sleep apnea, fatty liver disease, joint pain and hyperlipidemia
  • Decreased desire to eat
  • Decreased feeling of hunger

How is Gastric Sleeve Surgery Performed?

This procedure works by several different mechanisms. First, the newly created stomach pocket has a much smaller volume than the normal stomach and helps to significantly reduce the amount of food that can be consumed (and therefore the calories in). The bigger effect, however, is the surgery's impact on gut hormones, which affect a number of factors such as hunger, satiety, and blood sugar control. Small incisions are made in the abdominal wall to insert small trocars. The stomach is examined and the blood vessels on the side of the stomach are separated. A Bougie tube is inserted into the stomach to determine the size of the newly formed small stomach A special tool is used to separate the stomach into two parts The stomach is completely divided into two. The new stomach is formed, which has about 20-25% of the original stomach volume in the form of a tube.

Advantages Of Gastric Sleeve

  • Limits the amount of food the stomach can hold
  • Comparative studies have shown that sleeve gastrectomy surgery results in rapid and significant weight loss at a similar level to Roux-en-Y gastric bypass.
  • No foreign body use (Gastric Clamp) required and no bypass or redirection of food flow (RYGB)
  • Requires a relatively short hospital stay of about 2 days
  • It causes positive changes in intestinal hormones that suppress hunger, reduce appetite and increase satiety.

Disadvantages of Gastric Sleeve Surgery

  • It is an irreversible procedure
  • Has the potential to create long-term vitamin deficiencies
  • It has a higher early complication rate than gastric band surgery
