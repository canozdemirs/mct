What does a full body medical check-up in Turkey typically include?

A full-body medical check-up in Turkey is more than just a routine examination it is a comprehensive, technology driven assessment of your entire health profile. These check-ups typically include a detailed evaluation of all major systems such as cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, endocrine, musculoskeletal, and neurological. The process usually begins with a full blood panel, including tests for cholesterol, liver and kidney function, thyroid hormones, blood sugar levels, and vitamin deficiencies. This is followed by urinalysis, ECG (electrocardiogram), chest X-ray, abdominal ultrasound, and when necessary, advanced screenings like CT scans, MRI, or stress tests, depending on the patient’s age, symptoms, and risk factors.

Additionally, clinics in Turkey offer eye and ENT examinations, dental evaluations, and dermatological checks to provide a complete picture of the patient’s health. For women, packages often include gynecological exams, breast ultrasounds, or mammograms, while men may receive prostate-specific antigen (PSA) tests to assess prostate health. Many medical centers also incorporate nutritional assessments, lifestyle evaluations, and personalized counseling to create a tailored health plan based on the results.

In Turkey, these services are known for being thorough, efficient, and supported by state-of-the-art technology. Clinics prioritize patient comfort and ensure that each test is performed by skilled professionals using advanced diagnostic tools. This proactive approach to health screening allows for early detection of potential issues, offering patients peace of mind and greater control over their well-being. For anyone seeking preventive care in a modern, reliable, and patient focused environment, Turkey’s full body check-up programs provide a smart and comprehensive healthcare solution.
