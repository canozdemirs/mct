What is Otoplasty surgery?

Otoplasty, also known as cosmetic ear surgery, is a procedure designed to change the shape, position, or size of the ears. It is most commonly performed to correct ears that stick out too far from the head (prominent ears) or to reshape ears with congenital deformities or injuries. The surgery can be performed on both children and adults, typically after the ears have fully developed (around age 5 or older). It is a safe and effective way to improve facial harmony and boost self-confidence. Key points:
  • Usually done under local or general anesthesia
  • Minimal scarring, as incisions are usually behind the ear
  • Outpatient procedure with short recovery time
  • Permanent results in most cases

Who can have otoplasty in Turkey?

Otoplasty in Turkey is suitable for a wide range of patients who want to improve the appearance of their ears. You may be a good candidate if you:
  • Are in good general health
  • Are over the age of 5 (when the ears are fully developed)
  • Have prominent, protruding, asymmetrical, or misshapen ear
  • Have realistic expectations about the results
  • Are a non-smoker (or willing to quit temporarily before and after surgery)
This procedure is ideal for both children and adults who feel self-conscious about the appearance of their ears and want a long-term solution.

What techniques are commonly used for otoplasty in Turkey?

In Turkey, highly skilled plastic surgeons use advanced and personalized techniques for otoplasty to ensure natural-looking and long-lasting results. The most commonly used methods include: Incision-Based (Traditional) Otoplasty
  • Involves a small incision behind the ear
  • Cartilage is reshaped, repositioned, or removed to bring the ear closer to the head
  • Stitches are placed to hold the new shape permanently
Suture (Stitch) Technique
  • No cartilage is removed
  • Permanent sutures are used to fold and pin the ear back
  • Minimally invasive and suitable for mild to moderate cases
Cartilage Scoring or Sculpting
  • Cartilage is reshaped by making precise cuts or scoring it from the back
  • Allows more control over the final shape
Ear Reduction or Reconstruction (if needed
  • For patients with large ears (macrotia) or congenital deformities
  • Customized approaches are used for symmetry and balance
Each case is unique. That’s why at Medical Center Turkey, our partner surgeons carefully assess your ear structure and recommend the most suitable technique to achieve the best aesthetic and functional results.

What is the recovery time for otoplasty?

The recovery time for otoplasty is generally short and manageable, especially when post-operative instructions are followed carefully. Most patients experience a smooth healing process with minimal discomfort. Here’s what to expect: First 1–2 Days:
  • Mild pain, swelling, and tightness (easily controlled with medication)
  • A protective bandage or headwrap is worn to support the ears
1 Week:
  • Stitches (if not dissolvable) are usually removed
  • Most patients can return to work, school, or light daily activities
 2–3 Weeks:
  • Swelling and bruising reduce significantly
  • You may be advised to wear a soft headband at night for protection
4–6 Weeks:
  • Full return to physical activities (unless otherwise advised)
  • Final results start becoming more visible
3–6 Months:
  • Ears fully settle into their new shape
  • Scars fade and become barely noticeable
