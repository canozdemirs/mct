Recovery Process and Hair Growth Timeline

Because the medical team had prepared me for this stage, I remained calm. Understanding the natural hair transplant growth cycle made it easier to trust the process. By month three, I noticed the first signs of new hair growth. Thin strands began emerging in the transplanted areas. By month six, visible density improvement was evident.
