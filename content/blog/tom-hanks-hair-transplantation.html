What is Hair Loss?

A healthy scalp has an average of 100 thousand hairs, and it is considered normal for adults to shed 100-150 hairs per day, depending on washing and combing. Hair loss can be caused by hormonal and dietary issues, chemical exposure, genetic predisposition, systemic illnesses, hair growth problems, medicines, psychological stress, and scalp conditions. Hair loss in a healthy person might linger for up to two months. If the three-times-per-year hair loss phase lasts longer than two months, it may be a sign of more serious illnesses and call for medical attention. Hair loss often begins 3–4 months after the trigger and may stop 6–12 months after the trigger is addressed. Each hair has a life period that is broadly split into three. The longest period is the growth phase, during which the hair typically grows 1 cm every month. The hair then enters the resting phase, which lasts for a number of weeks, after finishing this phase. It will then start the hair loss phase after this phase, which typically lasts for two to three weeks. Within 2-4 months after entering the shedding phase, hair loses its attachment to the hair follicle and falls out. By combing, brushing, and washing the hair, this time may be cut short; but, it cannot be prolonged. Even if nothing is done to the hair at the conclusion of this time, the hair will naturally fall off. The hair follicle produces new hair to replace the lost hair. This cycle continues for a lifetime. At any given stage, 85-90% of the hair is in the growth phase, 1-2% is in the resting phase, and 10-15% is in the shedding phase. The logic of accepting shedding up to “100” hairs a day on days without bathing is normal, which comes from the physiology of the hair described above. Knowing these stages is also important in terms of understanding why the treatment takes a long time for hair loss.

What Causes Hair Loss?

Every existing hair strand has a life cycle. Every hair strand lives for 4-6 years before falling out and resting. Losing hair in this way is an extremely natural process and it is considered normal to lose 50-100 strands of hair a day. If more than this number is shed, if the amount of hair that can be touched has increased while taking a bath, or if we get 3-5 strands of hair when we pull the washed and dried hair by hand, this situation should be taken seriously. Hair loss can have many different causes. The important thing is to determine this cause and to make the appropriate treatment.

The reasons for hair loss in men and women are different. Hair loss seen in men is generally male-type hormone-sensitive hair loss that is genetic and seen in more than 50% of the population.
