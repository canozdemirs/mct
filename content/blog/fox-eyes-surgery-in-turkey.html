Fox Eyes Procedure

The Fox Eyes procedure is a cosmetic treatment designed to give the eyes a lifted, almond-shaped, and upward-angled appearance, resembling the eyes of a fox. The procedure has gained popularity due to its association with the "snatched" look, which involves creating a youthful, exotic, and elongated appearance of the eyes. These are Common Methods Used in Fox Eyes Procedure: Thread Lift (Minimally Invasive): Involves the use of dissolvable threads (such as PDO threads) inserted under the skin to lift the outer corners of the eyes. Threads are pulled to create a lifted effect on the brows and eyelids. Results typically last for 6 to 12 months as the threads dissolve and stimulate collagen production. Recovery is quick, with minimal downtime. Surgical Brow Lift (Invasive): Involves a surgical approach to raise the outer ends of the brows and eyelids. Small incisions are made to lift and tighten the skin, resulting in a permanent upward tilt of the eyes. Recovery may take several weeks, and the results are long-lasting. Blepharoplasty (Eyelid Surgery): Involves the removal of excess skin or fat from the upper or lower eyelids. This can help achieve a more open, lifted look around the eyes. The results are permanent, but it requires a longer recovery period than non-surgical methods. Canthoplasty (Lateral Eye Lift): A surgical procedure that reshapes the outer corner (canthus) of the eye. Provides a permanent upward lift and a more almond-shaped eye. Recovery may take up to two weeks, with the results being permanent. Procedure Overview:
  • Consultation: The surgeon will discuss the patient’s goals and determine the most appropriate method based on their facial anatomy.
  • Anesthesia: Minimally invasive procedures like thread lifts can be done under local anesthesia, while surgical options may require general anesthesia.
  • Procedure Time: Thread lifts may take about 30 minutes, while surgical methods can take 1-2 hours.
  • Recovery: Thread lifts typically have a short recovery time (a few days), while surgical methods can involve swelling and bruising for 1-2 weeks.
Results:
  • Non-surgical methods like thread lifts provide temporary results (6 to 12 months).
  • Surgical options like blepharoplasty, brow lifts, or canthoplasty offer more permanent and long-lasting results.

Risks and Side Effects of Fox Eyes Surgery

The Fox Eyes surgery, like any cosmetic procedure, carries risks and potential side effects. While the degree of risk depends on whether the procedure is minimally invasive (like a thread lift) or surgical (such as blepharoplasty or canthoplasty), there are common risks and side effects associated with both types.
  • Swelling and Bruising
  • Asymmetry
  • Scarring
  • Infection
  • Nerve Damage
  • Dry Eyes or Excessive Tearing
  • Drooping EyelidsOver-Correction
  • Vision Impairment
  • Discomfort or Pain

Recovery From Fox Eyes Surgery

Recovery from Fox Eyes surgery varies based on the procedure type—minimally invasive (thread lift) or more invasive (blepharoplasty or canthoplasty).
  • Thread Lift: Recovery takes 2 to 5 days, with minor swelling and bruising around the eyes. Discomfort is usually mild and manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers. Patients can return to normal activities in 1 to 2 days, avoiding strenuous exercise for about a week.
  • Blepharoplasty: Recovery takes 1 to 2 weeks, with more noticeable swelling and bruising, which typically subside in 7 to 10 days. Rest is recommended during the first few days, and light activities can resume after a week. Strenuous activities should be avoided for 2 to 3 weeks.
  • Canthoplasty: Recovery time ranges from 1 to 3 weeks, with significant swelling and bruising lasting up to 2 weeks. Pain and discomfort are managed with prescribed medication, and full recovery takes about 4 to 6 weeks.

General Tips for Recovery from Fox Eyes Surgery

  • Follow Post-Operative Instructions: Adhere strictly to your surgeon’s post-operative care instructions to ensure proper healing.
  • Keep Your Head Elevated: Sleep with your head elevated to reduce swelling.
  • Avoid Smoking and Alcohol: Both can slow the healing process and increase the risk of complications.
  • Be Patient with Results: It may take several weeks for swelling to subside and for the final results to become visible.
  • Avoid Sun Exposure: Protect the treated area from direct sunlight, as it can cause pigmentation changes and affect the healing process.
