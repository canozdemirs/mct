The Best Hair Transplant In Turkey Process

Before hair transplantation, a few tests are performed to see if the person is suitable for treatment. As long as no problems are encountered in the patient's analysis results, the treatment is approved. It is recommended that the person quit smoking, alcohol and blood thinners 1 (one) week before hair transplantation. A controlled examination of the person to be transplanted is carried out in the presence of a specialist doctor. During the examination, it is decided which method will be applied by looking at the hair loss of the patient. After the process to be applied is determined, the drawing process of the person begins. This process is very important. The drawing process will ensure that the transplanted hair looks natural or not. In this regard, it should be worked with experienced and expert people. Then, an estimated graft calculation is made based on the adequacy status of the donor area. After these processes, the patient is ready for the operation. The patient to whom FUE will be applied is shaved before hair transplantation. In the DHI procedure, the operation is started directly without shaving. After putting on the surgical gown, the patient is placed face down and local anesthesia is applied to the donor area.

Collection of Hair Roots

With the help of punch, mini incisions are made from the numbed donor area and the loosened roots are removed with a medical instrument such as tweezers. Hair follicles are taken from the donor area in single, double or triple groups (grafts) and kept in a special solution.

Opening Channels

Before the collected hair follicles are transplanted, micro channels are opened in the bald area for the hair to be transplanted. The process of opening channels is a job that requires expertise in itself. The angle and frequency of the channels is the most important issue in terms of obtaining a natural appearance.

Transfer of Hair Roots

After the area to be transplanted is anesthetized with local anesthesia, the canal is opened with a slit or sapphire tip in the transplant area of ​​the patient to whom FUE will be applied, and then the grafts are transferred. In the DHI method, transplantation is performed with a choi pen (implanter) without opening the canal. Finally, the hair transplantation process is completed by placing the collected hair follicles one by one into the opened micro channels.

Recovery Period

Transplanted hair can grow for 2 weeks. Complete loss of transplanted hair in an average of 1 month is not something to be afraid of. The hair shedding becomes visible in an average of 3 to 4 months, and within 6 months, more than half of it, within 1 year, the hair grows and takes its final form.

When Will I Get Result From Hair Transplant

After the hair transplantation process, all the transplanted hair follicles are shed. This is a completely normal state. Hair follicles shed within 1-2 months begin to grow as of the 3rd month. 6 months after the transplant, 90% of the transplanted hair will come out and start to grow. The removal of the remaining 10% may take up to 12 months, although it varies from person to person. As a result, we can say that the result of hair transplantation is fully received at the end of 12 months. Side Effects Of Hair Transplant Although hair transplantation does not have any long-term side effects, it may have some side effects at the time of the procedure. Findings such as swelling on the scalp, bruising around the eyes, temporary loss of sensation in the transplanted area, crusting in the areas where hair follicles are taken and transplanted, and itching on the scalp can be given as examples of these effects. These effects go away on their own in a short time.
