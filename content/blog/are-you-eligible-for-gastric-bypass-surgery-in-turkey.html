What are the general criteria for gastric bypass surgery?

Gastric bypass surgery is typically recommended for individuals who are severely obese and have struggled to achieve sustainable weight loss through traditional methods like diet and exercise. A key indicator for this procedure is a Body Mass Index (BMI) of 40 or higher. However, for those with a BMI between 35 and 40, surgery may still be considered if obesity-related health issues such as type 2 diabetes, sleep apnea, or hypertension are present.
