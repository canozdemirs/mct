How is Hair Transplantation Applied?

Before hair transplantation is applied, a preliminary interview and examination is performed with the patient. during this interview, the patient's hair analysis and a few blood tests are performed. As a result, the patient is given eligibility for surgery. During hair transplantation, firstly the patient's front hairline is drawn and so some drawings are made showing what number grafts are planted within which region and the way many grafts are taken from which region. After these determinations, anaesthesia is applied to the patient and graft removal is started. Grafts are taken one by one and arranged on a special plane. Then, the grafts that are thought to be insufficient for the transplantation process are eliminated. After this, tiny canals are opened within the area to be planted. These channels are just the proper size to suit the grafts. This also facilitates the attachment of the grafts.
