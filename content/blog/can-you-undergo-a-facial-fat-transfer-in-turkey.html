What is Facial Fat Transfer?

Turkey is a popular country with aesthetic surgeries with high rate of success. Facial fat transfer is a cosmetic procedure for individuals looking to restore volume to their face, smooth out wrinkles, and improve facial contours. Turkey is known for its advanced aesthetic treatments and affordable prices, which has made it a popular destination for medical tourism, including fat transfer surgeries. A facial fat transfer involves harvesting fat from one part of your body (commonly the abdomen, thighs, or flanks) using liposuction. This fat is then purified and carefully injected into areas of the face that have lost volume, such as the cheeks, under the eyes, around the mouth, or the jawline. The procedure helps to:  
  • Add volume to sunken areas.
  • Reduce fine lines and wrinkles.
  • Enhance facial contours for a more youthful appearance.

What to Expect During the Procedure?

If you’re considering facial fat transfer, Turkey is the ideal destination, and countless patients around the world highly recommend it for its exceptional results and world-class care. The process begins with a thorough consultation where you’ll discuss your goals with an expert surgeon and determine if the procedure is right for you. During the surgery, local anesthesia with sedation is commonly used, though general anesthesia may be an option depending on your needs. Fat is then gently harvested from areas with excess fat, such as the abdomen or thighs, using liposuction. This fat is carefully purified to remove impurities, ensuring it is safe and ready for injection. The surgeon will then strategically inject the purified fat into targeted areas like your cheeks, under the eyes, or around the mouth to restore volume and enhance your natural beauty. After the procedure, you can expect some swelling, bruising, and minor discomfort in both the donor and treated areas, but most patients recover within 1-2 weeks. Once the swelling subsides, you’ll begin to see the beautiful, natural-looking results. Deciding to Undergo a Facial Fat Transfer

It is a really important choice for you. Deciding whether to undergo a facial fat transfer depends on your personal aesthetic goals, overall health, and the advice of a qualified surgeon. You need to consider the benefits, risks and your own goals for the surgery. If you want to have proportional, natural, and young looking face ; facial fat transfer is for you. Understand Your Aesthetic Goals
  • Volume Loss: One of the primary reasons people opt for a facial fat transfer is to restore lost volume in areas of the face such as the cheeks, under-eye area, jawline, and temples. If you have noticed sagging or hollows in your face due to aging, weight loss, or genetics, fat grafting can help add youthful fullness.
  • Wrinkle Reduction: Fat transfer can soften wrinkles, lines, and creases, especially around the mouth, chin, and nose, giving you a smoother, rejuvenated look.
  • Non-Surgical Alternative: If you're looking for a more natural alternative to synthetic fillers, facial fat transfer offers a procedure that uses your own body fat for volume restoration.     
  Consider Your Age and Skin Condition
  • Aging: As we age, our face loses fat and skin elasticity, which can result in a hollow, sunken appearance. Fat transfer can help combat these signs of aging, making it a popular choice for individuals in their 30s and older.
  • Skin Quality: Fat transfer can be effective for those with good skin quality. If you have excess skin or significant sagging (e.g., drooping jowls), you may need to consider other procedures like a facelift in combination with fat transfer.
  Evaluate Your Health
  • General Health: You should be in overall good health with no major medical conditions (such as uncontrolled diabetes or cardiovascular problems) that could affect the healing process.
  • Healthy Fat Reserves: Since fat transfer requires harvesting fat from other areas of your body (typically the abdomen, thighs, or flanks), you should have enough fat available for extraction. If you’re too thin, you may not be an ideal candidate for this procedure.
