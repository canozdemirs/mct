What is dental work?

A dental procedure is anything involving work done on your mouth. Dental work can be done for medical or cosmetic purposes. Fillings, pulling out a tooth, dental crowns, surgery for gum disease, alignment of teeth, dental implants, and dental surgery for cancer can be examples of dental work.

Why do dental work?

After eating a meal, bacteria eat away tooth enamel by breaking down sugar on the teeth. This will lead to holes that are known as cavities. The transparent film of bacteria creates plaque that sticks to the teeth. This can be prevented by proper brushing and flossing. If you do not care about your teeth, plaques can lead to tooth decay, bad breath, tooth infections, loss of teeth, or further gum diseases. Getting dental work when there is a problem will lead to good overall health. It is up to the person's wish when it comes to cosmetic dental work. Cosmetic dental work is not related to dental health; it will just improve the physical appearance of the smile. What is teeth whitening?

In teeth whitening, teeth are bleached to reach a lighter look. It will not make them perfectly white, but it can result in whiter teeth than your previous shade. This procedure should only be done by a dental professional. The dentist will examine your teeth and make a proper mouthguard. Then, using your mouthguard at home, a bleaching gel will be applied regularly for a specific period of time that the dentist tells you. Another option is laser whitening which the dentist carries on. During laser whitening, a bleaching product is painted on your teeth. Then it is activated by a light or laser. This procedure will take almost an hour. There are some home kits for whitening, but they may be dangerous because the mouthguard may not fit the person's mouth, so the bleaching gel may leak, causing blisters and sensitivity.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are surgically implanted into the jaw. It can be used to restore the ability to chew or to improve appearance. Dental implants are a kind of support for artificial teeth such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. Dental implants can improve a person's life by replacing a lost tooth due to an injury or disease. What are dental crowns?

A dental crown is a cap that is shaped like a tooth. It can restore decayed, broken, or a weak teeth. It is usually used to cover dental implants and root canal-treated teeth. These crowns can be made from metal, resin, and porcelain. Dental crowns can be used for 5 to 15 years if you care about them properly. There are many types of crowns:

Metal crowns

Metal crowns can include gold, palladium, nickel, and chromium. They rarely break and last the longest. Also, they require a small amount of enamel removal. They are not preferred because of their metallic color, so that they can be used for out-of-sight molars.

All-resin crowns

All-resin crowns are usually less expensive than others. One drawback is that they are fragile and can break easily. They are often used in temporary crowns.

All-ceramic or porcelain crowns

All-ceramic or porcelain crowns can mimic the tooth enamel best than other types. They are preferred if the patient has an allergy to metals. The most popular material for ceramic crowns is zirconium dioxide. Zirconia crowns are highly durable, and they can stand heavy forces.

What are dental veneers?

Dental veneers are made uniquely for each patient. They are shells that fit over the front surfaces of your teeth. They are used to camouflage cracks, chips, stains, and other imperfections. This procedure is one of the most popular dentistry treatments.Veneers are made from high-quality dental materials such as tooth-colored composite or porcelain. Veneers are preferred by people who have:
  •  Broken teeth
  •  Gaps in the smile
  •  Stains that can not be improved by teeth whitening
  •  Small teeth
  •  Misshapen teeth
Veneers can only be done if the patient has no cavities or gum disease. If there are any issues regarding your teeth, they should be fixed before doing veneers. There are different types of veneers:
  • Composite veneers
  • Porcelain veneers
  • No-prep veneers
  • Removable veneers

What are braces?

Dental braces are used to correct many dental issues like crooked, gapped, rotated, or crowded teeth and malocclusion, which is an issue with how your teeth fit together. Not only do children wear braces, but it is also common among adults. Braces can be placed by a dentist too, but most often, it is done by orthodontists. A person is always young for braces. But, the best age for braces is the ages of 9 and 14. At those ages, jaws and facial bones are more flexible due to their ongoing development. Adult braces are also effective, but it may take longer to achieve the desired results. Braces use mild and constant pressure to shift the teeth into their right position over time. There are several types of braces:

Metal braces

Metal braces use stainless steel bands, brackets, and wires to shift the teeth. The dentist will glue one bracket on each tooth. Then, a thin, flexible wire will be placed over the brackets. Tiny elastic bands, known as ligatures, will keep the wire in place. These kinds of braces are visible when you smile. Ligatures can be chosen to be any color you want.

Ceramic braces

Ceramic braces work the same way as metal braces. The main difference is that brackets, wires, and ligatures used are tooth-colored. Thus they will not be visible when you smile. One disadvantage is that they are fragile, so they can break.

Lingual braces

These types of braces are done on the back surface of the teeth. You can choose lingual braces if you do not want others to see your braces.  

What is the difference between veneers and crowns?

Veneers only cover the front of your appearance. On the other hand, a dental crown covers the entire tooth structure. Dental veneers are used for cosmetic purposes to improve the smile, but they are not made to strengthen your teeth. In contrast, dental crowns improve the appearance while restoring damaged teeth.
