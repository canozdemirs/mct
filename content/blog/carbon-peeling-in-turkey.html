What is Carbon Peeling in Turkey?

Carbon peeling is a non-invasive skin rejuvenation procedure performed using a Q-Switched Nd:YAG laser. The treatment works by applying a carbon-based solution to the skin, which is then targeted by the laser to break down the carbon particles. This process helps exfoliate the skin, remove impurities, and stimulate skin renewal.

One of the key components of this treatment is activated carbon, a powerful substance widely used in advanced peeling procedures. Activated carbon is produced by exposing natural charcoal to very high temperatures, transforming it into a fine, dark powder with a porous structure. These tiny pores allow activated carbon to trap toxins, bacteria, and chemicals, making it highly effective at cleansing the skin. Despite being derived from charcoal, activated carbon has a unique structure that differs significantly from regular coal.

By converting activated carbon into a specialized peeling mask, a new generation of skin care treatments has been developed. Thanks to its ability to draw out dirt, excess oil, and bacteria from the skin, carbon peeling in Turkey is considered a highly effective method for deep cleansing and skin smoothing.
