What Are the Different Types of Jaw Surgery Available in Turkey?

Turkey has become one of the most preferred medical tourism destinations in the world, thanks to its success in jaw surgery. This success is directly linked to the country’s modern hospitals, internationally certified expert surgeons, and the use of cutting-edge technology. If you want to learn more about jaw surgery, keep reading to discover the five most common types of jaw procedures performed in Turkey. The five most common types of jaw surgery in Turkey:
  • Orthognathic Surgery (Jaw Realignment Surgery): This procedure corrects bite issues, facial asymmetry, and jaw misalignment, providing both functional and aesthetic improvements.
  • Genioplasty (Chin Surgery): A surgery designed to reshape or reposition the chin to enhance facial harmony.
  • Maxillary (Upper Jaw) Surgery: A procedure that corrects issues such as underdeveloped or overly advanced upper jaws, improving overall facial proportions.
  • Mandibular (Lower Jaw) Surgery: This surgery addresses an oversized or receding lower jaw, optimizing both chewing function and facial aesthetics.
  • TMJ Surgery (Temporomandibular Joint Surgery): Aimed at patients experiencing jaw pain, locking, or clicking sounds, this procedure significantly enhances quality of life.
