Why Do Temples Sag?

The biggest reason for the sagging of the forehead and temple area is the effects of gravity and aging brought by years. Depending on these effects, the deepening lines differ from person to person. There are some reasons for the deformations in the temple area. The most important one of them is gravity. The skin being alive and having a strong elasticity is related to gravity. As age progresses, elasticity decreases and hangs down due to gravity. The skin tissue, which loses its flexible structure over time, cannot resist the strong effect of gravity. No cosmetic product has the structure and feature to stop this sagging and stretching. Besides gravity; The increase in age and the texture of the skin are also two important factors. Unhealthy diets, smoking, stress, excessive use of facial expressions are some of the features that trigger and increase facial wrinkles and sagging. These reasons cause deformation especially in the eye, forehead and temple area.
