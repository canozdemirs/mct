Thing to do after having implants

About an hour after the implant treatment, you can remove the gauze sponges placed in your mouth and eat something. During the first 24 hours after surgery, it is very important that the foods you eat are soft. Likewise, avoid hot food and drink for a few hours after the implant procedure. Also, do not drink with a straw for at least 24 hours. These measures will give your mouth a better chance to heal properly. Post-implant bleeding is possible. Do not change diapers during the first hour unless the bleeding is controlled. The packages can be gently removed after an hour. If active bleeding continues, insert new gauze to pressurize the surgical site for another 30 minutes. The gauze can then be changed as needed (typically every 30 to 45 minutes). It is best to moisten the gauze with tap water and loosely soften it for more comfortable positioning. Swelling on your face is normal after surgery and will go away for 2-3 days after surgery. Swelling can be minimized by using a cold pack, ice pack, or a bag of frozen cloth wrapped in a towel and applied to the cheeks adjacent to the surgical area. This should be applied for twenty minutes during the first 24 hours after surgery. If you have been prescribed medication for swelling control, be sure to use it correctly. Good and correct oral hygiene practices after dental implant surgery are very important as always. You can continue brushing your teeth 24 hours after the implant procedure. Brushes with soft bristles will help you to brush gently. Other than that, rinsing your mouth with a little salt water can also help clean your mouth (1/4 teaspoon of salt per glass of water). You should not use additional products such as mouthwashes for 14 days after the implant.
