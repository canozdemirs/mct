What Procedures Does the Boob Job in Turkey Cover?

Boob job in Turkey; the operations are generally performed in three stages; breast augmentation surgeries, breast reduction surgeries, and breast lift surgeries.

What Are Breast Augmentation (Augmentation Mammoplasty) Surgeries?

Breast augmentation operations are done to give volume to breasts that are disproportionate to the body due to the size difference between the two breasts, sagging and deformations in the breast tissue due to weight gain and loss, breast loss due to previous illness, postpartum, developmental reasons, and shrinking due to age.

Who Can Have Breast Augmentation Surgeries?

To perform breast augmentation operations, women must be over the age of 18. Surgery is not applied to groups of patients younger than 18 years of age, since their body development is not completed.

What Is Breast Prosthesis?

A breast prosthesis is used to imitate breast tissue that has lost its volume or is not at the desired volume in breast augmentation surgeries. Implants or silicones are the common names for the breast prosthesis. A breast prosthesis is a gel consistency which is coated with hard silicone material. These implants, which have various physical properties, are divided into several groups according to size, shape, surface and content.

Where Is the Silicone Prosthesis Placed in Breast Augmentation Surgery?

There are three main areas where the prosthesis can be placed in breast augmentation surgeries. The first of these is to place the prosthesis just behind the breast. The second option is expressed as the "The Submuscular Breast Implant Method". The third option is "The Dual Plane Method", an application that covers both areas.

What Is the Submuscular Breast Implant Method?

The submuscular breast implant method is a breast augmentation operation technique performed by placing the breast prosthesis under a layer of membrane adhered to the muscle on the back of the breast. One of the most important advantages is that the pain of the patients is very less, as in the silicone prosthesis surgery placed behind the breast. Women who are thin and have less breast adipose tissue are more likely to have silicone. Silicone prosthesis placed behind the breast and under the muscle membrane does not cause pain.

What Is the Dual-Plane Method?

The dual-plane method is a breast augmentation operation technique performed by placing the breast prosthesis in such a way that it receives support from both muscle and breast tissue. The dual-plane involves placing the upper portion of the implant under the pectoral muscle, while allowing the lower half of the implant to sit directly under the breast tissue, over the pec muscle. It offers an advantage in achieving successful results, especially for patients who want to achieve a drop shape breast appearance, as it provides breast shaping with support from both areas. It provides a more natural appearance than the submuscular breast implant method. It also has a longer lifespan than the submuscular breast implant method. Pain in the muscle may occur in patients who have undergone breast augmentation with the submuscular dual-plane method.

What Are Breast Prostheses According to Their Shape?

Since it usually gives the desired breast image, its shape is compatible with the breast and its augmentation possibilities are frequently used. What Is Gynecomastia?

It is located under breast reduction operations in the aesthetic area. It is known as a breast reduction operation for men. Fat accumulates around the breast due to hormonal reasons and various diseases that cause gynecomastia.

What Causes Gynecomastia?

Variations in the amount of the hormone testosterone are a common cause of gynecomastia. It can be caused by a decrease in the amount of testosterone hormone or an increase in estrogen level. Natural hormonal changes, drug and alcohol usage, problems from different health issues, and the use of certain herbal treatments and supplements, and some medicines are all causes of gynecomastia.

Who Gets Gynecomastia?

The possibility of gynecomastia is relatively common during adolescence due to constantly changing hormone levels. In most cases of gynecomastia seen in adolescence, swollen breast tissue disappears without treatment. The prevalence of gynecomastia in adults starts to rise again between the ages of 50 and 69. Studies indicate that at least one out of every four men in this age group shows signs of gynecomastia.

What Are the Symptoms of Gynecomastia?

Symptoms of gynecomastia primarily include swollen breast tissue and sometimes breast tenderness. There may be swelling, pain, or tenderness in the chest. In addition, in case of discharge from one or both breasts, the individual should consult a doctor as soon as possible.

How Is Gynecomastia Treated?

There are two different treatment methods. In patients treated with the liposuction method, the fatty tissue is sucked through a 2-3 mm tube under anaesthesia. In mastectomy type surgical interventions, the mammary gland tissue is completely removed by the surgeon. This operation is normally performed with only a few tiny incisions.
