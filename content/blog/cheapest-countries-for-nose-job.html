What is rhinoplasty?

Rhinoplasty is a nose surgery that alters the form of the nose and sometimes can be referred to as a nose job. It may be performed to change the nose's appearance, enhance breathing, or both.  The upper portion of the nose's structure is made of bone. The lower section is cartilage. What are the types of rhinoplasty?

There are two major approaches for rhinoplasty,
  • Open rhinoplasty is a significant nose remodeling treatment. Your surgeon will create incisions to completely remove the skin of your nose from the bone and cartilage, allowing them to see the underlying structure of your nose.
  • Closed rhinoplasty is a simple nose remodeling technique. To reshape your nose, your surgeon will make incisions to detach your skin from the bone and cartilage.
Other types of rhinoplasty include,
  • Cosmetic rhinoplasty: This procedure enhances the appearance of your nose and face. 
  • Nonsurgical rhinoplasty (filler rhinoplasty): A type of cosmetic rhinoplasty in which dermal fillers are used to temporarily correct dips and irregularities in your nose. It can be used to raise a sagging nose tip or to smooth out a minor hump.
  • Functional rhinoplasty: It is applied following sickness, cancer treatment, or traumatic injury. Congenital abnormalities and a deviated septum can also be corrected with this form of reconstructive surgery.
  • Secondary rhinoplasty: Revises or corrects any issues that arise following the initial rhinoplasty operation. These issues can be modest, but they are frequently more difficult for your surgeon to treat.

What is the difference between rhinoplasty and septoplasty?

Rhinoplasty is a nose surgery that changes the form of the nose. It can be performed to improve breathing as well but the main purpose of the surgery is for aesthetic concerns. Septoplasty is a breathing improvement procedure. It straightens the nasal septum, which is a wall inside the nose that divides the nasal airways into right and left sides. When the septum is crooked, it can make breathing through the nose difficult. A septoplasty is frequently performed in conjunction with a rhinoplasty in Turkey.
