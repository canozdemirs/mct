What is cataract?

Cataract Surgery in Turkey is an informative article that describes the cataract treatment process offered in Turkey. Cataract surgery is a procedure performed to remove the natural lens of the eye when it becomes cloudy and to replace it with a clear, artificial intraocular lens. In a healthy eye, the natural lens is transparent, but cataracts cause this lens to become cloudy, leading to blurred or impaired vision.

The procedure is carried out by an ophthalmologist and is typically performed under local anesthesia, allowing patients to go home the same day without the need for an overnight hospital stay. Cataract surgery is one of the most common eye procedures worldwide and is considered highly safe and effective, offering significant improvement in vision and quality of life.

Cataract Surgery in Turkey is an article that explains the reasons of cataract surgery in Turkey. Cataract surgery is performed to treat cataracts. Cataracts can cause blurred vision and increase glare from lights. If cataracts make it difficult for you to do your normal activities, your doctor may recommend cataract surgery. Surgery may also be recommended when cataracts cause other problems in your eye. For example, if cataracts make it difficult for the ophthalmologist to examine the back of the eye to monitor or treat other eye problems, such as age-related macular degeneration or diabetic retinopathy, surgery may be recommended. In most cases, although waiting a little while to have cataract surgery will not harm your eyes, waiting too long can cause permanent damage to the eye. In addition, the operation of cataracts that are delayed and hardened becomes more difficult.

What are the types of lenses used in cataract surgery?

  • Cataract Surgery in Turkey is an article that describes the types of lenses that used in cataract surgery.
  • Fixed-focus monofocal: This type of lens has a single focal power for distance vision. Reading often requires the use of reading glasses.
  • Accommodation monofocal: Although these lenses have only a single focusing power, they can respond to eye muscle movements and shift focus to near or distant objects.
  • Multifocal: These lenses are similar to glasses with bifocal or progressive lenses. Different areas of the lens have different focusing powers, allowing for near, intermediate and distance vision.
  • Astigmatism correction (toric): If you have high astigmatism, a toric lens can help correct your vision.
  • Add-on lens (Complementary lens): These are special lenses designed to correct the refractive errors that remain after the lens is installed after cataract surgery or to get rid of near-sighted people who have been fitted with a single vision lens and cannot see near. With a second surgery, a second lens is attached in front of the existing lens.

How is cataract surgery performed?

Cataract Surgery in Turkey is an article that describes the way of cataract surgery. Generally, cataract surgery takes 10 minutes for one eye when performed by an experienced physician. Both eye operations are not performed on the same day. Usually there is a break of 1 week. Before the operation, drops will be injected into your eyes to dilate your pupil. Local anesthetic drops are instilled to numb the area and a sedative is given to help you relax. With sedation, you will remain awake during surgery but won't feel a thing. During cataract surgery, the old lens that has lost its clarity is removed and a new artificial lens is inserted. However, in some special cases (especially in babies), an artificial lens may not be inserted after the cataractous lens is removed. Lens surgery may be performed again in the future.

Recovery process after cataract surgery

Cataract Surgery in Turkey is an article that describes the process after cataract surgery.
  • After cataract surgery, your vision will become clear within a few days. Your vision may be a little blurry at first until your eyes heal and adjust.
  • Colors may appear brighter after your surgery because you are looking through a new, clear lens. Cataracts are usually yellow or brown in color before surgery, making the appearance of the color pale.
  • You will usually have eye checks the day after your surgery, the following week, and about a month later to monitor healing.
  • It is common to experience itching and slight discomfort for several days following surgery. Avoid rubbing your eyes.
  • At the end of the surgery, your eye will be covered with a bandage. The bandage is not removed until the next morning for control.
  • The use of eye drops and vitamins is recommended for 1 month after the operation to prevent infection, reduce inflammation and control eye pressure.
  • After a few days, most of the discomfort disappears.

What are the results of cataract surgery?

Cataract Surgery in Turkey is an article that describes the results of cataract surgery. Vision is successfully restored in people who have cataract surgery. After a while, people who have had cataract surgery develop a secondary cataract called a posterior capsule. The medical term for this common occurrence is known as posterior capsule opacification (PCO). This happens when the back of the lens capsule — the part of the lens that is not removed during surgery and supports the lens implant — becomes cloudy and impairs your vision. If a multifocal lens (smart lens) is inserted during cataract surgery, people can see near and far comfortably throughout their lives without glasses. Nowadays, in cataract surgeries, we can not only remove the cataract but also correct the refractive errors (eye degrees). Myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism and even near vision can be corrected.
