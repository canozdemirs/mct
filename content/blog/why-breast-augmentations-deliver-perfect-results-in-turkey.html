What are the most popular types of breast implants used in Turkey and why?

When it comes to breast augmentation in Turkey, one of the most important decisions is choosing the right breast implant. Turkish clinics offer a wide range of FDA approved implant types, and each one is selected based on the patient’s goals, body shape, and lifestyle. The most popular choice is silicone breast implants. These implants are known for their natural look and soft feel. Silicone implants hold their shape well and are less likely to wrinkle or ripple under the skin. Many patients choose silicone for a more realistic appearance and long lasting results. Another popular option is round implants. These are ideal for women who want more upper breast fullness and a noticeable lift. Round implants create a fuller, more youthful shape and are often used in cosmetic breast augmentation. Some patients prefer anatomical (teardrop) implants, which are shaped to follow the natural contour of the breast. These implants give a more subtle and natural look, especially for those with smaller frames or minimal breast tissue. Textured implants are also used in some cases to help prevent movement after surgery. However, smooth implants remain a top choice because they move more naturally with the breast tissue. In Turkey, breast implant selection is highly personalized. Surgeons help each patient choose the best implant based on their body and expectations. That’s why breast augmentation in Turkey continues to deliver excellent, custom tailored results.
