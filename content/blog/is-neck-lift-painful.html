What is Neck Lift Surgery?

Neck lift surgery, also called a lower rhytidectomy, is a cosmetic procedure designed to improve the appearance of the neck by removing excess skin and fat, tightening underlying muscles, and smoothing wrinkles. It effectively addresses concerns such as sagging skin, a double chin, and visible neck bands, resulting in a more youthful and defined neck and jawline.

The procedure typically involves small incisions behind the ears and sometimes under the chin, allowing the surgeon to lift, tighten, and reposition tissues for optimal contour. Recovery generally takes a few weeks, during which swelling and mild discomfort are normal. The results are long-lasting and can significantly enhance the overall facial profile.

Who is Candidate for Neck Lift Surgery in Turkey?

A candidate for neck lift surgery in Turkey, or anywhere else, generally includes individuals who:
  • Are in Good Health: Ideal candidates should be in good overall health, with no underlying medical conditions that could affect healing or increase surgical risks.
  • Have Realistic Expectations: They should have a clear understanding of what the surgery can achieve and realistic expectations about the results.

  • Desire Improvement in Appearance: They should be seeking cosmetic improvement and not a complete transformation.

How is Neck Lift Surgery Performed?

Neck lift surgery, also known as cervicoplasty or platysmaplasty, is performed to improve the appearance of the neck by removing excess skin and fat and tightening the underlying muscles. Here’s a general overview of the procedure:
  • Consultation and Planning: Before the surgery, you’ll have a consultation with a plastic surgeon to discuss your goals, review your medical history, and plan the surgery. The surgeon will assess your neck and determine the best approach for your needs.
  • Anesthesia: The surgery is usually performed under general anesthesia, but in some cases, local anesthesia with sedation may be used.
  • Incisions: The surgeon makes incisions either under the chin or behind the ears, depending on the extent of the lift needed. These incisions allow access to the underlying tissues.
  • Fat Removal and Skin Tightening: The surgeon may remove excess fat through liposuction or other techniques. The underlying muscles, such as the platysma muscle, may be tightened to improve the neck’s contour. Excess skin is then trimmed away.
  • Muscle Repair: In cases where there is significant muscle banding, the surgeon will stitch the platysma muscles together to create a smoother, tighter appearance.
  • Closing the Incisions: The incisions are closed with sutures or skin adhesives. The surgeon will take care to place the incisions in areas that are less visible, like under the chin or behind the ears.
  • Recovery: After the surgery, you’ll need to follow post-operative care instructions. This typically includes wearing a compression garment to reduce swelling and promote healing. You may also need to avoid strenuous activities and follow a specific skincare regimen.
  • Follow-up Visits: You’ll have follow-up appointments with your surgeon to monitor your recovery, remove any stitches, and address any concerns.
Recovery time varies, but many patients can return to work and normal activities within a few weeks, although some swelling and bruising may persist for a bit longer.

Is A Neck lift Painful?

Neck lift surgery itself is usually not painful due to anesthesia, but there may be some discomfort and soreness during the recovery period. Here’s what you might expect in terms of pain and discomfort:
  • During Surgery: General anesthesia or sedation is used during the procedure, so you won't feel any pain while the surgery is being performed.
  • Post-Operative Discomfort: After the surgery, you might experience some pain, soreness, and swelling in the neck area. This is normal and can be managed with prescribed pain medications or over-the-counter pain relievers as recommended by your surgeon.
  • Swelling and Bruising: Swelling and bruising are common after a neck lift and can contribute to discomfort. These symptoms typically subside over a few weeks.
  • Tenderness: The treated areas might be tender to the touch. This tenderness generally decreases as the healing progresses.
  • Recovery Period: Most people find that the discomfort is manageable and diminishes significantly within the first few days to weeks after the surgery. Following your surgeon’s post-operative care instructions, such as wearing a compression garment and keeping your head elevated, can help ease discomfort and promote healing.
If you have concerns about pain or discomfort, it's important to discuss them with your surgeon before the procedure. They can provide detailed information on what to expect and how to manage any discomfort during recovery.
