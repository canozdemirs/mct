List of Required Documentation For A Medical Visa in Turkey

The following is a list of important documents you will need to apply for a medical tourism visa: The applicant must have their original passport, which must be valid for at least six months from the date of departure. A minimum of two blank pages must be available for visa stamps. The applicant must include copies of their old passports, which must be filled out and signed by the applicant, and demonstrate their previous travel details. Two current passport-size photographs with a matt or semi-matt finish, 80% face coverage, white backdrop, and no borderlines must be carried by the applicant. The photograph must be 2.5″x 2.5″ in size and should not be utilized in any way before the visa application. The applicant’s cover letter, which includes the applicant’s name, passport information, and travel details, as well as the objective for the trip and duration of stay. Hotel confirmation in accordance with the travel plan, as well as a confirmed return air ticket that reflects the travel itinerary. Travel insurance is required for anybody visiting Turkey.
