What is Liposuction?

Liposuction is the process of removing locally accumulated fat with tubes called cannulas with the help of negative pressure. It is ensured that the regional excesses that cannot be removed despite the sports and diet are absorbed and removed from the body. Liposuction is plastic surgery. Liposuction is also known as 'Liposculpture'. It is usually performed on the hips, belly, thighs, buttocks or face to correct shape by a specialist surgeon. Liposuction can also be performed in conjunction with other aesthetic surgeries such as facelifts and breast lifts. The operation is performed with 4 main methods;
  • Classical Liposuction
  • Laser Liposuction
  • Ultrasound Liposuction (VASER)
  • Radiofrequency Liposuction
The difference between laser liposuction from classical liposuction is that the fat detected in the area is first removed after it is broken down by the laser. In Vaser liposuction, these fats are broken down by using ultrasonic sound waves and then removed from the body. Since the lipids are removed after they become liquid, fluctuations that can be seen in the classical liposuction method are not encountered. Since the sound waves used in the Vaser liposuction method only target fats, the possibility of damaging the important tissues around it decreases and the amounts of pain, bruising and swelling are minimized. In radiofrequency liposuction, these procedures are performed by melting the fat by radiofrequency waves. The difference from other methods is that it is not only a fat removal operation but also helps to tighten the skin.

What is Liposuction Classical? What is the procedure for classical liposuction?

In the classical liposuction application, incisions between 3 and 5 millimetres are opened. Thus, a solution is injected into the adipose tissue through the opened incision. Fats break down and take the form of liquid. Then, the liquid lipids are taken by vacuum. Incision scars are cut from invisible areas.

What is liposuction by laser? How does laser liposuction work?

Laser liposuction is a procedure for removing fat deposits that do not respond to diet or exercise and give the body an unattractive appearance. It differs from traditional liposuction techniques. The fat tissue is initially melted and liquefied during this procedure, which is done with the use of a laser. The liquefied fat deposits are removed from the body with thin, metal pipes to be used in the vacuum system. It then tightens the body. On average, this operation takes 1 to 1.5 hours. What is VASER Liposuction and How Is It Applied?

Vaser (vibration amplification of sound energy at resonance) technology, which is one of the most modern liposuction methods today, is a method based on the breakdown of fat cells with ultrasonic sound vibrations. Fat tissue in the areas to be liposuction is melted with very thin metal cannulas that produce ultrasonic sound waves through small incisions (0.5 cm) made on the skin. Then, the melted fats are taken out of the body by means of thin cannulas.

What are the benefits of the Vaser Liposuction method?

Compared to classical liposuction methods, fat removal is facilitated in this method, as the fat tissue is melted with ultrasonic sound waves. This also shortens the processing time. The VASER technique protects blood arteries, lymph vessels, nerves, and other structures that support the skin by targeting just fat cells during the fat removal procedure. With the absence of vascular damage, the recovery process is faster as there is less pain, bruising and oedema after the procedure. This method, by selectively targeting only the adipose tissue, provides convenience in the application in areas such as the back where the connective tissue is dense and in men's breast area (in the treatment of gynecomastia). Due to the preservation of the vascular and nerve structures, it also provides the opportunity to interfere with the superficial fat cells just under the skin without disturbing the skin circulation, which allows the skin to tighten more during recovery.

Is liposuction a weight loss procedure? How much fat can be taken?

Liposuction is not a weight-loss procedure, despite common assumptions. Instead, it is a fat reduction procedure. The amount of fat that can be removed in an area during a single procedure will vary depending on several factors, including the surgeon's assessment of the patient's health and aesthetic goals.

Can anyone have liposuction?

In this article, you will find everything you need to know about liposuction surgery; also, who can be a candidate. Liposuction is not a slimming technique. It is also not a cellulite treatment. It is a minimally invasive surgical technique. So it needs to be a substitute for your overall health. It is recommended that you be in the 30% of your body mass index, that is, close to your ideal weight. It is important to have a firm and flexible skin. You must not smoke. If you have health problems related to blood circulation or if you have heart disease, diabetes or a weak immune system, you should get approval from your specialist doctors before the procedure. Also, tell your surgeon about any allergies you have and any medications you take. It is recommended that you stop taking medications such as blood thinners and some pain relievers a few weeks before the surgery.

What is the recovery process of liposuction surgery?

A hospital stay is not usually required, although this will vary depending on the type of surgery you have. Again, depending on the type of technique used and the characteristics of your skin structure, bruising, swelling and pain may occur. The surgical team offers the patient comforting options with painkillers after the surgery. Resting for a while, slowing down your life and getting into a healthy diet are among the rules that must be followed in order to heal quickly. Your surgeon may want you to wear a corset for a while after surgery to control oedema and your new body shape. You can usually return to work within a few days and do normal activities within 2 weeks.

Will there be any scars after the procedure?

The cannula is a small instrument and therefore scars are also small. The number of scars varies according to the size of the treated area. The scar also varies depending on the skin quality of the patient. With the latest medical technology, surgeons use techniques that will minimize trauma.

What kind of results should the patient expect after surgery?

Plastic surgeons can help you predict real results. Immediately after surgery, some swelling and skin relaxation are normal. Therefore, it may take several weeks to see the full results of the procedure. The fat cells removed during liposuction are permanently removed from the body. However, the body will produce fat cells again after the surgery. For this reason, you should pay attention to your lifestyle and follow your exercise plan after treatment, along with a healthy diet.

Does fat occur in the same areas again after liposuction?

In this article, you will find everything you need to know about liposuction surgery; also, if the fats are coming after the operation or not. The amount of fat cells is constant. These cells expand when we gain weight and contract when we lose weight. Since some of the fat cells are removed from the environment after this procedure, there is no increase in fat in the same areas as long as we pay attention to our weight.

Will the results be permanent?

Fat cells are permanently removed during liposuction. However, new fat cells might grow in other places of your body, causing you to gain weight. Follow a diet high in lean protein, fruits and vegetables, grains and low fat to maintain the new shape after surgery. And you should exercise regularly.
