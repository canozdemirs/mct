Hair Growth Phase After Hair Transplant

Graft Growth

Graft growth takes place in an average of 1 year and a definite result is obtained at the end of 1 year. The following timeline will help you monitor graft growth.

1-6 Days After the Operation:

Depending on the individual, a small amount of swelling in the transplant location may be visible a few days following the procedure. This usually starts on the 3rd day and ends on the 6th day (usually no swelling is observed as a result of the necessary treatments). Exfoliation occurs in the area where your hair follicles are taken within 5-7 days. Depending on the skin type, it may be slightly pinkish in the .7th day treatment areas.

7-10 Days After Operation:

Your hair has grown 1-2 mm longer and your appearance has improved. You can start the second-week washing procedure in your file for the complete disappearance of the crusts.

1-3 Months After the Operation:

After a few weeks, it starts to grow with noticeable itching. It is completely normal to see acne and forehead oiliness. It will take 3 months for this new growth process. Newly growing hair is thin but will thicken after the growth process is completed. Some hairs will also appear short on the roots. These will eventually begin to expand.

5-6 Months After the Operation:

In the 5th and 6th months, most of the hair has completed its growth phase. Some hairs may also be featherier or under the skin. The new hairs are 2-3 cm long and will gradually thicken.

8-10 Months After the Operation:

Between the 8th and 10th months, the patient follows the noticeable development from the operation to this time. Now the hair has grown and thickened. A much better appearance has emerged compared to a few months ago.

1 Year Post-Operation:

Within 1 year after the operation, the patient saw the result more clearly. Although the change of hair will still continue, a noticeable success has been achieved in 1 year. The hair will now continue to grow and develop throughout the patient's life and can be cut and shaped as he wishes. Nothing special is recommended for hair care. Simple shampoos and other hair care items will do.  

What To Do After the Hair Transplantation Surgery?

"How Long Is the Hair Transplant Recovery Time?" is very commonly asked. Some recommendations can be followed to speed up the healing process. Asepsis-antisepsis rules applied for all micro-surgical procedures are also valid for hair transplantation. If the hair transplant operation is performed in a sterile manner and under appropriate conditions, the risk of infection will be very low. Although the recovery process after hair transplantation varies from patient to patient, it covers about 1 year. Following surgery, the first three days are crucial. There may be some pain on the first night, but if you use the painkillers prescribed by your doctor, you can have a comfortable night. In the first days after sowing, you should lie on your back as much as possible and not make any contact with the planted area. The next day after hair transplantation, they will call you to the clinic to remove the bandage on your neck and make the dressing. Afterward, it will be sufficient to use the anti-edema medications given by your doctor for the first three days. During the first 3 days, there may be minor bleeding in the area where the transplanted hair follicles are taken, and in the transplanted area, this is normal. To prevent inflammation, you should use the antibiotic course given by your doctor regularly for the first five days after the operation, without interruption. Using alcohol is not advised. Since the antibiotic will mix directly into your blood, it may interact with the drugs you use and cause negative results. Since smoking will also negatively affect the healing process, in the same way, it would be appropriate to recommend not smoking for at least 1 week after the operation. At the end of the first three-day period, the first washing process begins. You should carry out the washing process for 15 days starting from the third day, with the special care lotion and shampoo recommended by your doctor, as instructed to you. With the regular washing process, the crusts formed after planting will soften and will be completely shed at the end of the 15th day. Heavy and compelling sports should be avoided for at least 1 month after hair transplantation because sports cause blood pressure and heart rate to increase. The increase in heart rate can damage the transplanted hair follicles by causing bleeding in the microchannels where the hair follicles are transplanted. We do not recommend that you stay away from activities that will tire you out and make you sweat for at least a week. However, 4-5 days after the operation, you can do light-paced walks that will not tire you and will not make you sweat. After the hair transplant procedure, you need to protect the transplanted area from sunlight for about 2 weeks. When you go out, you can wear a hat that will not overwhelm your head. By the first month following hair transplantation, a significant procedure will have been finished. At this stage, it would be appropriate to talk about 'shock shedding'. Your transplanted hair will fall out within a few weeks following the transplantation. There is no need to worry as 90% of patients go through this phase. This is because the grafts switch to the sleep phase (telogen) by activating the defense mechanism due to the trauma experienced during the removal of hair follicles in hair transplantation. Your transplanted hair will enter a new growth cycle from the 2nd to the 4th month and will start to grow healthily. We advise you to be patient during this process. After the fourth month, your hair will grow even longer and start to form its structure. From the 8th month, you will see that your transplanted hair has become thicker and thicker. About 1 year after the operation, your transplanted hair will become thicker and take on a natural look like your existing hair.
