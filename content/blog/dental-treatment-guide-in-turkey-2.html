What is Cyst Operation?

A cyst operation, or cyst removal surgery, is a procedure to remove a cyst—a fluid-filled sac that can form in various parts of the body, often under the skin or in organs. The operation involves making a small incision over the cyst, removing it along with its sac to prevent recurrence, and closing the area with stitches. This procedure may be done under local or general anesthesia, depending on the cyst’s size and location. Cyst operations are typically recommended if the cyst is painful, infected, or poses health risks.

How Much Does Cyst Operation Cost in Turkey?

The cost of a cyst operation in Turkey varies widely based on factors like the type and location of the cyst, the hospital or clinic chosen, and whether additional services (such as anesthesia and post-op care) are needed. On average, prices range from €169 to €1,800. Minor cyst removals, like those under the skin (e.g., sebaceous cysts), are on the lower end, while cysts in more complex or sensitive areas may cost more due to surgical complexity. What is a Frenectomy Operation?

A frenectomy is a minor surgical procedure that removes or alters the frenum—a small fold of tissue that connects parts of the mouth, such as under the tongue (lingual frenum) or inside the upper lip (labial frenum). This procedure is often recommended for people with issues like tongue tie (restricted tongue movement), speech difficulties, or gaps between teeth caused by a tight frenum. A frenectomy can be done with traditional surgical tools or laser technology, usually under local anesthesia, and generally has a quick recovery time with minimal discomfort.

How Much Does Frenectomy Operation Cost in Turkey?

The price varies depending on the clinic or hospital, the type of frenectomy (lingual or labial), and whether traditional surgery or laser technology is used. Laser frenectomies are often more expensive due to the precision and reduced recovery time they offer.
