Do clinics in Turkey use the latest techniques like FUE and DHI?

When patients search for modern solutions, one of the most common questions is whether FUE and DHI hair transplant in Turkey is widely available. The answer is yes. Clinics across Turkey have adopted these advanced techniques, making the country a global leader in hair restoration. FUE, or Follicular Unit Extraction, is highly preferred because it minimizes scarring and speeds up recovery. In this method, individual grafts are extracted and implanted with precision. Turkish doctors have extensive experience with FUE, ensuring natural density and smooth results. Patients often choose this option for its reliability and long lasting success. DHI, or Direct Hair Implantation, is another popular method. With this technique, grafts are placed directly into the scalp using a special implanter pen. This allows for greater control over angle and depth, creating a hairline that looks extremely natural. Many patients appreciate that DHI requires less downtime, which is especially valuable for international visitors. At Medical Center, both FUE and DHI techniques are performed with modern equipment and patient focused planning. Surgeons carefully evaluate hair type, scalp condition, and personal goals before choosing the right method. This tailored approach ensures that every patient achieves results that match their expectations.
