What types of eye surgeries are available in Turkey

Refractive (Vision-Correction) Surgeries
  • LASIK, including advanced femtosecond (bladeless) LASIK for myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism
  • PRK / LASEK, surface ablation options suitable for patients with thin corneas
  • SMILE (Small Incision Lenticule Extraction), minimally invasive and flap-free, is increasingly common
  • Refractive Lens Exchange (RLE) or clear lens extraction — replacing a clear lens with an intraocular lens (IOL), often for presbyopia or high prescriptions
Cataract Surgery
  • Phacoemulsification (ultrasound-assisted removal
  • Femtosecond laser–assisted cataract surgery for precision incisions
  • Replacement with intraocular lenses (IOLs)—monofocal, multifocal, toric, trifocal, EDOF or “smart” premium lenses
Intraocular Lens Implantation (IOL)
  • Used with cataract or refractive lens exchange, offering options like monofocal, multifocal, toric, trifocal, and EDOF smart lenses
Glaucoma Surgeries
  • Laser treatments (e.g., trabeculoplasty, iridotomy)
  • Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery (MIGS), trabeculectomy, tube shunts
Retinal Surgeries & Treatments
  • Vitrectomy, retinal detachment repair, macular hole surgery
  • Anti‑VEGF injections, laser photocoagulation for diabetic retinopathy or macular degeneration
Corneal Procedures
  • Corneal transplants (penetrating or lamellar keratoplasty)
  • Keratoconus treatments
  • Pterygium removal, corneal cross-linking
Eye Muscle (Strabismus) Surgery
  • Corrects misaligned eyes to restore alignment and binocular vision
Oculoplastic & Eyelid Surgeries
  • Blepharoplasty, ptosis repair, eyelid lifts, often both cosmetic and functional

How do I choose the best eye surgeon in Turkey?

Choosing the right eye surgeon is crucial to achieving safe and successful results. In Turkey, many ophthalmologists are internationally trained and highly specialized, but it’s important to look for certain key criteria:
  • Experience & Credentials – Check the surgeon’s qualifications, years of experience, and specialization (LASIK, cataracts, lens implants, etc.).
  • Clinic Accreditation – Make sure the clinic is certified by the Turkish Ministry of Health and preferably by international organizations like JCI.
  • Technology & Techniques – Choose a clinic that uses the latest equipment such as femtosecond lasers, SMILE systems, and premium IOLs.
  • Patient Reviews & Results – Look for verified testimonials and before–after cases from previous international patients.
  • Language & Communication – Ensure the surgeon or their team can communicate clearly in English or your native language.
  • Transparency – A trustworthy clinic will provide clear pricing, detailed treatment plans, and ongoing support.

How long is the recovery period after eye surgery in Turkey?

The recovery period after eye surgery in Turkey varies depending on the type of procedure performed:
  • LASIK and SMILE surgeries
Most patients experience improved vision within 24 to 48 hours. Full stabilization typically takes about 1 to 2 weeks, during which patients should avoid strenuous activities and follow post-op care instructions carefully.
  • PRK surgery
Recovery is slower than LASIK, often taking 3 to 5 days for initial healing and up to a month for complete visual stabilization.
  • Cataract surgery
Initial vision improvement is usually noticeable within a few days. Full recovery can take 4 to 6 weeks, depending on the patient’s overall eye health.
  • Other surgeries (e.g., corneal transplants, glaucoma surgery)
Recovery times vary widely and can range from weeks to months, depending on complexity.
