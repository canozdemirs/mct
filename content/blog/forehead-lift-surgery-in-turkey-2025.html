What are the different types of forehead lift surgery available in Turkey?

Are you looking for a way to achieve a youthful, refreshed look by reducing forehead wrinkles and sagging brows? If so, a forehead lift in Turkey might be the perfect solution for you! Known for its world-class surgeons, state-of-the-art clinics, and affordable prices, Turkey has become a global hotspot for cosmetic procedures. But before you book your appointment, let’s explore the different types of forehead lift surgery available in Turkey—so you can choose the best option for your beauty goals!
  • Classic (Coronal) Forehead Lift – The Traditional Approach
This time-tested method involves making an incision across the scalp, hidden within the hairline, to lift and tighten the forehead skin. It’s an effective option for those with significant sagging and deep forehead wrinkles. While the results are long-lasting and dramatic, it does require a longer recovery period.
  • Temporal (Lateral) Brow Lift – A Subtle, Yet Effective Lift
If you’re concerned about drooping outer eyebrows and crow’s feet, the temporal brow lift could be your best option. This technique focuses on lifting the outer portion of the eyebrows, giving your face a more youthful and refreshed look. It’s less invasive than a full forehead lift, making it a great choice for those who want a subtle enhancement.
  • Direct Brow Lift – Targeted and Precise
For patients who want to reshape and reposition the eyebrows, a direct brow lift is a great choice. Unlike other forehead lift procedures, this method removes excess skin just above the eyebrows to create a more defined arch. It’s commonly performed on men or those who don’t mind a visible scar in exchange for dramatic brow enhancement.

What is the recovery process like after a forehead lift surgery?

The recovery process after a forehead lift surgery, also known as a brow lift, can vary depending on the type of procedure performed and the individual’s overall health. However, here’s a general outline of what to expect:
  • First Few Days: Mild swelling, bruising, and tightness are normal. Keep your head elevated and apply cold compresses to reduce discomfort.
  • First Week: Stitches or staples are usually removed within 7–10 days. Most patients feel comfortable returning to light activities.
  • 2–4 Weeks: Swelling continues to fade, and results start becoming more visible. Avoid heavy lifting and intense workouts.
  • Full Recovery: By 4–6 weeks, you’ll notice a refreshed, youthful look with natural, long-lasting results.
The exact recovery timeline will depend on the individual, so it's important to consult with your surgeon about any concerns and your personal recovery plan.
