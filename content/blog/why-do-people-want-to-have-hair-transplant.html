Types of Hair Loss

Male Pattern Hair Loss

Hair loss that occurs due to the effect of androgens on a genetic basis is called "Androgenetic hair loss". It is seen in about half of men and 20-25% of women. Although patients occasionally report significant hair loss, it is often an insidious loss (unlike telogen hair loss) and the situation improves with time. Sometimes patients notice that their hair is thinning rather than hair lost. In essence, it can be observed that the hair is thinned at the top of the head and is thinner than it is at the rear. It is challenging to identify in the early stages, especially in women, and is readily mistaken with other types of hair loss. It should be known that the treatment will take years.

Alopecia Type Hair Loss

Alopecia areata; It is a frequently observed disease that appears suddenly, and is characterized by one or several rounds, completely balding areas of varying diameters. Although it goes by the moniker "ringkran," it is not infectious and is not a fungal illness, unlike what its name would imply. It can be observed in the beard region in addition to the hair. With therapy, it often regresses within a few months or years. Do not ignore your hair loss if it persists. In case of persistent spills, it is very important to consult a dermatologist, be examined get the necessary tests and go for treatment.

In long-term hypothyroidism, permanent root loss develops and overworking of the thyroid gland, in other words, hyperthyroidism may be the cause of hair loss.
