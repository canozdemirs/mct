What is a Bichectomy?

Bichectomy, also called cheek reduction surgery, is the removal of excess fat from the cheeks to increase the cheekbones and balance the proportions of the face. Fat is removed from inside the mouth to give the face a more attractive and balanced appearance, and large and swollen aspects of the face that cause individual discomfort are corrected. This technique emphasizes the cheekbones, smooths the face, and gives a slim and youthful appearance. During the bichectomy surgery, a 100mm incision is made at this point to access the fat, and about 40-50 percent of the fat is extracted. People undergo bichectomies because puffy cheeks and invisible cheekbones can lead to low self-esteem. Another reason why people undergo this type of cosmetic procedure is that Hollywood stars have stereotypically narrow faces and prominent cheekbones; That's why people want to look like stars in TV series and movies.

Bihectomy Procedure

Bihectomy is a quick and technically simple aesthetic-functional treatment. Your surgeon will likely ask you to stop certain medications before the procedure. These include blood thinners such as aspirin. If you are a smoker, you should quit smoking four weeks before surgery. and arrange for laboratory tests or medical evaluations Your operation includes the following steps:
  • This procedure begins with skin lines on your lower cheekbones based on a surgical plan that you and your surgeon have made.
  • Then, like a dental procedure, a pain reliever is injected into your cheek.
  • When you're done, make a small incision on the inside of the cheek near the upper molar.
  • The buccal fat pad is gradually torn off and removed by cautery.
  • Finally, the incision is closed with stitches which will dissolve on their own after about a week.
Buccal fat reduction is an outpatient surgical treatment that is usually performed under local anesthesia when not combined with other cosmetic surgeries. And it only takes a few minutes to complete. The discomfort is minor, and recovery is easy. However, you will be limited to soft foods for a few days after surgery, and bruising is rare and usually minimal. Cheek fat reduction results, on the other hand, are permanent; however, your entire face continues to age and can change as you lose or gain weight.
