What Is a Graft?

A graft is the process of transferring tissue from one part of the body to another. The grafting process is widely used in various medical fields, including hair transplantation in Turkey, where it has become a common and well-developed practice. In grafting, the tissue can be taken from the patient’s own body (autograft) or, in some cases, from another individual (allograft).

The tissues used in grafting include skin, bone, blood vessels, nerves, fat tissue, dermis, hair follicles, nail beds, and stem cells. In most grafting procedures, a single type of tissue is transplanted. These tissues may consist of dermis, subcutaneous fat, skin, or cartilage, depending on the medical purpose. Each type of graft has a specific usage area based on the patient’s needs.

Skin grafts are commonly used in the field of plastic surgery in Turkey, particularly in cases involving tissue loss such as burns, chronic wounds, skin tumors, and traumatic injuries. In hair transplantation, the graft refers to the hair follicle along with the nourishing tissue attached to it. These grafts are carefully extracted and implanted to ensure healthy growth and natural results, which has contributed to Turkey’s strong reputation in graft-based medical procedures.

In this method, grafts play a crucial role in determining the overall cost, as the price of the procedure is calculated based on the number of grafts to be transplanted.

FUE Hair Transplant

The FUE hair transplantation process is one of the most used techniques in hair transplantation worldwide. Which is also the hair transplantation method used in John Travolta's Hair Transplantation. This procedure involves neither piercing the skin nor making an incision on the scalp. Follicular Unit Extraction is referred to by the acronym FUE. With 1 mm punches, this technique was initially applied in Japan in 1988. In 2002, the technique first appeared in the medical literature. The FUE procedure involves removing individual hair follicles from the region designated as the donor area, separating them from the epidermis, and implanting them in the desired location. In the same way, hair follicles removed from the donor region are transplanted to the target location one at a time rather than all at once. Because of this, this treatment requires more sessions than other methods because hair follicles are implanted. Nowadays, a micromotor is used in place of punches for this technique. The FUE technique of hair transplantation is quite popular. It matters how this approach is used. First and foremost, trained healthcare providers should use the procedure. What Are Techniques For FUE Hair Transplantation?

  • Technique for Micro FUE Hair Transplants It is a better iteration of the manual FUE method. Using tools with tiny points, hair follicles are removed during the micro hair transplantation method. This method makes use of tiny-tipped micromotors. The tips work based on drilling holes that are between 0.6 and 0.9 mm in diameter. On the scalp, tiny indentations are made, and the hair follicle and the supporting tissues are separated and removed.
  • Saffir FUE Hair Transplant Saffir FUE and Micro FUE aren't truly procedures, just like the Micro FUE method. They are only FUE-related variants. The tips used in Saffir FUE are made of sapphire rather than metal, which is the sole change. It works well for lessening crusting rates by opening tiny chambers in the scalp. Additionally, it helps to speed up the phases of recuperation. Compared to other needle architectures, microneedles with sapphire tips are healthier.
  • Soft FUE Hair Transplant Despite being referred to be a hair transplantation sub-method, FUE is really a sub-innovation of the FUE technique. The hair transplant procedure is assisted with sedative-focused medications to soothe the patient while having little impact on his consciousness, according to the definition of SOFT FUE.
