What is Neck lift surgery?

A neck lift, also known as platysmaplasty or lower rhytidectomy, is a cosmetic surgical procedure that improves visible signs of aging in the jawline and neck. It is designed to:
  • Remove excess skin and fat from the neck area
  • Tighten underlying muscles (platysma)
  • Improve the contour of the jawline and eliminate sagging or "turkey neck" appearanc
  • Smooth out wrinkles and loose skin on the neck
Neck lift surgery can be performed on its own or combined with other procedures like a facelift, chin liposuction, or eyelid surgery for more comprehensive facial rejuvenation. It is typically done under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation, and the results are long-lasting with proper care.

What is the recovery process like after a neck lift in Turkey?

The recovery process after a neck lift surgery in Turkey is carefully managed to ensure your safety, comfort, and optimal results. Here’s what you can typically expect:
  • Immediately After Surgery
You will spend 1 night in the hospital under close medical supervision. Swelling, bruising, and mild discomfort are normal and will be managed with prescribed medications.
  • First Week
Most patients experience swelling and some tightness in the neck area for about 7–10 days. You’ll need to keep your head elevated and avoid strenuous activities. Light walking is encouraged to improve circulation. Dressings and sutures are usually removed within 5–7 days.
  • Weeks 2 to 4
Swelling and bruising gradually diminish. You can usually return to light daily activities and work, depending on your comfort level. Avoid heavy lifting, vigorous exercise, and sun exposure on scars.
  • After One Month
Most visible swelling and bruising resolve. You’ll start seeing the improved contour and tighter appearance of your neck. Scars continue to heal and fade over several months.
