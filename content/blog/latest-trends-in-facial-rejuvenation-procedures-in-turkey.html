What non-surgical facial rejuvenation treatments are gaining popularity in Turkey?

In Turkey, several non-surgical facial rejuvenation treatments are gaining popularity as individuals seek minimally invasive procedures with quick recovery times and natural results. Among the leading options are injectables like dermal fillers (e.g., Juvéderm and Restylane) and neurotoxins such as Botox. These treatments are designed to reduce wrinkles, restore volume, and smooth facial features. Laser lifts, such as those using Nd technology, are also trending, offering a non-invasive alternative to facelifts by stimulating collagen production and tightening skin without the need for incisions. Additionally, Kybella, an injectable treatment targeting fat under the chin, is popular for contouring the lower face without surgery​. These procedures cater to the growing demand for treatments that allow people to maintain a youthful appearance with minimal downtime and no need for extensive recovery, making them highly appealing in Turkey's aesthetic market.

What are the most requested facial rejuvenation treatments in Turkey?

These treatments are highly sought after due to their ability to provide significant rejuvenation with little to no recovery time, making them attractive options for those seeking quick, minimally invasive solutions.
