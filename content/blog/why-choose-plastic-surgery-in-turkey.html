What is plastic surgery?

Repairment and reconstruction of lost or damaged skin and tissue are accomplished through plastic surgery. Plastic surgery's primary goal is to as closely mimic the normal function and look for tissue and skin as possible.

What is the difference between plastic and cosmetic surgery?

A unique area of medicine called cosmetic surgery makes use of both surgical and pharmaceutical techniques to enhance appearance. The head, neck, and body as a whole are all acceptable candidates for cosmetic surgery. Since the areas treated lack much visual appeal but still function normally, cosmetic surgery is not required. On the other hand, plastic surgery is a branch of medicine that focuses on correcting face and body deformities that result from illnesses, burns, trauma, and congenital malformations. In other words, reconstructive in nature, plastic surgery is performed to repair damaged bodily parts whereas plastic surgery is done just to change a person's appearance to achieve what they think to have a more attractive look.

Does medical insurance cover plastic surgeries?

The details of your insurance policy will determine whether or not plastic surgery is covered. Most insurance providers classify plastic surgery operations as cosmetic, reconstructive, or medically necessary to maintain quality of life or bodily function. Cosmetic plastic surgery is typically not covered by insurance, while reconstructive or medically essential plastic surgery is more likely to be. The policy may cause a change in this. However, the majority of insurance companies don't pay for elective cosmetic surgery. Understanding the many forms of plastic surgery accessible is necessary to determine what procedures are covered by insurance. Depending on the insurance provider you choose, the kind of coverage you have, and the specific procedure you want, insurance may or may not cover plastic surgery.

What are the risks of plastic surgery?

The hazards of plastic surgery are the same as those of any form of surgery. The degree of danger is influenced by the size of the affected area, the skill of the surgeon, and the patient's general health. Specific risks are associated with several operations, but generic concerns include discomfort and suffering, bleeding, infection, and scarring. If you have unexpected pain, swelling, discharge, or other adverse effects following surgery, call your surgeon, medical staff, or general practitioner right away.

What are the most popular types of plastic surgeries in Turkey?

Rhinoplasty

The most popular plastic surgery procedure in Turkey is rhinoplasty. Since the nose is the main feature of the face and determines its expression, many people seek medical advice to improve its appearance. Rhinoplasty, or "nose job," is the surgically altering of the nose's appearance. Mostly cartilage at the bottom and bone at the top make up the structure of the nose. Rhinoplasty may be performed to improve breathing, alter the nose's appearance, or both. With your surgeon, go over the advantages of rhinoplasty and whether it is the correct procedure for you. When planning a rhinoplasty, your surgeon will consider your other facial features, the skin on your nose, and the adjustments you wish to make. If you are a candidate for surgery, your surgeon will develop a special plan just for you.

Hair Transplantation

Hair transplantation is extremely widespread in Turkey as a type of plastic surgery. In this surgery, hair follicles are removed from one area of the body, referred to as the "donor site," and then surgically transplanted to another area of the body that is bald or thinning out, referred to as the "receiver site." Male pattern baldness is the main condition treated by the technique. When patients receive hair transplants, they permanently restore their hair, just as if they had never lost it.

Liposuction

Liposuction can also be listed as a prevalent type of plastic surgery in Turkey. The greatest convenience of liposuction surgery is that it does not relate to weight loss, which is demanding to achieve. By this way liposuction undertakes the role of body shaping on its own, making it more desirable. It involves eliminating little pockets of fat that are difficult to lose through exercise and a healthy diet. It is done on areas of the body where fat tends to build up, such as the buttocks, hips, thighs, and stomach. The goal is to alter your body form, and results are typically long-lasting if you maintain a healthy weight. It works best on those with a healthy weight and on tight skin.

Breast Augmentation

Breast augmentation surgery is another type of surgery that is widely performed in Turkey. It can also be referred to as a “boob job”.  General anesthesia is used during breast implant surgery. The procedure entails: placing the implant either behind your chest muscle or between your breast tissue and the implant and making an incision in the skin close to or below the breast (as discussed during your consultation) securing the wound with stitches and a dressing. The popularity of breast augmentation procedures is rising as surgeons increasingly provide exceptionally natural-looking results.

Breast Reduction

Breast reduction is one of the most common plastic surgery in Turkey. Skin, breast tissue, and fat are removed during breast reduction surgery, also known as reduction mammaplasty. This procedure is preferred especially by mothers with enlarged breasts and frequent pregnancies. Consult a board-certified plastic surgeon if you are considering breast reduction surgery. It's critical to comprehend the dangers and potential problems associated with breast reduction surgery. Knowing what the procedure can and cannot do is also crucial.

Eyelid Aesthetics

The most popular method of facial plastic surgery in Turkey is eyelid aesthetics, usually referred to as blepharoplasty. In the medical community, blepharoplasty is the name of a surgical procedure used to improve the appearance of the eyelids. The person appears older due to bagging or sagging of the eyes and eyelids, which immediately affects the facial expression. Eyelid aesthetics includes not just the sagging of the eyelids but also the removal of extra fat tissue, skin, and muscle from the lower or upper eyelids.
