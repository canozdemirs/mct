How is Gynecomastia Diagnosed in Turkey?

Men experiencing breast enlargement or related concerns should first consult a specialist doctor in Turkey. A qualified physician can determine whether the growth is due to adipose (fat) tissue or glandular tissue, which is crucial for proper diagnosis and treatment.

During the initial consultation, the doctor will ask about family history and any related medical conditions. While these questions provide useful background information, they are not sufficient for a definitive diagnosis. A physical examination of the breasts is performed, and if the glandular tissue is more than 0.5 cm, gynecomastia may be suspected.

To ensure accuracy, additional tests such as a mammogram or breast ultrasound are often recommended. These imaging techniques allow a detailed evaluation of the breast tissue. Blood tests may also be requested to rule out hormonal imbalances or other underlying conditions.

In Turkey, experienced specialists combine the results of physical examination, imaging tests, and laboratory tests to provide a precise diagnosis and develop a personalized treatment plan for gynecomastia.
