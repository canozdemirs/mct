What are liposuction and abdominoplasty?

1. What is liposuction?

Liposuction is a surgical method applied to people who do not have weight problems such as obesity but have lubrication that cannot be eliminated by regional slimming methods, to remove excess fat tissue and to give the body a better shape. Liposuction is generally known as fat removal, and this application is mainly applied to areas where stubborn lubrication and regional excesses such as hips, hips, abdomen, waist, and knees are seen. In addition, liposuction is one of the most applied and preferred aesthetic operations by patients from past years to the present. Liposuction is a procedure performed by vacuuming and removing the fat tissues, which are made more fluid by various methods, through 1-2 mm incisions, using thin and long cannulas with a diameter of 2-3 mm.

2. Who can prefer liposuction?

As in every surgical operation, the opinion of a specialist doctor is important in the liposuction procedure. However, one of the points that should be known is that Liposuction is not a slimming method, it is only a method that provides regional thinning and body lines to become smoother. Patients with a weight problem other than regional resistant lubrication are recommended to apply for a controlled diet and sports program first, and then it is more appropriate for patients to apply this method for excess fat that does not go away with diet. It is more appropriate for the patients who will prefer the liposuction procedure to prefer those who do not have a systemic health problem, which is not pregnant or have just given birth. Liposuction is a method that can be applied to everyone, but before indicating the application to people with systemic health problems such as heart disease and diabetes, risk factors should be determined by applying several tests and necessary precautions should be taken.

3. What is abdominoplasty (Tummy-Tuck Surgery)?

Abdominoplasty is a surgical operation in which the muscles in the abdomen are stretched and strengthened by removing excess fat and sagging skin tissue in the lower and middle areas of the abdomen. Abdominoplasty can be performed for aesthetic purposes or functional purposes if it prevents the patient's excess fat from moving. Abdominoplasty is not an obesity treatment and is different. It is ensured that people suffering from obesity lose weight before undergoing surgery. Abdominoplasty is performed in hospitals and general anaesthesia is required, but if the operation is limited to a very small area, local anaesthesia may be preferred. Thanks to the operation, the appearance of the belly and abdomen will be much better than before, but the scar on the abdomen is permanent after the operation.

4. Who can prefer abdominoplasty (Tummy-Tuck Surgery)?

Abdominoplasty is suitable for healthy men and women. The most suitable people for abdominoplasty surgery are those who are close to their ideal body weight, do not smoke, and take care of their health. Diet and exercise have helped you lose weight, but if your tummy is still protruding and women who have had several pregnancies may opt for tummy tuck surgery to tighten their abdominal muscles. If you are planning to become pregnant, you will need to postpone the surgery until you have a child. The muscles to be compressed during the surgery can be separated during pregnancy and the surgical effect can be eliminated. If your goal is to lose weight, tummy tuck surgery is not an operation that will help you lose weight and will not provide many benefits.  

What are the differences between liposuction and abdominoplasty?

Liposuction and tummy tuck, both procedures performed by Plastic surgeons, aim to improve the appearance of your stomach, making it appear flatter, tighter, and smaller. Although both aim to improve the appearance of your midsection, these procedures are different and have different risks and consequences.
  • Liposuction is a simple procedure with little risk or recovery downtime. Liposuction is an operation that removes small fat deposits in the body on the hips, thighs, buttocks, or stomach.
  • Abdominoplasty is considered a more serious operation. Abdominoplasty removes excess fat and skin that cannot be fixed with diet and exercise. Your doctor or surgeon will help determine which procedure might be right for you.
