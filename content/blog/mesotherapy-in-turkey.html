What is body mesotherapy?

We can divide body mesotherapy into two as cellulite mesotherapy and regional thinning. Cellulite is a cosmetic problem that affects 85-90% of adult women. Considerations After Mesotherapy In Turkey

There are certain points to be considered after the mesotherapy procedure, which can be applied to different parts of the body in many different ways. Paying attention to these points is important both to obtain high efficiency from the application and to experience a faster recovery process. The main items to be considered after the application are as follows:
  • Before each session, blood thinners should be avoided.
  • After the application session, the application area should not be in contact with water for approximately 15 hours.
  • Forceful movements should be avoided for 48 hours after each session.
  • Make-up should not be applied to the points where mesotherapy is applied for approximately one day.
  • Tight clothes should not be preferred in the area where mesotherapy is applied.

What are the Differences Between Mesotherapy and PRP?

The substances used in mesotherapy and PRP applications are different. In the mesotherapy application, many substances such as hyaluronic acid, growth factors, amino acids are injected into the skin, while in the PRP application, the person's own blood is separated and injected back into the person.
