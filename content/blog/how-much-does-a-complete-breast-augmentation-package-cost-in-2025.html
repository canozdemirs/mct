What is Breast Agumentation Surgery?

Breast augmentation in Turkey is a surgical procedure designed to enhance the size, shape, and symmetry of the breasts. This can be achieved through implants filled with silicone or saline, or via fat grafting, where fat is taken from another part of the body and transferred to the breasts.

Many patients choose breast augmentation in Turkey for cosmetic reasons such as increasing breast volume, restoring shape after pregnancy or weight loss, or correcting asymmetry. It is also used in breast reconstruction following mastectomy.

Performed under general anesthesia, breast augmentation in Turkey allows most patients to return to normal activities within a few weeks. While the procedure is generally safe, potential risks include infection, scarring, implant rupture, or capsular contracture.

How is Breast Agumentation Process Progressing?

The breast augmentation process in Turkey is carefully structured to ensure safety, achieve optimal results, and support a smooth recovery. Understanding each stage helps you prepare and feel confident about your surgery abroad.

Consultation with a Plastic Surgeon

Initial Assessment: Your journey starts with a consultation with a board-certified plastic surgeon. During this session, your goals, medical history, and any concerns are discussed in detail.

Examination and Measurements: The surgeon evaluates your chest size, breast tissue, and overall body proportions to determine the most suitable implant type and size for your body.

Options and Recommendations: Surgeons explain different implant options (saline or silicone), shapes, sizes, and profiles to match your desired outcome. The surgical approach, including incision and implant placement options, is also reviewed.

Pre-Op Instructions: You will receive detailed guidance on preparing for surgery, such as lifestyle adjustments, medications to avoid, and any required preoperative tests.

Pre-Operative Preparation

Medical Evaluation: Based on your health history, your surgeon may request tests like blood work or imaging to confirm that you are fit for surgery.

Surgery Planning: The surgeon schedules your procedure, explains the anesthesia type (usually general anesthesia), and provides an estimated recovery timeline.

Arranging Support: It is essential to arrange transportation for after your surgery, as you will be unable to drive yourself home post-anesthesia. Support from a friend or family member ensures a safe and stress-free return to your accommodation.

The breast augmentation procedure in Turkey is designed to ensure patient safety, comfort, and optimal aesthetic results. Here’s a detailed look at what happens during and after the surgery.

Anesthesia and Incision

Anesthesia: On the day of your surgery, you will receive general anesthesia to remain comfortable and asleep throughout the procedure.

Incision Locations: The surgeon makes an incision to insert the implants. Common incision sites include:

  • Inframammary (under the breast): The most common site, resulting in minimal visible scarring.

  • Periareolar (around the areola): The incision is made along the edge of the darker skin of the areola.

  • Transaxillary (in the armpit): This approach leaves no visible breast scarring.

Implant Placement: Depending on your body type and chosen technique, the implant is placed either under the breast tissue or beneath the chest muscle.

Closing the Incision: After placement, incisions are closed with sutures, and a surgical bra or bandages are applied to support healing.

Post-Operative Care and Recovery

Initial Recovery: Immediately after surgery, you will stay in a recovery room as anesthesia wears off. Feeling groggy or sore for several hours is normal. Arranging transportation home is essential.

Recovery at Home:

  • Swelling, bruising, and mild discomfort are common in the first few days. Pain medication may be prescribed.

  • Follow your surgeon’s post-op instructions, including keeping your breasts elevated, avoiding strenuous activity, and wearing a supportive bra.

Follow-Up Appointments: Return visits with your surgeon ensure proper healing, allow for stitch removal (if needed), and confirm implants are positioned correctly.

Activity Restrictions: Avoid lifting heavy objects or high-impact activities for several weeks. Your surgeon will guide you on when it is safe to resume normal routines.

Long-Term Results of Breast Augmentation in Turkey

Healing and Final Results: Swelling generally decreases within a few weeks, and the final breast shape becomes evident within 3 to 6 months. Implants settle into their final position as tissues adjust.

Long-Term Care: Regular follow-ups help monitor implant condition and overall satisfaction. Some implants may need replacement or exchange after several years, depending on type.

Possible Complications: Although generally safe, potential risks include infection, changes in sensation, implant rupture, or capsular contracture. Monitoring your breasts and consulting your surgeon if unusual symptoms arise is crucial.

Considerations for Future Adjustments

Over time, you may opt for revision surgery to change implant size, shape, or type. Some patients also consider a breast lift or additional procedures to maintain aesthetic results as they age.

What to Expect After Breast Agumentation Procedure in Turkey?

After breast augmentation in Turkey, understanding the recovery process is important for a smooth and safe healing journey. Here’s what you can expect:

Immediately After Surgery

  • Effects of Anesthesia: You may feel groggy or drowsy upon waking from anesthesia. Mild discomfort is normal as the anesthesia wears off.

  • Initial Pain and Swelling: Swelling, bruising, and soreness around the chest area are common and may last from a few days to several weeks. Pain is generally manageable with prescribed medications.

First Few Days of Recovery

  • Rest and Healing: Rest is crucial during the initial days to allow your body to heal. Avoid strenuous activities, especially lifting heavy objects.

  • Drains and Bandages: Some patients may have small drains to remove excess fluid, which are usually removed within a few days. Surgical bras or bandages help support proper healing.

  • Follow-Up Appointments: Returning to your clinic for a follow-up ensures that healing is on track and stitches are removed if necessary.

First Two Weeks After Breast Augmentation in Turkey

  • Swelling and Bruising: Swelling and bruising are common during the first two weeks after breast augmentation in Turkey, but they gradually decrease. The final shape of your breasts may not fully appear until the swelling subsides.

  • Restricted Activities: Avoid physical exertion, including exercise and heavy lifting, for at least two weeks. You may also be advised not to sleep on your stomach or side during this period.

  • Pain Relief: Some discomfort is normal, but pain should be manageable with over-the-counter or prescribed medications from your surgeon.

Longer-Term Recovery (1-3 Months) After Breast Augmentation in Turkey

  • Gradual Shape and Sensitivity: Breasts will slowly settle into their final shape and size. Temporary numbness or sensitivity around the nipples may occur but usually resolves over time.

  • Resuming Normal Activities: Light activities can generally resume after 4-6 weeks, while more intensive exercise may be allowed after 2-3 months, following your surgeon’s guidance.

  • Support Garment: Wearing a supportive bra for several weeks helps the implants settle in the correct position and reduces swelling.

Potential Risks and Complications

  • Infection: Rare but possible. Following all post-operative care instructions and attending follow-ups helps minimize this risk.

  • Capsular Contracture: Scar tissue around the implant may tighten, which can affect the breast’s appearance or feel.

  • Implant Positioning: Implants may slightly shift during healing; adjustments might be needed in some cases.

Long-Term Care After Breast Augmentation in Turkey

  • Regular Check-Ups: Annual visits to your surgeon are recommended to monitor implant condition.

  • Lifetime Considerations: Breast implants are durable but may require replacement over time due to wear, rupture, or personal aesthetic preferences.
