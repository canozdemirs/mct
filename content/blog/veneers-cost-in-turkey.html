Frequently Asked Questions About Veneers

What are the benefits of dental veneers?

People who have discolored, worn down, chipped, misaligned, spaced, uneven, or oddly shaped front teeth can benefit from dental veneers.

What does it take to receive a dental veneer?

In most cases, a porcelain veneer is obtained by a multi-step method. Making an appointment with a prosthodontist is the first step. At this session, you can discuss your goals and concerns, and a prosthodontist will explain how veneers can help you. It's conceivable that you'll need X-rays or impressions of your teeth. The next step is to prepare the tooth for the veneer. After a small amount of tooth structure is removed, an impression is taken. The prosthodontist will then place temporary veneers on the teeth. At the next appointment, the temporaries are removed and the final veneer is placed on your tooth.

What are the advantages of dental veneers? 

Because only a small amount of tooth material is removed, veneers keep the most amount of tooth structure feasible. Dental veneers can improve the look of your teeth while also being well tolerated by the gums. Veneers can be tailored in color and, in the case of porcelain veneers, are stain resistant.

What type of person is a good candidate for porcelain veneers?

You could be interested in veneers if your teeth are discoloured or chipped. Veneers will cover up your current teeth. They're also popular for smile makeovers or for people who want to achieve their ideal smile.

What is the average lifespan of dental veneers?

The average lifespan of a veneer is between 5 and 10 years. Other than normal brushing, flossing, and visits to the prosthodontist, they don't require any particular care. They can live considerably longer if they are properly cared for. Professional cleanings every six months and a yearly exam are strongly recommended.

How many veneers will I require? and How do you calculate veneers cost in Turkey?

To decide how many veneers are needed or whether veneers are the best option, a prosthodontic consultation is required. Having an even amount of veneers is a good idea. The best cosmetic effects are achieved with two, four, eight, ten, or twelve veneers.

What is the best way to look after my veneers?

Brushing for two minutes twice a day, flossing daily, and wearing a bite guard at night are all effective strategies to safeguard veneers. Avoiding really hard foods, such as almonds, or cracking shells with the front veneers is also recommended.

What is the most prevalent reason for replacing veneers?

Under normal circumstances, the porcelain veneer is highly sturdy and should not crack. The root of the tooth may become revealed if gum recession develops. New veneers may be required to cover the roots. Veneered teeth have a somewhat reduced integrity, and the veneer is attached to the existing teeth. If the veneers are properly cared for, including frequent brushing with toothpaste and flossing, there is no increased risk of rotting.

Can I preview my veneers before they go in?

By taking a picture of the patient's face and digitally altering the teeth, a digital grin preview can be created. This will offer a patient a decent sense of how veneers will look and help them decide if they are suited for them.

How can I get veneers that match the rest of my teeth?

Your prosthodontist will address this matter. Most people have their veneers made in a lighter hue than their natural teeth and then whiten their teeth to match.

Is the procedure painful?

Patients are usually numb during the veneering procedure. Once the numbness takes effect, there should be no pain during the process. After the numbness wears off in lesser cases (two or four veneers), there will be little to no discomfort. In cases involving eight or more veneers, patients should expect some gum and probably jaw irritation. Over-the-counter drugs can usually relieve discomfort.

Will I need to have my gums contoured?

In some circumstances, contouring the gums will improve the cosmetic effect of a veneer. It's done to elongate small teeth and achieve symmetry. The majority of gum contouring treatments produce no additional discomfort during or after the procedure. If the teeth are extremely short, surgical contouring may be required. For this operation, patients are sent to a periodontist.

What habits should I get rid of or cut out?

Stop nail-biting, straw chewing, fork nibbling, opening bags with teeth, pulling on garments or gloves with teeth, and anything else that puts undue strain on the veneers right away.

Can I brush and floss my teeth after getting veneers, and do I still need to see a dentist for professional hygiene?

Yes! Brush all of your teeth in the same manner. You can floss all of your veneered teeth, and you should keep up with your regular hygiene checkups as usual.
