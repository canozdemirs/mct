Nose Aesthetic Surgery - How Is Rhinoplasty Performed?

Different surgical techniques are preferred during the rhinoplasty or rhinoplasty procedure depending on the nature of the aesthetic issue with the nose, the characteristics of the nose's tissue, the patient's expectations from the surgery, and whether or not discomforts like sinusitis, intranasal curvatures, or intranasal flesh growths that are intended to be addressed during the same surgery can be achieved. Aesthetic nose surgeries are performed under general anaesthesia except for special cases, and the duration of the operation takes between 1.5-2.5 hours depending on the content of the intervention and the technique to be used. Individuals who have already undergone nose surgery may require a lengthier procedure time, as may patients in whom the sinuses must be cut through during the procedure or in whom it is necessary to remove cartilage from the ribs or ear. The fundamental idea behind rhinoplasty is to separate the bone and cartilage structures of the nose from the nasal skin and mucous membrane that line it, either entirely through the nose or through incisions made from the skin section between the two nostrils. This is done by applying various techniques and technologies to the nose's bone and cartilage structures. After the shape has been determined, the skin and mucosa are repositioned. Except in rare circumstances, no direct manipulation of the skin covering the nose occurs during cosmetic nose procedures.

What are the procedures to be done before nose surgery?

Before the rhinoplasty surgery, be sure to share with your doctor all the medications you are using and your habits such as smoking and alcohol use. In particular, drugs with blood-thinning effects should be discontinued one week before the operation, while some other drugs may need to be used without interruption, including on the day of surgery. Apart from this, an anesthesiologist evaluation and blood tests will be performed to control bleeding risk and some infections before the surgery. If you have a disease such as high blood pressure, heart rhythm disorder, or diabetes, it will be necessary to make a preliminary assessment of the surgery by the relevant speciality and to get recommendations, if any.

How long does rhinoplasty take approximately?

Rhinoplasty can take between 1.5 and 4 hours depending on the content of the problem in the nose, the surgical technique to be preferred, whether the patient has had surgery before and whether there is a need for cartilage removal from the ear or ribs during the surgery.

Is rhinoplasty surgery painful?

The factor that causes pain in nose surgeries is the large tampons placed in the nose after the surgery. Nowadays, since thin silicone sheets are used instead of these tampons, and in some cases, only sutures are placed without even silicone, there is usually no significant pain complaint after the surgery.

When are the silicone sheets and plaster placed in the nose removed in nose surgery?

While the silicone sheets inside the nose are removed on the 2nd or 3rd day, the thermoplastic plaster outside the nose stays for 7-10 days. Since the silicone sheets do not stick into the nose, pain does not occur during removal.

How many days do I need to stay in the hospital after nose surgery?

It is possible for patients to rest for a few hours after the surgery and go home, but since it is much easier in the hospital environment to intervene in conditions such as bleeding or anaesthesia-related blood pressure drop and nausea, it is preferable for patients to stay in the hospital on the day of surgery and be discharged the next day.

When can one return to social life after nose surgery?

After the thermoplastic plaster is placed on the nose after the surgery is removed (7-10 days), it is possible to return to social life.

What method is rhinoplasty surgery performed with? Open or Closed?

Depending on the patient's nose structure, the content of the problem and the doctor's preference, the operation can be performed with both open and closed techniques.

What is the best season for rhinoplasty surgery?

Since rhinoplasty is an operation that can usually be performed in 4 seasons, the appropriate period for surgery is the period when patients can stay away from school or work for 10-12 days for this surgery.
