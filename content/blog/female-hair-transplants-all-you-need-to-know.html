What is female hair transplant?

Female hair transplantation is a widely performed procedure designed to increase hair density or cover balding areas by transferring healthy hair follicles to regions affected by hair loss. Contrary to common belief, hair transplantation is not only for middle-aged men; it is also frequently preferred by women experiencing hair thinning, female-pattern baldness, or even male-pattern baldness due to genetic or hormonal factors.

The main goal of Female Hair Transplants: All You Need To Know is to eliminate unwanted thinning and provide patients with a natural-looking result both immediately after the procedure and over time. With the use of advanced technologies and high-tech medical devices, achieving the desired look in female hair transplantation has become easier and more effective than ever.

In this procedure, both doctors and patients share the same objective: restoring confidence, achieving fuller and natural-looking hair, and improving overall satisfaction with one’s appearance. Although the process may seem simple due to modern techniques and experienced medical teams, female hair transplantation is a transformative treatment that offers profound emotional and physical benefits for many women.

Female Hair Transplantation has been successfully applied for many years and many parameters should be investigated before the surgery such as optimal country, correct doctor, and clinic selection.

Which tecniques used in female hair transplant?

Follicular unit extraction (FUE) is frequently used for hair transplantation in women, which is also the most commonly used technique in male hair transplantation procedures. The difference between this technique and another technique, follicular unit transplantation, is that the scar left on the donor area after the procedure is too small to be visible. The area where the hair density is high and the hair follicles are healthy is called the donor area. In the follicular unit extraction process, hair grafts containing hair follicles in groups of 2 or 3 are transplanted from the donor area Micro channels are the area where the hair grafts will be placed in the target area, which is decided together by the patient and the doctor in advance and is usually marked by drawing with a pencil.During the procedure, the surgeon places healthy hair grafts from the donor area with a micropunch into the micro channels opened in the target area. Here, the surgeon can transfer one hair follicle to each micro channel or can transplant multiple follicles. Here, the skill of the surgeon and the team who will perform the hair transplantation, the technology used and the quality of the patient's hair follicles cooperate to achieve good results. The most critical point of the procedure is choosing the right physician and analyzing the expectations and results well.

Is there any pain during and after the procedure?

Just before the procedure, local anesthesia is applied to the patient and the area where hair transplantation will be performed is completely anesthetized. In this way, the patient does not suffer any pain. In some exceptional cases, general anesthesia may be applied to the patient if there is a special request from the patient, but this is a very rare situation. During the procedure, the patient is awake and pain-free with local anesthesia. Regular use of the medications given afterwards plays an important role against the risk of possible infection and bacteria.

How long does female hair transplantation takes?

Female hair transplantation is a one-day procedure and takes an average of 6-8 hours. This period may vary depending on the patient's hair density and the number of hair follicles to be transferred. Patients can go home or to their accommodation immediately after the procedure.
