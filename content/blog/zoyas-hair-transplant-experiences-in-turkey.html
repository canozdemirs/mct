Recovery Process and Gradual Hair Growth

The first week after my hair transplant in Turkey required careful adherence to aftercare instructions. Sleeping in an elevated position and avoiding direct contact with the transplanted area were essential steps. The clinic performed the first wash and demonstrated how to gently clean my scalp without disturbing the grafts. Mild swelling and small scabs developed during the initial days, which gradually subsided. By the end of the first week, most visible signs of the procedure had diminished. I was pleasantly surprised by how manageable the hair transplant recovery process was. Around week three, I experienced temporary shedding known as shock loss. Because the team had thoroughly explained this phase beforehand, I remained calm. Understanding that this stage is part of the natural hair transplant growth cycle helped me stay patient. New hair growth began around the fourth month. Fine strands gradually filled in the previously thinning areas. By month six, I noticed a significant improvement in density. Styling my hair became easier, and I no longer relied on camouflage products. By month nine to twelve, the full hair transplant results became visible thicker, fuller, and completely natural looking. The gradual transformation reinforced my confidence in choosing a hair transplant in Turkey. Each month brought visible progress, confirming that patience truly pays off in the hair restoration journey.
