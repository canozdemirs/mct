How to Determine the Most Appropriate Nose Shape for Your Face Type?

In general, thousands of nose and face types that are considered aesthetically beautiful in men and women were examined and some angles, dimensions and proportions in both the nose and face were determined. Some of those;
  • Nose length,
  • Nose tip projection (height),
  • The ratio between nasal ridge and nasal tip projection,
  • forehead-nose angle,
  • Nose-lip angle,
  • The width of the base of the nose,
  • The size and shape of the nostrils.
When viewed from the front, profile and diagonally, an aesthetic nose image is obtained when these proportions and dimensions, which are aesthetically important, are adhered to. We would like to address an issue that is well known to plastic surgeons but not sufficiently understood by patients. Namely; Not every desired shape can be given to every nose type. For example, it is not possible to make a nose with thick and oily skin too small and give fine angular details. Again, shrinking the nose as much as possible does not give a more aesthetic result, it may be necessary to make a part of the nose more prominent while reducing a part of the nose to ensure harmony in the nose. For example, while the nose belt is being filed, the height of the nose tip is increased, thus achieving an aesthetic ratio between the nose length and the tip of the nose. Or, a flatter nasal ridge can be obtained by filling a very sunken nasal root. Determining which of these possible changes is more suitable for that person requires serious experience and aesthetic foresight. Another very important issue in rhinoplasty is that there are some gender-specific differences in male and female noses, and these details should be paid attention to. For example, giving a man a curved and overturned nose will weaken the masculine features of the face and give the face a feminine appearance. On the other hand, there is no one-size-fits-all nose shape that fits every man's or woman's face. A small, curved and upturned nose will not look natural on a woman's face with strong cheekbones and chin edges and prominent facial angles. In such a face type, the nasal ridge is flatter and the nose angle is not very upturned, a strong nose will be more compatible with the face. While one foot is rhinoplasty to achieve aesthetic harmony between the nose and face, the other foot is aesthetic touches to other parts of the face. For example, in a patient with a flat forehead, dim cheekbones, a small lower jaw tip (bird face, bird face deformity), make a nose as beautiful as you want, and the result will not be perfect. It is not right to try to reduce the size of the nose too much in this type of face. Instead, it is more accurate to make the cheekbones and lower jaw tip prominent enough. In this way, while the nose is reduced a little, the surrounding structures are also enlarged a little and the required aesthetic harmony is achieved. Clarifying the cheekbones will make the nose appear smaller. In a face type where the midface is back and sunken, the lower jaw is in front and there is a closing disorder between the teeth, before the rhinoplasty, the incompatibility between the lower and upper jaw should be eliminated by using orthognathic surgical methods, and then the new nose should be shaped on this basis. When talking about the nose shape suitable for a face where the lower jaw corners and chewing muscles are very prominent and the chin tip is relatively small, it should be taken into consideration that not only the nose but also the chin corners can be filed and a more oval and proportional face can be obtained by moving the chin tip forward. Different face types according to the height of the upper, middle and lower parts of the face, width of the forehead, width of the face on the line passing through the cheekbones, protrusion of the cheekbones, whether the corners of the lower jaw are prominent or not, the shape of the lower jaw line and whether the lower jaw tip is large or small when the face is viewed from the opposite side. we encounter. Some of the most common face types in general are as follows;
  • Diamond  face
  • Oval face
  • Round face
  • Heart shaped face
  • Square face
  • Rectangular face

Which Nose Shape Is Suitable For Diamond Face Type in Turkey?

In the lozenge-shaped face type, the widest part of the face in the horizontal plane is the plane passing through the cheekbones in Turkey. While the forehead and chin corners are narrower, the chin tip is prominent. The forehead is curved, the cheekbones, jawline and chin tip are prominent, and the transitions are more angled. This face type, which is proportional in both the horizontal and vertical planes, is one of the most aesthetic face types in Turkey. In this face type, the nasal tip projection should be prominent and the nasal ridge should be relatively flat. Such a nose exhibits a more harmonious image with the prominent cheekbones and chin tip of the face. The lip-nose angle should not be too wide, that is, it should not be too upturned. The transitions between the nose sections should also be more angled, as in the jaw line.

Which Nose Is Suitable For Oval Face Type?

The chin line is more rounded, the chin corners are not very angled. The upper half of the face is more prominent than the lower half. In the horizontal plane, the widest part of the face is the plane that passes through the cheekbones. This face type adds a feminine feature to the face. The lines are not angular and sharp, the transitions are smoother. The faces of many women who are considered beautiful fit the oval face type. In this face type, the nose lines should be more rounded and smoother in accordance with the jaw line, sharp lines should be avoided.

Which Nose Is Suitable For Round Face Type?

The width of the face is equal to or close to the length. The jaw line is more rounded, there are no sharp angled transitions. The lower and upper halves of the face are also equal. In this face type, the nose lines can also be more curved and smoother. On the other hand, in such a face, it can be considered to increase the height of the chin tip with an implant or fat injection, to reduce the fullness of the cheeks with bichectomy or liposuction, to highlight the cheekbones and to make the face look more oval than round. In this way, it is possible to have a more aesthetic face than can only be obtained with natural nose job

Which Nose Is Suitable for Heart or Inverted Triangle Face Type?

In this face type, the widest part of the face is the plane passing through the cheekbones, the jaw line shows a sudden narrowing towards the chin tip, the chin tip is small and narrow. A nose that is not small enough on such a face will accentuate the already prominent middle face. In addition to a small nose, if the edges of the chin and jawline are made fuller and oval with fat injection, a more balanced and overall more aesthetic face is obtained with the middle face.

Which Nose Is Suitable For Square Face Type?

In this face type, the width of the face is close to the length, and unlike the round face, the corners of the chin and the transitions at the tip of the chin are more sharply angled. The distance between the two jaws is equal to the distance through the cheekbones and forehead planes. Since the facial features of such a face are straight and angled, the nose lines should be more straight and sharply angled accordingly. Since the length of the face is not longer than the width, the length of the nose should also be kept short. In this face type, a face with longer and softer lines can be obtained by making the cheekbones more prominent with fat injection, filing the corners of the chin, injecting fat into the jaw line and lengthening the chin tip. When these procedures are performed, a nose with softer lines can be made.

Which Nose Is Suitable For Rectangular Face Type?

In the rectangular face type, the distances between the forehead, cheekbones and chin corners are equal to each other, similar to the square face, but unlike the square face, the length of the face is longer than the width. In this face type, the jaw line shows a clear angulation and gives the face a hard look. In this face type, the nose length should be parallel to the mid face height. A short and small nose will not look natural on such a face. Since the jaw line and the entire face contour are angular, the angles between the nose structures should be more pronounced and not too soft. On the other hand, filing the corners of the chin will give the face a more oval appearance.

Which Nose Is Suitable For Long Face Type in Turkey?

In this face type, the height of the face is significantly higher than the width of the face. The cheekbones are not very prominent and wide, whereas the face is long in the upper, middle and lower 1/3 parts. On such a face, the length of the nose should also be long in proportion to the face. A small, curved and upturned nose will not look natural on a long face. On the other hand, it is possible to increase the width of the face and bring the aspect ratio to a more natural size by highlighting the cheekbones with fat filling. On the faces where the chin tip is very prominent and long, reducing the chin tip will provide a more proportional and aesthetic appearance in Turkey. Hokka Nose – Which Face Type Is Curved Nose Suitable For?

In the past years, in our country, when aesthetic rhinoplasty has just been introduced, the image resulting from making the ridge of the nose more curved than necessary due to incorrectly used surgical techniques, lifting the tip of the nose more than necessary, and shortening the length of the nose has been incorrectly defined as an inkwell nose. While the noses made in this way do not show any harmony with the other parts of the face, serious respiratory problems are also encountered due to the collapse of the middle part of the nose.   In the real inkwell nose or curved nose concept, the nose back is 2 mm behind the imaginary line between the nose root and the tip of the nose. The ridge of the nose, including the tip of the nose, is not in the form of a completely straight line, there is an angulation between the ridge of the nose and the tip of the nose. The tip of the nose is not wide and fleshy, but small and kind. In addition, the lip-nose angle is also a few degrees wider than normal, that is, the nose is more upturned, but still within normal limits. If we use the term inkwell nose for the criteria I have described, we can say that the inkwell nose gives a very feminine and soft expression to the face. We can say that this nose type is more suitable for oval or round face types, especially thin-skinned, chin and facial lines are not sharp and angled.
