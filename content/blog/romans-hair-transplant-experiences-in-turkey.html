Post Procedure Care and Continuous Support

After the procedure, I received detailed and easy to follow aftercare instructions. The medical team carefully explained how to wash my hair, protect the grafts, and manage daily activities during the recovery period. This guidance made the healing process feel much more manageable. I was also provided with a complete aftercare kit, including medications and special products designed to support healing and hair growth. Having everything prepared in advance helped me follow the recovery routine correctly and with confidence. One of the most reassuring aspects of my experience was the continuous support after leaving the clinic. The Medical Center team stayed in regular contact with me, checking on my progress and responding quickly whenever I had questions or concerns. Whether it was about redness, scabbing, or temporary shedding, I always received clear and helpful guidance. This ongoing follow up made me feel supported throughout the entire recovery journey and showed how much importance was placed on patient satisfaction and long term results.

Recovery, Results, and My Experience Living in Istanbul

The days following my hair transplant were an important part of the overall experience, and Medical Center managed this phase with the same level of care as the procedure itself. Knowing exactly what to do helped me feel confident and relaxed during recovery. In the first few days, there was mild swelling and redness, which I had been prepared for. The clinic staff stayed in touch, checked my progress, and responded quickly whenever I had questions. Spending my recovery time in Istanbul was an unexpected highlight. Choosing to have my hair transplant in Turkey allowed me to combine medical care with a memorable travel experience. Looking back, this journey was not just about hair restoration, but also about feeling supported, understood, and cared for. My hair transplant experience in Turkey, especially with Medical Center, exceeded my expectations and left me feeling truly satisfied with my decision.
