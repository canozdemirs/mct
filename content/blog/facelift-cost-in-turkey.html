1. What is Facelift Surgery (Rhytidectomy)?

Facelift surgery, or rhytidectomy, is a cosmetic surgical procedure designed to reduce the visible signs of aging on the face and neck. This surgery aims to improve the appearance by removing excess skin, tightening underlying tissues and muscles, and redraping the skin on the face and neck. Facelift surgery can also be performed simultaneously with rhinoplasty, eyelid aesthetics, and filler applications on the needs of patients and the recommendations of plastic surgeons. Many different surgical techniques can be applied during facelift operations performed under general anesthesia. Mini Facelift (Lower Facelift)

It is a surgical operation that lasts around 2 hours and is performed under general anesthesia. The incision for a small facelift starts at the front of the ear and extends behind the ear lobe. Mini facelift surgery in Turkey is used to treat sagging skin around the jawline and cheeks, and is also known as a lower facelift.

Mid-facelift surgery:

It's also referred to as a cheek lift. Mid-facial lift surgery is used to pull the cheeks back for a more youthful appearance and to produce a more defined face. To elevate the cheeks, incisions are performed in the eyelids.

Brow lift surgery:

It's also referred to as a forehead lift. Brow lift surgery is used to reduce wrinkles, improve frown lines, and lift sagging brows into a more youthful position. Above the brow, at the hairline, incisions are made.

Temple lift:

It is a process that is used to remove wrinkles from the forehead, and lines between the brows, and completely raise drooping brows and forehead. Within the hair above the ears, a bilateral temporal incision is created.

Neck lift surgery:

Lower rhytidectomy is another name for it. It is a technique that is used to enhance the appearance of aging in the jawline and neck. Incisions are made in the hairline at the level of the sideburns, then around the ear and into the posterior hair.

Full facelift surgery and Cost:

A facelift is a surgical procedure that typically lasts 3 to 4 hours and is performed under general anesthesia. In a complete facelift, the incision usually begins at the temples, follows the hairline, and extends behind the earlobes, allowing the surgeon to address not only the lower face but also the upper facial areas, particularly the temples. Unlike a mini facelift, this approach provides more comprehensive facial rejuvenation. The most suitable type of facelift depends on the patient’s facial structure, aging concerns, and the surgeon’s professional evaluation. Facelift procedures are generally performed using either a traditional facelift, where incisions vary based on the surgical plan, or an endoscopic facelift, which utilizes small surgical cameras and minimally invasive techniques.

3. Who Can Have a Facelift (Rhytidectomy)?

There is no specific age restriction for facial aesthetics applications. However, if there is no medical necessity, it is necessary to be over the age of 18 to benefit from aesthetic practices. Especially in men and women who have passed the age of 40, sagging and various deformations in the face area begin to appear depending on age. Although the changes that occur on the skin are related to age, some external factors cause these changes to become more pronounced. Smoking and alcohol use, not taking care of facial cleansing, irregular diet, air pollution, not consuming enough water, excessive sunlight exposure, and excessive stress are important factors in the formation of early and obvious sagging and lines on the skin. To reverse this situation and delay the signs of aging, facial aesthetics operations are used. Facial aesthetics is applied to specific areas as well as sagging and lines seen on the skin. Men and women from all age groups over the age of 18 may have facial aesthetics for different areas such as the nose, forehead, and eye area where they feel uncomfortable.

5. What are the Results of a Facelift (Rhytidectomy)?

The results of a facelift, or rhytidectomy, generally include a more youthful and rejuvenated appearance. Specific outcomes can vary based on the extent of the procedure, the skill of the surgeon, and individual patient factors, but typically include:

Improved Facial Contours

  • Lifted Cheeks: The cheeks are elevated to a more youthful position.
  • Defined Jawline: Reduction of jowls and a firmer jawline.
  • Smoothed Neck: Removal of sagging skin and excess fat under the chin and neck, leading to a smoother neck contour.

Reduction of Wrinkles and Sagging Skin

  • Minimized Wrinkles: Significant reduction in deep wrinkles and creases around the nose, mouth, and chin.
  • Tightened Skin: Tighter, firmer skin on the face and neck, reducing the appearance of sagging.

Enhanced Overall Appearance

  • Youthful Look: An overall younger and fresher appearance.
  • Natural Results: Modern techniques aim to achieve natural-looking results without a "pulled" or overly tight appearance.

Psychological Benefits

  • Increased Confidence: Improved self-esteem and confidence due to a more youthful and refreshed appearance.
  • Positive Self-Image: Enhanced satisfaction with one's appearance can lead to a more positive self-image.

Longevity of Results

  • Long-Lasting Effects: While a facelift does not stop the aging process, the results can last many years. Factors such as genetics, lifestyle, and skin care can influence the longevity of the results.

Maintenance and Post-Surgery Care

  • Ongoing Skincare: To maintain results, patients are often advised to follow a good skincare routine and protect their skin from sun damage.
  • Healthy Lifestyle: Maintaining a healthy lifestyle, including a balanced diet and regular exercise, can help prolong the benefits of the surgery.

Considerations and Realistic Expectations

  • Subtle Improvements: Some patients may achieve subtle rather than dramatic changes, especially if opting for less extensive procedures.
  • Individual Variation: Results vary from person to person based on factors like skin type, age, and the extent of the aging process.
Overall, the goal of a facelift is to create a more youthful and rested appearance, enhancing the patient's natural beauty and boosting their self-confidence.
