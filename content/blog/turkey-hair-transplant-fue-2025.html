What is FUE hair transplant?

FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) is a modern and minimally invasive hair transplant technique that involves extracting individual hair follicles from a donor area—usually the back or sides of the scalp—and implanting them into areas affected by hair loss or thinning. Unlike traditional methods that involve removing a strip of scalp (such as FUT), FUE leaves no linear scar and allows for a quicker recovery. Each follicle is carefully harvested using a micro-punch tool and placed with precision to ensure a natural-looking hairline and density. This technique is highly popular in Turkey due to its effectiveness, natural results, and the availability of experienced surgeons offering high-quality procedures at affordable prices. How does the FUE hair transplant procedure work?

The FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) hair transplant procedure is performed in several precise steps to ensure natural and long-lasting results:
  • Consultation and Planning:
The process begins with a detailed consultation, where the surgeon examines your scalp, discusses your goals, and designs a suitable hairline. The number of grafts needed is also determined.
  • Preparation and Anesthesia:
On the day of the procedure, the donor and recipient areas are shaved and cleaned. Local anesthesia is applied to ensure a completely pain-free experience.
  • Follicle Extraction:
Using a micro-punch tool (usually 0.6–1.0 mm in diameter), individual hair follicles are gently extracted from the donor area—typically the back or sides of the head.
  • Graft Preservation:
The extracted follicles are carefully stored in a special solution to maintain their vitality until implantation.
  • Recipient Site Creation:
Small incisions are made in the recipient area according to the natural direction, angle, and density of your hair. This is key to achieving a natural look.
  • Implantation:
The hair follicles are then implanted one by one into the prepared sites. This step requires high precision to ensure even distribution and natural appearance.
  • Post-Procedure Care:
After the procedure, you'll receive detailed aftercare instructions and medications to support healing and graft survival. Most patients can return to normal activities within a few days.
