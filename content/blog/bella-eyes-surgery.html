What is the recovery process like after Bella Eyes Surgery?

The recovery process after Bella Eyes Surgery is usually smooth and comfortable when post-operative instructions are followed carefully. This procedure is designed to enhance the appearance of the eyes while allowing a relatively fast healing period. Most patients are pleasantly surprised by how manageable the recovery phase is.

Immediately after Bella Eyes Surgery, mild swelling and light bruising around the eyes are common. These effects are temporary and usually decrease within the first few days.
