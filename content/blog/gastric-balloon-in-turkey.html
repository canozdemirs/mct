How Are Balloons Use?

The balloon is inserted into the stomach endoscopically. This procedure takes about 20 to 25 minutes. First, a diagnostic endoscopy is done to make sure you don't have a medical condition that could prevent balloon placement (such as an ulcer). If no abnormalities are observed, a soft, deflated balloon made of a silicone elastomer-based material is placed on the abdomen. Once the balloon is placed in your abdomen, it is filled with blue fluid through an inflation tube (catheter) attached to the balloon, and the procedure is complete. The balloon begins to float freely in the stomach. The procedure is performed under sedation to ensure patient comfort and the patient wakes up immediately after the procedure and is discharged from the hospital after a 2 hour observation period in the ward.
