What is Neck Lift?

A neck lift, also called cervicoplasty or platysmaplasty, is a cosmetic surgery designed to enhance the appearance of the neck and jawline. It is ideal for individuals with sagging skin or excess fat in the neck area. After undergoing a neck lift in Turkey, patients can achieve a smoother, more youthful neck with a well-defined jawline. Thanks to experienced surgeons in Turkey, you can have this procedure performed safely to attain a beautiful, youthful, and naturally contoured neck.
  • Reduction of Excess Skin: The surgery removes sagging or excess skin from the neck, which can develop due to aging or weight loss.
 
  • Tightening of Neck Muscles: The platysma muscles, which can separate and form vertical bands over time, are tightened or repositioned to restore a smoother, more defined contour to the neck.
 
  • Removal of Excess Fat: Liposuction is sometimes used in conjunction with the neck lift to remove excess fat under the chin and around the neck, further enhancing the neck's shape.
 
  • Improved Jawline Definition: By lifting the neck and jawline area, the procedure helps to restore or enhance the contour of the lower face, improving the overall profile.

Procedure Overview

  • Anesthesia: The surgery is typically done under general anesthesia or local anesthesia with sedation.
  • Incision: Small incisions are made either under the chin or behind the ears. The surgeon then tightens the skin and muscles and removes any excess fat.
  • Recovery: Recovery can take several weeks, with swelling and bruising subsiding within the first few days. Results gradually improve over time.
A neck lift is often combined with other facial procedures, such as a facelift, to provide comprehensive rejuvenation of the lower face and neck.

What to Expect After the Neck Lift Surgery in Turkey?

After undergoing neck lift surgery in Turkey, patients can look forward to a structured recovery process designed to ensure safety and optimal results. Below is an overview of what to expect during each phase of recovery:

Immediate Post-Surgery (First Few Hours to Days)

  • Pain & Discomfort: It’s normal to experience some swelling, bruising, tightness, and discomfort in the neck area. These symptoms are typically managed with prescribed pain relievers and cold compresses.
  • Dressings & Drains: Bandages or compression garments may be applied to reduce swelling and support the neck. If drains are used to remove excess fluid, they’ll be removed during a follow-up visit.
  • Monitoring: You’ll spend the initial hours in a recovery room under close observation to ensure there are no complications, such as infection or excessive bleeding.

The First Week

  • Swelling & Bruising: Swelling may peak around the second or third day but will gradually subside.
  • Rest & Care: Rest is crucial during this time. Keeping your head elevated, even while sleeping, can help minimize swelling.
  • Follow-up Appointment: You’ll likely have your first follow-up within a few days after surgery. Your surgeon will assess your healing, remove any stitches if necessary, and provide detailed at-home care instructions.

The First Month

  • Noticeable Improvements: As initial swelling and bruising fade, you’ll start to see enhanced neck and jawline contours. Some residual swelling may persist for a few weeks.
  • Light Activities: Walking and other light activities can be resumed, but avoid strenuous exercise, bending your head down, or heavy lifting.
  • Scar Healing: Any surgical incisions will begin to heal. Initially, scars may appear red or raised, but with proper care (e.g., scar creams, silicone sheets), they will gradually fade and flatten.

Long-Term Recovery (Up to 6 Months)

  • Full Recovery: Swelling and tightness should completely resolve within 3 to 6 months, revealing the final results of your surgery. The neck and jawline will look smoother and more youthful.
  • Longevity of Results: While the results are long-lasting, maintaining a healthy lifestyle and protecting your skin from sun exposure can help prolong the effects.
  • Follow-up Care: Regular check-ups with your surgeon ensure your recovery stays on track and address any concerns you may have.
With proper care and adherence to your surgeon’s guidance, you can enjoy a rejuvenated, youthful neck appearance that boosts your confidence and enhances your overall look.  

Aftercare Considerations

  • Sun Protection: Protecting your neck from sun exposure is crucial to prevent hyperpigmentation or scarring.
  • Avoiding Smoking: Smoking can impair healing, so it’s advisable to refrain from smoking during the recovery period.
  • Diet & Hydration: Eating nutritious foods and staying hydrated supports the healing process.
Potential Risks and Complications Though neck lift surgery in Turkey is typically safe when performed by a qualified surgeon, there are risks, as with any surgery, including:
  • Infection
  • Hematoma (blood collection under the skin)
  • Nerve damage (leading to temporary or permanent numbness)
  • Scarring
  • Asymmetry
