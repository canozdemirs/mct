What is a breast implant replacement ?

A breast implant is a prothesis that is surgically inserted into the breast. This procedure aims to replace old breast implants with new ones. Breast implant replacement can be done due to an occurring problem or to further change the size or shape of the breasts. As a result, breasts can be lifted or reduced, reshaped, increased, or decreased in size. One popular question is how often breast implants should be replaced. By reading further, you can understand the topic in detail.

What are the types of breast implants?

There are two types of breast implants which are saline and silicone gel filled implants. Both have an outer silicone shell. If a silicone shell is filled with sterile saline, which is salt water, then it is called a saline-filled implant. The shell can be filled with saline either pre-operation or during operation. If the shell is filled with silicone gel, then it is called silicone gel implant. Silicone gel-filled implants can cause more risk in the case of a leak.

In order to increase the life of the breast implants, you need to follow some rules. First of all, rest and recover well after the surgery. If you exercise, wear supportive undergarments. What are the types of breast implant incisions?

There are three possible incision places on the body.
  • The crease under the breast (inframammary)
  • Under the arm (axillary)
  • Around the nipple (periareolar)
Incision type will determine how the scar will look like. The most common incision type is the inframammary incision which is the crease under the breast. It is usually not visible and can be covered easily by a bra. This is commonly preferred among women who want to breastfeed because this incision offers the fewest complication. The axillary incision is done in the armpit. The doctor makes a tunnel through the fat and inserts the implant there. It is only visible if sleeveless clothes are worn. The last one is periareolar. The periareolar incision is done around the nipple. The scar is hidden very well. It can lead to a decrease in nipple sensation very rarely.

How often do breast implants need to be replaced?

A typical breast implant can, on average, be used for 10 to 15 years. If the patient has no problem, there is no need for breast implant replacement.  If you want to know reasons why a breast implant should be replaced, please keep reading.

Why a breast implant should be replaced?

How often do breast implants need to be replaced? A breast implant can not be used forever. Ruptures can happen over time which may affect the person's health. The two main reasons a breast implant should be replaced are breast implant rupture and capsular contracture. Mammograms, physical traumas, or even time can cause breast implant ruptures. Breast implants get old, and they can crack over time. Although both saline and silicone breast implants have a tough outer shell, both can rupture. If the saline implant ruptures, the saline inside will leak, and it will be absorbed by the body. When it comes to silicone implants, things change. For the older generation silicone implants, silicone gel filling is very thin so that it can get into the lymph nodes. On the other hand, newer-generation silicone implants have a very thick gel inside. Thus, even if the shell cracks, silicone gel will stay within the implant. Moreover, capsular contracture may also lead to implant replacement. Because a breast implant is a foreign structure for your body, it forms scar tissue around it as a natural body response. If this scar tissue becomes too thick and causes pain, then this incidence is called capsular contracture. There are several causes for capsular contracture, but one of the most popular modern views is that it is caused by a "biofilm". It is thought that the layer of bacteria grows around the implant and causes discomfort and inflammation. A capsular contracture typically necessitates the removal of the implant. However, in more than a decade and hundreds of breast surgeries, less than 1% of patients have experienced this. Capsular contracture can be treated by removing the implant and clearing the scar tissue around it. Other things like breast implant-related illness, implant displacement, and implant malfunction can also lead to breast implant replacement.

What are the signs of breast implant replacement?

There are some signs showing that you need to replace your breast implants. Ruptured implant, defleated implant, asymmetry of the breasts, firmness, tenderness, change in position of the implant, abnormal breast shape, pain, and discomfort can be the signs of breast implant replacement. Also, a rare case called breast implant illness can happen among patients. Symptoms of breast implant illness can be chronic fatigue, headaches, muscle aches, hair loss, shortness of breath, and anxiety. Although this case is rare, if you are experiencing any of the symptoms, please get advice from your doctor to avoid further problems.

What happens if a breast implant is not replaced when needed?

As mentioned above, if hard breasts or capsular contracture forms, the thick scar capsule can be treated with or without implant replacement. If a rupture happens in a saline implant, it is recommended to replace them within one or two months. If not replaced, further capsular deformity will occur, which eventually will become visible. A rupture may not affect the breast shape or size of silicone implants. It may just make breasts softer. Because it can not be detected with the naked eye or physical examination, an ultrasound or MRI should be done every five to seven years to avoid ‘’silent ruptures’’. There are no scientifically known harms to the body from silicone gel leakage, but it is recommended to replace the broken implant as a precaution.

How does breast implant replacement surgery happen in Turkey?

Breast implant replacement surgery is becoming highly popular in Turkey. After the patient is examined by a doctor and potential risks are discussed, the process starts. Although it will depend on the patient's wishes, medical history, and doctors' advice, generally silicone gel-filled implants are preferred in Turkey. Saline-filled implants are not preferred because silicone gel-filled implants give a more natural look and feel; it will cause less capsular contracture, and rupture occurrence is lower. The surgery happens as follows: The older scar can be used to reach the implant pocket. The implant will be removed. If there is a rupture, the pocket will be cleaned. If there is a capsular contracture, the tissue will be removed partly or entirely. Sometimes pocket can be reshaped or newly prepared. Then, after all these adjustments, the new implant will be placed. The surgery is performed under general anesthesia, and it can last from 30 to 150 minutes.

How to prepare for the surgery?

Before getting into the surgery, the patient should be ready mentally. It is not just a physical journey. Also, the surgeon should be researched beforehand. Not every doctor can be trusted. To prepare for the surgery, you should regularly exercise, have a good diet and stick to that after the surgery too. After the surgery, caring for children or pets will be hard due to physical pain. You can arrange someone to look after them, if you have any. In addition, if you have a smoking habit try to quit because it delays the healing process and may lead to complications.

How does recovery after surgery happen?

Depending on the treatment type and the patient's unique healing process, recovery following breast implant replacement surgery can vary. Patients should often anticipate experiencing tightness, edema, and pain in the chest area for a few weeks. Antibiotics and painkillers may be provided based on the patient's particular circumstances to assist in managing discomfort. Patients could also be instructed to wear a specific bra to help their healing process. They will be told to take it easy for a few days, avoid lifting, and avoid sitting up straight as much as possible. They should be able to move around but be prepared to have some soreness for a while. They should request assistance with cleaning, cooking, and other everyday tasks. Bruising and swelling will start to settle after about three weeks. It will take time for the breasts to settle as they will be swollen initially, and it could be up to six weeks before the new shape appears. Patients should carefully monitor their implants and general health beyond the initial recovery time. A follow up with their doctor is necessary. Patients can anticipate a successful recovery from breast implant replacement surgery with the appropriate care and attention.

What are the risks and potential complications of breast implant replacement surgery?

Breast implant replacement entails removing and replacing old implants with new ones. There are risks and potential consequences involved with the surgery, even though it can be a safe and effective approach to alter the size and form of breasts. Infection, scarring, implant rupture, and alterations in feeling are a few of these. Even while breast implant replacement issues are uncommon, there is always a chance of complications. Capsular contracture, a hardening of scar tissue that causes hardness and distortion of the implant, is the most significant risk associated with this operation. Capsular contracture can occur very early in rare circumstances, although it usually happens after the implants have been in place for a while.
