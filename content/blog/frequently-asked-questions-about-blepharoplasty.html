What is eyelid aesthetics (blepharoplasty)?

Plastic surgeries performed on the eyelid are called 'blepharoplasty' in the medical literature. The eyes are one of the most important organs that provide the first contact in communication and give meaning to the face. The skin of the eyelids is very thin, so they are very quickly affected by the negative effects of the environment and time. The signs of aging in the face area first appear with wrinkles around the eyes, which we call crow's feet, and eye bags under the eyes, starting from the age of 30. Over time, this situation manifests as excess skin on the upper eyelids, ie sagging and intense wrinkles, and under-eye bags on the lower eyelids, discoloration of the skin and sagging. This problem, which makes the face look tired, sad and older than it is by distorting the expression, can sometimes reach very advanced dimensions and reduce the angle of view, causing headaches and even migraine complaints. The treatment of this condition, which brings along many health problems other than visual appearance, is quite easy.

Why is blepharoplasty done?

Eyelid surgery is not performed only for aesthetic purposes. Eyelid drooping, which may be congenital or age-related, seriously affects the patient's vision. Eyelid surgeries are necessary for health in order to have better and clear vision. In addition, if the patient has skin tumors on the eyelid, surgical intervention may be required.

How many hours does blepharoplasty take?

Eyelid surgeries usually take one to one and a half hours and most patients can return home the same day without staying in the hospital. In eyelid surgeries, excess skin is removed, sagging is removed and under-eye bags are removed, if any. What is the age limit for blepharoplasty?

Eyelid aesthetics is usually performed after the age of 30 if there is excess skin on the upper eyelid due to genetics or aging. Problems in the lower eyelid; bagging or excess skin usually becomes evident from the age of 40. It is more suitable to be done from this age.

What is the recovery process like after surgery?

 At the end of upper and/or lower eyelid operations, small sterile bands that do not completely cover the eye are used for dressing. These bands are renewed after 3 days and removed on the fifth day. After the operation, it is necessary to apply ice for two days as recommended to reduce swelling, edema and bruises. There may be bleeding in the form of slight leakage on the first day. This is very natural. Pain is almost nonexistent. There will be a comfortable post-operative period. The operation area should not be wet for the first 3 days. You can take a bath after the 3rd day control. Return to social life after upper and lower eyelid surgery takes place in 1 week. Make-up can be applied from the 2nd week.

Is it painfull?

Post-operative pain is almost non-existent. There will be a comfortable post-operative period.

Will there be any scars?

In eyelid surgeries, incisions are made following the natural lines. The scars of the incisions made in the fold of the lid for the upper eyelid and on the lines just below the eyelashes on the lower lid, with the advantage of the thinness of the skin, become obscure in a very short time by approaching the skin color. Will there be bruising or swelling after operation?

After eyelid aesthetic surgery, a small amount of edema and redness may occur around the eyes. On the 2nd and 3rd days, the swelling begins to decrease. In the first week, the stitches and bands are removed and the patient regains a normal appearance.

What is the anastheszia type?

Eyelid aesthetic surgeries are performed in hospital conditions, under the supervision of an anesthesiologist, under local or general anesthesia and in the operating room.

Can patince with contact lensen have this operation?

Patients who have blepharoplasty surgery with the complaint of droopy eyelids can start using lenses 1 week after the operation.

What should be considered after eyelid aesthetics?

Eyelid aesthetics is an application that is meticulously performed in expert hands today. It is important to know the following after the operation: Swelling may occur around the eyes in the days following eyelid surgery, Post-operative drug treatment should not be interrupted, The eye should be protected with drops, For one week following the surgery, the lying position should be adjusted with the head up. Who Can't Have Blepharoplasty?

  • circulation disorders
  • thyroid disorders
