What is the procedure?

To achieve your personalized butt implant goals, you will be given implants in a variety of shapes and sizes. What are the possible risks and complications?

Risks can be minimized by working with a skilled plastic surgeon. However, complications following butt implant surgery are common and can include:
  • Blood buildup under your skin (hematoma)
  • Chronic pain
  • Fluid buildup under your skin (seroma)
  • Infection
  • Permanent scarring
  • Shifting of implants
  • Skin discoloration
  • Uneven appearance
  • Wrong placement of the implants (malposition).
  • Permanent tightening of the muscles, tendons, or tissues around your implant (contracture)
  • The implants might rupture or burst

When the results become visible?

Following butt implant surgery, swelling is typical. As a result, you might not see your complete results for three to six months after surgery, depending on how quickly the swelling goes down. Following butt implant surgery, you'll have some scars. The position of the incisions affects how much scarring there is. Ask your plastic surgeon where they will make the incisions and whether they will be noticeable once you have healed to find out. Do they look natural?

Where your surgeon installs the implants will determine how natural-looking they appear. However, the implant may protrude from the butt muscle. Implants implanted inside the butt muscle lessen the chance of injuring the sciatic nerve. Implants placed beneath your muscle appear more natural, but they also increase the risk of nerve injury.

Are butt implants FDA approved?

Injections for body enhancement and contouring have not received FDA approval. Injections made of silicone and hydrogel are among them. The organization warns that attempts at injections for these kinds of operations might result in dangerous side effects like infection, scarring and disfigurement, stroke, and death. The FDA views any injections into the buttocks, including Sculptra, as off-label.
