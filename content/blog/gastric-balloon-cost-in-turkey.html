What is Obesity and Morbid Obesity?

We can briefly define obesity as excessive fat accumulation within the body. WHO (World Health Organization) defines obesity as “abnormal, excessive accumulation of fat within the form and rate which will cause diseases within the body”. Morbid Obesity is defined as severe obesity that threatens life because of the fatal problems it causes. Those with a Body Mass Index (BMI) above 40 are considered morbidly obese. Body fat percentage is 25-30% in women and -20 in men.

How is the Gastric Balloon Inserted?

The patient, whose gastric balloon will be inserted, must be fasted for 8 hours before this procedure. For example, if the procedure will be done at 09:00 in the morning, the patient can eat something at 01:00 at the latest. If possible, the fasting period can be increased up to 10 hours. There is no other preparation to be done for the gastric balloon insertion procedure. At the time of the procedure, the person goes to the hospital or the clinic where the procedure will be performed on an empty stomach and an intravenous line is opened to the person before the procedure. Various sedatives are given to the patient's body through this intravenous line. Thus, the patient is placed in a semi-asleep position. In some cases, the method of complete sleep can also be used. In the method in which the patient is completely put to sleep, the patient's breathing is not stopped. And it is provided to sleep for a period of about 15 minutes. Whichever method is chosen, this procedure is extremely painless. First of all, an illuminated and small camera called an endoscope is used to place the gastric balloon inside the stomach. Thanks to the image that this camera projects on the monitors outside, the doctor can see the internal structure of the stomach clearly and in detail. And can calculate the fine details of the process. This camera, which is approximately 1 cm thick, is lowered into the patient's stomach through the esophagus. In the meantime, not only the stomach of the patient, but also the esophagus and duodenum are examined. In other words, a detailed endoscopic examination is performed during the procedure. If the patient does not have ulcers, gastritis, reflux or suspicious lesions that may prevent the insertion of the gastric balloon, the decision to inflate the gastric balloon is taken. After this stage, the gastric balloon is slowly lowered into the person's stomach. This balloon is not inflated into the stomach. The inflation process is done while the balloon is in the stomach. To inflate the balloon, a physiological serum with a dye in methylene blue color is used. The reason for using a colored dye is that if there is a leak or a hole in the gastric balloon for any reason, the patient will notice it in his urine or in the large toilet, so that complications can be easily noticed. The gastric balloon inserted into the patient is inflated with air or this blue serum to a volume of 450-500 cc. And so the process ends. This process takes about 12-13 minutes. After the procedure is over, the patient is awakened. You can be discharged from the hospital after a rest period of approximately 1-2 hours. After this procedure, there are no serious side effects. In this way, people can continue their daily lives normally.

How to Remove Gastric Balloon?

It may take 6 months to 1 year for the person to lose weight, follow the diet and gain regular eating habits. In this process, the removal time of the gastric balloon is determined according to the health status of the person, the weight to be lost and the diet to be applied. When this time has elapsed, it is recommended to remove the balloon. Otherwise, the balloon risks bursting when the time is exceeded. This process takes about 15 minutes. The gastric balloon removal procedure performed by Istanbul Obesity Surgery specialists is performed as follows:
  • The endoscopic apparatus used to place the balloon is sent back to the stomach for removal.
  • The liquid in the balloon in the stomach is emptied.
  • The gastric balloon, which is emptied and reduced in volume, is removed by pulling upwards from the stomach with the help of endoscopic pipes.
