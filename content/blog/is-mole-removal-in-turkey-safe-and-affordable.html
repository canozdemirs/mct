What methods are commonly used for mole removal in Turkey?

There are several safe and effective methods commonly used for mole removal in Turkey. The choice of method usually depends on the size, type, depth, and location of the mole, as well as the patient’s skin type and personal preferences. A proper consultation helps determine the most suitable technique. Laser removal is one of the most popular non-surgical methods. It is especially effective for small, flat, and non-cancerous moles. The laser targets the pigment in the mole, breaking it down without damaging the surrounding skin. Healing time is short, and scarring is minimal, making it a preferred option for facial areas. Surgical excision is another widely used method. This method is often recommended for larger or deeper moles, especially if there is any concern about skin cancer. Stitches may be required, and a small scar might remain, but the results are usually clean and permanent.  Shave excision is a technique used for raised moles. In this method, the mole is shaved off the skin surface using a special blade. It’s quick and done under local anesthesia. This technique may leave a flat mark, but it heals well in most cases. Cryotherapy is a less common option, involving the freezing of the mole with liquid nitrogen. It works best for small, non-cancerous moles and usually requires multiple sessions. At Medical Center Turkey, dermatologists choose the most appropriate mole removal method based on a detailed skin analysis, ensuring both safety and patient satisfaction.
