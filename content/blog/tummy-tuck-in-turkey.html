Tummy Tuck, also known as abdominal plastic surgery, is a plastic surgery procedure that aims to flatten the abdomen and tighten the muscles under the abdomen by removing unwanted fat and skin from the middle and bottom. How is Tummy Tuck in Turkey Performed?

Abdominal surgery is a surgical process to reshape the abdominal area of ​​the body by removing excess skin and stretching and suturing the rest. The ideal result of a tummy tuck in Turkey is a flat stomach with tight muscles. Tummy Tuck in Turkey Can Be Described in Two Steps:

Incision

On a full stomach (up to 360 tummy tuck), the incision is usually made from thigh to thigh and can be hidden under underwear through the lower abdomen. A second incision can be made around the belly button to remove unwanted skin and reposition the mid-abdominal area (also known as a tummy tuck belly button). The incisions made in mini tummy tucks are insignificant compared to a full tummy tuck, so do not exceed 180º (less than thigh to thigh).

Remove Excess Fat & Stretch the Skin

After surgery on the incision, excess fat and skin are removed. The muscles that are stretched are then collected and sutured. Finally, the remaining skin is pulled down and your belly button is repositioned.

Two Types of Tummy Tuck

Tummy Tuck (Mini):

A mini tummy tuck is performed to remove sagging skin and fatty tissue in the lower abdomen (below the belly button). Even the mini tummy tuck and tummy tuck do not include diastasis recti (severe abdominal muscles) caused by pregnancy or weight fluctuations. The comparative advantages of a mini tummy tuck include shorter procedure times, faster recovery, and smaller incisions and scars than full tummy tucks.

Tummy Tuck (Full):

A complete abdominoplasty focuses on the abdominal muscles. Such muscles can lose their natural shape due to pregnancy gaps or weight fluctuations. A full abdominoplasty will remove all the abdominal skin, restore abdominal muscles, and usually reposition your belly button.
