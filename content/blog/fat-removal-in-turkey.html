How is Liposuction Surgery Performed?

In liposuction surgery, the fat tissue under the skin is sucked with the help of hollow tubes (cannulas) inserted into the skin through cuts of a few millimeters. The suction is done with a vacuuming device attached to these small tubes. The word “suction” in the name of the process also refers to this vacuuming technique. As with muscle cells, fat cells do not increase in number after puberty, but they gain volume. The aim of liposuction surgery is to reduce the number of fat cells in the relevant area. Liposuction surgery is performed under general or local anesthesia (sedation) in a hospital operating room environment. Depending on the amount of fat to be removed, the duration of the operation varies between half an hour and 6 hours. The incisions at the cannula entrance locations are stitched using self-dissolving sutures. Most of the time, 1 stitch is plenty for each entry point.

Is There Any Pain After Liposuction Surgery?

Post-surgery is usually comfortable. Medication might be used to reduce the slight pain. After the procedure, the patient is clothed in a customized corset and a particular bandage is put on the vacuum-applied regions. After the operation, the patient is dressed. For five to six weeks, the patient wears the corset continuously. There may be purplish colour changes in the vacuum-applied areas. There may be swelling, tingling and burning sensation. These disappear spontaneously within 3 weeks. The scars of the operation also become vague within 2-6 months.

After Liposuction Surgery

Usually, hospitalization is not required after surgery. In more extensive operations, it may be necessary to stay in the hospital for 1 night. In the first 3-4 days after the operation, there will be pain and ache in the operation and suture areas. Oral painkillers are sufficient to relieve these pains. There will be mild to moderate swelling and bruising in the first week. These will disappear over time. It is usually possible to return to work after 3-4 days. After the surgery, you can start walking and jogging two weeks later. For heavier sports, it would be appropriate to wait 6-8 weeks. After the operation, the use of corsets is beneficial and recommended for the adaptation of the skin tissue to the area where the fat is removed and does not sag. Depending on the procedure or technique employed, the period of corset usage varies. Your doctor will give the necessary guidance on how long you should use a corset.

Laser Liposuction:

Laser liposuction is a minimally invasive operation that aims to melt the fat under the skin without the necessity of general anesthesia. The process of melting and removing the fat is carried out by means of an FDA-approved device in which laser beams are used. The advantage of the application, which is performed with a system that directly targets the fat cells, compared to liposuction is that the recovery period is shorter.

Cryolipolysis:

It is aimed to destroy the fat cells under the skin by freezing without surgical intervention by means of the FDA-approved cold lipolysis device. Cryolipolysis is not a surgical operation, unlike liposuction surgery. It is an application that can be applied in sessions as a part of your daily life and you can continue your life after the session. Advantages of cold lipolysis over liposuction:
  • Edema and bruises related to capillaries are not seen.
  • It is not a painful practice.
  • You can continue working during the day.
  • It is a non-invasive application.
  • It does not require general or local anesthesia.
As with liposuction surgery, the fat mass in your body will not decrease immediately after the surgery. Depending on the ongoing sessions and the lubrication in your body, the results are obtained as the sessions continue over time. Fat cells crystallized by cold lipolysis are cleared by the immune system.

Vaser Liposuction:

Vaser liposuction or ultrasonic liposuction method is performed for the purpose of fat loss from the body, similar to liposuction surgery; however, it is an operation that does not require general anesthesia. The process of melting and removing fat is carried out with a device that transmits ultrasonographic sound waves under the skin and with an FDA-approved technology in a way that minimizes surgical intervention. With a system that directly targets fat cells, the recovery time is shorter than in liposuction surgery.
