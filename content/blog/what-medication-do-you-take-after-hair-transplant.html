Things to Avoid After Hair Transplant

  • Always avoid heavy work such as sweating, getting tired or taking a shower after hair transplantation. In addition, sweating damages hair follicles and prevents their healthy growth.
  • Avoid stressful and tense environments. Stress is one of the most important causes of hair loss. Do not place yourself in a stressful environment after transplantation, especially if you have lost your hair due to stress.
  • Never drink alcohol as you will need antibiotics after planting. Since alcohol enters the circulatory system directly from the blood, it is harmful to newly planted hair.
  • Never wash your hair on the first day. On the second day, you have to go to the hospital and have it washed by a specialist. We recommend that you wash your hair only after the 3rd day. It is difficult to get the desired result for hair transplant results if it has been washed beforehand.
  • Care should be taken not to touch or hit the transplanted area.
  • For the first three days, you should abstain from smoking, coffee, tea, and alcohol. Newly planted hair follicles are affected very quickly as they contain drugs and irritants that enter the blood directly.
  • The flocked area should be protected from sun and rain for at least 15 days.
  • You should stay away from hot or cold environments, bathe in cold water instead of hot water, and stay away from Turkish baths, saunas, pools and the sea. Such sites also increase the risk of infection and reduce the success of the surgery.
  • It is obligatory to wash the head that has not been washed for the first 3 days with a shampoo with a pH value of 5.5, antibacterial and chemical content low for 15 days starting from the 3rd day. This type of shampoo kills bacteria and bacteria on the scalp and promotes hair follicle growth.
  • Scars and crusts on the scalp disappear in about a week after hair transplantation. The incisions and scars formed during the surgery are completely removed. You don't have to worry about that.
  • If you feel itchy on your scalp, let your doctor know and ask for medication. Never apply substances such as jelly, spray or brightener to the transplant area.
