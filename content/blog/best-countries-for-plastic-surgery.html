Under what conditions is cosmetic surgery necessary?

Plastic surgery, which focuses on improving the patient's appearance, is defined as changing organ shape and tissue transfer to correct deformities. In general, there is a common belief that many people undergo plastic surgery just to improve their appearance or feel better. It is clear that after plastic surgery operation, patients would achieve the desired results with the transplantation, implementation, or lifting process. However, in some cases, plastic surgery is not only an option but also a strong necessity for patients' overall health. For example, in cases that are trauma-linked or post-accident, damaged or impaired tissue/organ must be intervened with plastic surgery operations. In some cases, especially congenital or post-traumatic ones, tissue tears, and deficiency must be treated with plastic surgery.
