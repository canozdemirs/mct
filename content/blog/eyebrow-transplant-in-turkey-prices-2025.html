What techniques are used for eyebrow transplants in Turkey?

In Turkey, eyebrow transplants are typically performed using two primary techniques: Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) and Direct Hair Implantation (DHI).
  • Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE): This is the most common method, where individual hair follicles are extracted from a donor area (usually the back of the head) using a small punch tool. These follicles are then implanted into the eyebrow area with precision to match the natural hair growth pattern. FUE is highly regarded for its minimal scarring and natural-looking results.
  • Direct Hair Implantation (DHI): This is a more advanced form of FUE, where the follicles are implanted directly into the recipient area without creating incisions beforehand. A specialized pen-like tool places the hair follicles at the correct angle and depth, leading to more precise and dense results. DHI is known for quicker recovery and even more natural results.
Highly skilled surgeons in Turkey often perform these techniques, ensuring that the transplanted hair blends seamlessly with the existing eyebrow hair, providing a fuller, more defined look. Both methods offer long-lasting, permanent results.

How long does it take to see results after an eyebrow transplant?

Following an eyebrow transplant, noticeable results usually appear within 3 to 6 months as the transplanted hair follicles begin to grow in their new location. In the initial phase, it’s normal for the transplanted hairs to shed, which is a typical part of the process. Gradually, new hairs start to grow, with the final outcome generally visible around 9 to 12 months. This timeframe allows the hair to mature and blend naturally, achieving the desired fullness and shape for the eyebrows. Patience is key, as the hair growth process can vary slightly depending on individual factors like hair growth rate, skin type, and the specific technique used. Regular follow-up appointments with the surgeon help ensure optimal results and monitor the healing process.

Are eyebrow transplants permanent?

Yes, eyebrow transplants are generally permanent. The transplanted hair follicles are taken from a donor area (usually the scalp) and are genetically programmed to continue growing as they would in their original location. Once transplanted into the eyebrow area, these hairs grow naturally, providing long-lasting results. However, the transplanted hairs may initially shed and then grow back, and over time, they may require occasional trimming. Since the transplanted hairs are from the patient’s own body, they will continue to grow throughout their life, giving them a permanent solution for fuller, natural-looking eyebrows. However, it's important to note that while the transplant itself is permanent, the maintenance of eyebrow shape and thickness may require regular grooming.

What factors affect the price of an eyebrow transplant in Turkey?

The price of an eyebrow transplant in Turkey can be influenced by several factors:
  • Technique Used: The method chosen for the transplant, such as Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) or Direct Hair Implantation (DHI), can impact the cost. DHI, being a more advanced technique, is typically more expensive due to its precision and minimal invasiveness.
  • Surgeon Expertise: The experience and reputation of the surgeon play a significant role in the price. Highly experienced surgeons or those with a track record of successful, natural-looking results may charge more.
  • Clinic Reputation: Well-established clinics with modern facilities and international certifications (like JCI accreditation) generally charge higher fees for their services due to the quality of care and advanced technologies they offer.
  • Location: The city or region where the surgery takes place can also affect the price. Clinics in major cities like Istanbul, which are popular for medical tourism, may have slightly higher fees due to demand and operational costs.
  • Package Inclusions: Some clinics offer all-inclusive packages that include consultation, surgery, aftercare, and transportation, which can increase the overall cost. Patients might also opt for packages that provide additional services like post-op treatments or follow-up consultations.
  • Number of Grafts: The total number of hair follicles required for the transplant can also influence the price. A larger eyebrow design with higher graft numbers will naturally cost more.
