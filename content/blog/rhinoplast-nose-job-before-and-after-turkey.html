What is Rhinoplasty?

Rhinoplasty is the reshaping of the nose shape through plastic surgery. The shape of the nose is determined genetically, but it can also change later due to a blow or a previous surgical intervention. With rhinoplasty surgery, the size of the nose can be changed, the tip of the nose and the back of the nose can be changed, and the shape of the nostrils can be changed. During the same surgery, intranasal curvatures that cause difficulty in breathing can be corrected. The aim of the procedures is to correct the shape of the nose without disrupting nasal breathing.
