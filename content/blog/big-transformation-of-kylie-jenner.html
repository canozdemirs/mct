What is Breast Augmentation Surgery?

Breast augmentation surgery, in essence, is a cosmetic procedure used by women whose breasts are smaller than desired. Breast augmentation surgeries are operations that can be performed very easily today, even almost ordinary ones. Today, silicone gel-filled prosthesis (breast implant) is accepted as the gold standard in breast augmentation surgeries. Fat injections, which have been popular recently, are also a viable method in suitable patients; With its more permanent effect, breast augmentation surgery emerged as an established standard method.

Operation

In a hospital setting, the procedure is carried out in the operating room while the patient is under general anaesthesia. It takes half to an hour. A 4-5 cm incision is used to insert the prosthesis. The incision can be made in one of three locations: the armpit, the lower section of the nipple (circumareolar), or the submammary fold, which is less frequently used (axillary). Over time, the incision scar gets hazy. Depending on the patient's breast anatomy and thickness, the prosthesis can be positioned either below the breast tissue (subglandular) or behind the chest muscle (subpectoral).

What Exactly Is a Silicone Breast Implant or Prosthesis?

Breast prostheses are breast-shaped objects filled with silicone gel or saline and have an exterior wall made of silicone or polyurethane. They come in a variety of sizes depending on their height and diameter (projection). According to their forms, there are two main types: round and anatomical (drop). If a rounded form is wanted, the droplet type is chosen. If decollete and an increase in breast volume are also desired, just the lowest half of the breast will be full. The prosthesis's surface might be either rough or smooth.

How Is the Shape of the Prosthesis Decided?

In general, prostheses used in breast augmentation surgery can be in two forms; round (hemisphere), or tear ( anatomical). Round prostheses are approximately half of a sphere. They are mostly preferred for breasts that have a smooth shape but lack volume. Anatomical prostheses are prostheses similar in shape to the shape of the natural breast, with a fuller lower pole and a thinner upper pole. There is no single type of these prostheses. There are different types according to the shape of the person's deformity. Generally, this type of prosthesis is used in patients with deformities that need to be corrected, as well as volume deficiencies. What Should Be Considered Before Breast Augmentation Surgery?

  • Before the operation, it would be appropriate to inform your responsible physician about your general health condition. In any case, your doctor will ask for the necessary examinations to prepare for the operation.
  • The use of painkillers (especially aspirin) should be discontinued at least 8 days before the operation. Again, herbal teas, natural food supplements, etc. should be discontinued, as they may increase the risk of blood thinning and bleeding.
  • Smoking should be stopped 2 weeks before, if possible, as it will impair the quality of wound healing.
  • Since bleeding tendency will increase in the body in general during the menstruation period, it would be appropriate to provide information on this subject even if your doctor does not ask.
  • Although breast prosthesis does not cause cancer, it can make it difficult to detect a mass that may occur later. For this reason, the condition of the breast should be recorded with a radiological examination such as breast ultrasound or mammography before surgery.
